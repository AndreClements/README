## Methodology that outlived the project

The durable, non-pitch-specific finding — a *concern-versus-agency* distinction transposed from
`docs/lib/theory_of_art.md` onto an economy — is repo-level rather than project-level. If it is
written up for public use, it belongs in `docs/lib/`, stated in its own terms and without the
commissioner, the platform or the pitch attached.
