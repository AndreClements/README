## 2. The Algorithm: "The Crowd"
**Constraint:** Single surface. No refresh (One sheet of paper).
**Process:** Iterative layering of time-frames and related poses

### Summary
* we perform a multi-pass methodology on two levels. Each phase has 3 Poses: Standing, sitting/kneeling & lying down

### Phase A: The Phantom (Error Correction)
*   **Duration:** 30 seconds for each pose.
*   **Tool:** Blending Stump + Charcoal Powder.
*   **Logic:** Low-authority mark-making. Allows for search and error without consequence, engaging tip awareness.
*   **Output:** Atmospheric under-drawing.

### Phase B: The Structure (Harmonics)
*   **Duration:** 90 seconds for each pose.
*   **Tool:** Blending Stump + Charcoal Powder.
*   **Logic:** As before & adds Geometric mapping. Identifying the relationships between C-Curves and S-Curves.
*   **Output:** Volumetric mesh.

### Phase C: The Incision (Control)
*   **Duration:** 4.5 minutes for each pose.
*   **Tool:** As before & adds Chisel Point (Razor Edge) sharpened charcoal.
*   **Logic:** As before & adds High-authority selective definition.
*   **The Spurgeon Subroutine:**
    1.  `Tease()`: Simulation / Hover.
    2.  `Touch()`: Calibration / Light Contact.
    3.  `Commit()`: Execution / High Impact.
