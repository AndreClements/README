**The integration is itself a signature function.** Verify the paper *performs* the conceptual weaving of the three works (Landscapify / multipartiality / distributed signature) rather than merely asserting it — the 1/N ↔ dignity equivalence stated early and plainly, anchored in material decision points (35 sources at 1/35; two alignment modes; 12 strategies in a √2 frame), not as metaphor.

**Anti-patterns to avoid** (voice §7): "not X but Y" → additive; em-dash / semicolon as primary joint; resolving tensions; performing expertise; explaining the Afrikaans; spectacularising. Treat the signature **as method, not ornament** — academic register and voice cohere because the moves are epistemic stances.
