## 2. A3 — the local-code decision

**Recommendation: drop the local "A3" code from the paper.**

In the paper, A3 (dignity conservation) is **invoked, never tested**. The dignity work runs
through the **gate/defeater**, the **Presence Ledger**, and **1/N** — not through the formal
`conserve(D) := (∏ᵢdᵢ > 0) ∧ (minᵢ dᵢ ≥ η)` condition, which is never evaluated in the paper.
The code earns nothing locally except a **Gate-3 collision** with A3★.

Drop the code and the collision dies at the root: the renamed beauty claim stands with **no
near-neighbour**, and the glossary no longer has to apologise for two confusable symbols. The
dignity-conservation *concept* stays in prose; the *formal* A3 is cited to the thesis
([§2.5](parametric_authorship.md)) by reference, where it is actually used.

This is the cleaner half of the rename: **rename one symbol and remove the other**, rather than
keep both and warn the reader to mind the diacritic.

| **Constraint Legibility** | ✗ names the *legibility axis* (the predictor), not the beauty | ✓ | ✓ | pending | ✓ | ~ | **Re-route:** a fine name for the **legibility probe**, not for the claim |

### Recommendation

**Legible Grace** — it is the only candidate that carries all three of *reception* ("legible" =
the constraint is read), *felt beauty* ("grace"), and *the freely-given / dignity-preserving*
valence ("grace" = given, not taken) in one slot, passing every gate but the operator's
read-aloud. **BODY check pending.**

**Lowest-leak alternative: The Enabling Read** — if the read-aloud finds "grace" too
theological or too soft, fall back to this; its only cost is naming the predictor rather than
the felt quality.

**Spin-off:** *Constraint Legibility* is recommended as the name for the **four-prompt probe**
(currently "the legibility probe"), since it names exactly the legibility axis the probe
measures.

## 5. Block 4 — interrogating the central claim (proposed paper diffs)

### 5a. Conjunct / probe mismatch

A3★'s verbatim statement (line 202) asserts **~6 predicates** on the constraint: *coherent,
generative, freely-chosen (continuously revocable), traceable through execution,
dignity-preserving, read as enabling rather than restrictive*. The four-prompt probe (lines
210–219) isolates only **4**: legibility, enabling/restrictive, dignity, strain. *Coherent* and
*traceable-through-execution* are asserted but never probed.

**Proposal: prune the asserted conjuncts to the four the probe can see**, folding *coherent* and
*traceable-through-execution* into the **legibility** prompt's gloss (they are *what legibility
consists of*, not separate testable terms). Make the mismatch visible, not silent.

> **Diff — §6, line 202 (the verbatim statement):**
>
> **Before:**
> *"…the sensation of beauty correlates with (tracks, defeasibly) the legibility of a coherent,
> generative constraint: one explicitly authored or freely chosen, traceable through its
> execution, dignity-preserving, and read by the perceiver as enabling rather than merely
> restrictive."*
>
> **After (pruned to what the probe sees; the two folded predicates kept as the gloss of
> "legible"):**
> *"…the sensation of beauty correlates with (tracks, defeasibly) the **legibility** of a
> freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint — where a constraint is legible when it reads as
> coherent and traceable through its execution — and is **read by the perceiver as enabling
> rather than merely restrictive**. The probe tests exactly these terms: legibility,
> enabling/restrictive reading, dignity, and the strain; the claim asserts no predicate the
> instrument cannot see."*

### 5b. No-variance pilot (the confound)

The viewer-side pilot runs on three works that are **uniformly** high-legibility, 1/N, and
dignity-preserving. With no variance in the predictor, the pilot can confirm *"legible works are
felt beautiful"* but **cannot show beauty tracks legibility** — there is nothing low-legibility
or dignity-eroded to contrast against. Confirmation without contrast is a confound.

**Proposal: name the confound explicitly, and specify a minimal contrast set.** Two moves, either
or both:

- **Contrast set (described, not exhibited):** one **low-legibility** comparator (e.g. a
  deliberately *crowned/weighted* composite — the same stock at non-equal weights, so the 1/N
  rule is no longer legible) and/or one **dignity-eroded** comparator (the consent-harvest
  counterexample already named at line 206, presented as a described case, never made). Variance
  in the predictor is what lets the probe test *tracking* rather than mere *presence*.
- **Stated limitation (minimum):** a line in §6 and §8 avowing that, absent contrast, the pilot
  tests presence not tracking.

> **Diff — §6, after line 221 (probe two-passes paragraph), add:**
>
> *"One limit is built into the stimulus, and is named here rather than left silent: the three
> wall works are uniformly high-legibility, 1/N, and dignity-preserving, so the pilot has no
> variance in its predictor. It can show that legible, dignity-preserving works are felt as
> beautiful; it cannot yet show that beauty **tracks** legibility, because nothing in the set
> reads as low-legibility or dignity-eroded to contrast against. Tracking needs a contrast: a
> deliberately crowned (non-1/N) composite whose rule is no longer legible, and the
> consent-harvest case named above, both described rather than exhibited. Until that contrast is
> run, the pilot tests presence, not covariance — and Legible Grace stays, in the honest word,
> mostly asserted."*

> **Diff — §8 (Limitations), add to the limitations list:**
>
> *"The viewer pilot confounds presence with tracking: with no low-legibility or dignity-eroded
> comparator in the set, it cannot isolate covariance. A minimal contrast set is specified in §6
> and is the first thing a follow-up should run."*
