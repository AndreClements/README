## Abstract

> **In plain English.** This paper takes one afternoon in a life-drawing room, where people pose *as objects* and a small group draws them, and reads it as a piece of research. The lens is *parametric authorship*: the idea that an artist authors **rules** that produce **families** of things, and that the meaning lives in the mapping from rule to family, not in any single mark. I show that the same idea reads a workshop, an ethics of consent, a piece of software architecture, and three composite pictures on a gallery wall, all at once. It helps to hold one picture throughout: a single body read at two scales, a body posed in a room and a figure drawn out of thirty-five pictures on a wall, with parametric authorship as the connective tissue between them. The thread that ties them is one small decision: hold every source at equal weight (1/N). That decision is also the ethics. A purpose-built *presence ledger* records whether the room kept its promises. Some readers will think a paper this wide is trying to do too much. The reply runs the other way: holding many systems at once, and staying answerable across all of them, is exactly the skill the paper is about.

**Abstract (academic).** This practice-led paper argues that Parametric Authorship (PA) operates as *transdisciplinary connective tissue*: a shared grammar under which a consent-based life-drawing event becomes legible at once as studio practice, analytic ethics and aesthetics, software-architecture governance, computational generative art, pedagogy, and semiotics. The paper reads these disciplines as organs of one body, a figure offered as a lens rather than a claim of literal identity. The case is the *Objectification Workshop* (designed for the Eve Song happening, University of Pretoria, 2026), read together with three layered-averaging works exhibited in the same show. PA's formal apparatus is cited from a companion working thesis and not re-derived. The paper carries only the Parametric Author Function tuple and the dignity-conservation condition it needs. The argument is organised around one load-bearing joint, the place where a formal decision and an ethical one turn out to be the same: equal weighting (1/N) as dignity conservation. From there it operationalises a posited claim (A3★): that the felt beauty of an authored artefact tracks, defeasibly, the legibility of a coherent, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint. A bespoke *presence ledger* is offered as the data instrument that turns the room into auditable evidence. The breadth of the reading is itself part of the claim: the same grammar names a wider practice, transdisciplinary programming, the keeping of responsibility and integrity across nested systems (including the dark, machine-composed layers the paper was itself built on), so the reach is offered as rigour rather than apologised for as scope. Governance follows METHODOLOGY_CII at tier T3.

**Keywords:** parametric authorship; objectification; consent; dignity; life drawing; layered averaging; object-oriented ontology; practice-led research; semiotics; South African contemporary art.

> **A small glossary, for readers arriving from different disciplines.**
> **Parametric authorship (PA)** — authoring the *rules* that produce a *family* of works, rather than inscribing one finished work. **PAF** — the Parametric Author Function, the authored tuple `⟨S, 𝒫, C, E, Φ⟩`: Substrates, Parameter space, Constraints, Environment, and a Shaping functional (how much your settings actually matter). **A1, Map-over-Mark** — meaning is borne by the mapping from rules to family, not by any single mark. **A3 and A3★** (distinct symbols, worth keeping apart on a fast read) — **A3** is the *dignity-conservation* condition: the product of the three dignity scores stays positive and none falls below a declared floor. **A3★** is the posited *beauty* claim: felt beauty tracks, defeasibly, a coherent, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint (developed in [§6](#the-a3-beauty-thread)). **Hyperstrate** — the cross-cutting layer of tools, defaults, policies, and logistics that any authoring runs *on*. **Dignity tensor** — dignity scored across three scopes (personal, object, system); the conservation condition is that their product stays above zero and none drops below a declared floor. **Auth★** — an agency metric (named here, derived in the companion working thesis). **Maculate** — marked, flawed, worked-with rather than purified. **The family** — the set of works a constraint yields; the unit of the practice. **As-if / if-not / and-yet** — engage provisionally; audit where it strains; hold the contradiction. **Empty Turn** — the sovereign right to stop, without penalty. **Transdisciplinary programming** — the practice this paper names for itself: keeping responsibility and striving toward integrity across complex nested systems, the breadth being the method rather than a flaw. **The nesting** — systems inside systems (mark in pose in room in institution; function in module in model in pipeline), the layered terrain authorship runs across. **The answerable maker** — the one who signs the gate; authorship distributes across the nesting, responsibility concentrates here. **Dark code** — code trusted by its gate (its tests) rather than by its reading; a feature of deep nesting, and the developer's ordinary condition.

## 1. Introduction — From the unnamed gaze to the designed constraint

To draw from life is to look hard at a person and make of them, for a while, an object of attention. For the length of a pose they become, to the hand, less a whole person than a problem of edges: a line, a mass, the weight of a shoulder under a fall of light. The life room has long had good reasons to leave that mostly unspoken: a working focus to keep, a courtesy to the sitter, a craft whose conventions were earned honestly and still serve. This paper does not arrive to correct the tradition, nor to claim that naming is the better way. It begins where a more pointed companion essay leaves off (Clements, [*Objectively Objectifying Objectification*](https://andresclements.com/objectively-objectifying-objectification/)) by making one dynamic explicit rather than ambient (and only the one the inquiry turns on), then asking what is left of authorship once the looking is named. What it offers is not a confession, and not a charge laid against the convention: it is a method, set beside the conventional one rather than over it.

I was invited to exhibit. I have decided to treat that invitation *as-if* it were a piece of presented research: provisionally, instrumentally, and with a straight face about the rigour even while keeping the tone light. The stance is borrowed wholesale from the repository's engagement methodology (METHODOLOGY_CII §1): engage *as-if*, audit *if-not*, hold the *and-yet*. *If-not* a formal presentation, then still: work made that I can stand behind, shown well. *And yet*: the theory of language at the foot of the operator's own README applies here, and fans out. Language is a magical, world-creating force. Language is also just the noise made by a piece of meat in a mouth. The two readings do not resolve. A held pose is the same shape of problem. A body worked *as-if* a chair is world-creating (it makes a study, a family, a small room of meaning) and it is also just a body, meat under a fall of light. Meaning fans out across the gap between those readings and never collapses to either. That is the engine of everything below.

The paper studies one practice at two scales. At the scale of a body: the **Objectification Workshop**, in which sitters pose as objects (a chair, a doorway, a tree) under actively held consent, and a small room draws them. At the scale of a wall: three composite works in the same exhibition ([*Shroud of Eve*](https://andresclements.com/artwork/shroud-of-eve/), [*Study of Eve*](https://andresclements.com/artwork/study-of-eve/), [*Twelve Refractions*](https://andresclements.com/artwork/twelve-refractions/)), each made by holding many source images at equal weight until a figure emerges that none of them alone contains. The wager of the paper is that the two are one practice read at two scales of a single body, and that parametric authorship is the connective tissue that holds it as one. The disciplines that follow (semiotics, the ethics and aesthetics of objectification, software architecture, the computational) are organs of that one body. The 1/N, equal weight with no source crowned, is its load-bearing joint: the place where a formal decision and an ethical one turn out to be the same decision (under conditions [§4.2](#the-layered-averaging-works-parametric-authorship-at-exhibition-scale) makes explicit, since equal weighting prevents crowning without, on its own, guaranteeing dignity). Hold that picture, and the breadth below reads as one anatomy rather than a parliament of fields.

The posture throughout is mediating rather than adjudicating, in a conflict-resilience register. The several lenses meet in this one room, and the aim is to let them hold one another in tension rather than to crown any of them. Where the paper presses, it presses on a specific, clearly articulated dynamic (whether the exit stays live, whether the consent can be audited), and not on the conventional practice as such, which keeps its merit and often keeps its care by means of its own. What follows is offered as a lens: polished, and pointed here at one room. Where a reader points it next, and what they see through it, stays theirs. The paper holds no attachment to the view.

**Research questions.**

1. **(RQ1)** What is authorship when the work is a *family* (of drawings, of composites) produced under a chosen constraint, rather than a single inscribed mark?
2. **(RQ2)** Under what room conditions does deliberate objectification stay *benign* in Nussbaum's (1995) sense, and how does a governance architecture make those conditions legible and auditable rather than merely asserted?
3. **(RQ3)** Does the felt beauty of such an artefact track the legibility of a coherent, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint (the posited claim A3★), and what would falsify that?

A note on register, because the paper will keep two of them in one room. It is academic where it reasons and hedges; it is plainer, and occasionally bodily, where it does not. This is deliberate, and it is methodological: the operator's voice signature treats [multipartiality](https://github.com/AndreClements/README/blob/main/docs/lib/MULTI_PARTIALITY.md) (standing with more than one party at once, without collapsing the difference) as an ethical stance, not a stylistic flourish. A paper about holding many readings at equal weight had better hold its own registers that way too.

## 2. Theoretical framework — three bodies of thought, one connective tissue

### Two registers of claim: the constitutive and the scoped

Start with the body, since the whole paper does. The repository's first axiom is that the body is the first and final arena: a body brings forth a world by working its own surfaces, taking a stimulus, making a response, and keeping the adaptation as a mark written into the tissue. That loop, stimulus → response → adaptation, is the bodily name for what the working thesis calls authoring a world by operating one's own closure (Maturana and Varela 1980; Thompson 2007). Read through authorship, the loop is parametric authorship at its widest reach. This is the paper's constitutive tier, and it is one of two. The work of this section is to mark the cut between the tiers before either claim does any further work: one boundary, drawn once, then doing four jobs at once.

**The constitutive claim is unfalsifiable, and that is its type rather than its flaw.** At its deepest reach the hypothesis is total: everything is parametric authorship, all the way up and all the way down. A claim that forbids no observation has no empirical content in Popper's sense, and this one forbids none. It is offered, deliberately, in the register of constitutive claims rather than testable ones, in the company of process, will, *différance*, autopoiesis: judged by fertility, by coherence, by what it lets a maker see and build, never by a crucial experiment. To ask *what is not parametric authorship* at this level is to receive the honest answer, *nothing*, and to take that answer as a statement about the kind of theory it is. Leaning on the autopoietic ancestor confirms which tier PA-universal lives on. It does not rescue PA from the cut: the ancestor inherits its power and its unfalsifiability together, and that honesty is the graft's strength. The meat-noise couplet says it twice over: the noise (the process, the meat) and the world-creating (the bringing-forth) are two faces of one operation, and PA-universal is that couplet read through authorship.

**Below the constitutive claim the register changes, and with it the rules of judgement.** A3★ and the room-claims are scoped. They carry defeaters, name their boundaries, and answer to the Presence Ledger and to evidence. They are falsifiable precisely because they are bounded down out of the universal, and the bounding is what buys the falsifiability. A3★ has an outside (the sublime, the unchosen, the formless) and a falsifier (coherence felt as beautiful while dignity erodes) only because it is a scoped claim and not the constitutive one. So the two questions that looked like one are two: what is outside PA (nothing, at the constitutive level), and what is outside A3★ (a great deal, named, at the scoped level). The single cut between the levels keeps the universal claim from borrowing the empirical credit of the apparatus around it, and keeps the scoped claims from inheriting the universal claim's immunity to disproof.

**Ethics sits between the two, and it is emergent rather than elemental.** The dignity gate is not imported as an absolute, and not discovered as a law of the universe. It arises, the way an autopoietic regularity or a held convention arises, within the coupling of the parties in the room. It is contingent, chosen rather than inflicted, revocable, re-earned pose by pose, and it is causally real for all that: which is why *emergent* serves the argument where a strict epiphenomenalism, leaving the gate doing no work, would not. An emergent ethic runs one standing risk: that it collapse into the frame of whoever is strongest in the room, a facilitator's taste in the costume of a floor. The 1/N is the answer, the paper's own central equivalence doing ethical service: the floor is held by the equal-weighted quorum and not by any single author, no party's frame crowned, dignity conserved as a product across all scopes so that no surplus anywhere can buy the flattening of anyone. The gate is emergent, and it is non-arbitrary, because it is multipartite-emergent and not mono-authored.

**And the same cut locates responsibility.** If everything authors, then *who authored* can no longer find the answerable party: the answer being everyone, and the wind besides. Authorship distributes at the constitutive level, exactly as the hypothesis says it must. Responsibility does not: it concentrates, and the figure already says where. The sailor authors the set of the sails and the choice to put out, and does not author the wind (the wind is `E`, the environment the function does not govern). The voyage is genuinely co-authored. The account for it is owed by the one who set the control surfaces and chose to launch this run. *Authorship is dead, long live authorship:* the death is the dissolution of the single inscribing hand into the arrangement, and the life is the answerable maker who sets the arrangement going and signs for what it brings forth. *En tog.*

### Width as rigour: one practice across nested systems

A criticism arrives before the work is even finished, and it is worth meeting in the open rather than at the foot of the page: that an argument reaching from a life-drawing pose to software architecture to a wall of composites is simply too wide to be one thing. The answer is not a narrowing. The answer is the name of the practice. This is transdisciplinary programming: the work of keeping and making sense of responsibility, and striving toward integrity, in and around complex nested systems. Width is what the terrain already is. A grammar narrower than the systems it governs cannot hold their integrity, and an organ is not scatter. The body figure earns its keep right here, because a body is the thing that holds many systems at once and answers for the whole.

The terrain is nested, layer inside layer. A mark inside a pose, a pose inside a room, a room inside an institution. A function inside a module, a module inside a model, a model inside a pipeline that runs on a computational entanglement of interests and agendas nobody read end to end. Call it the nesting. Authoring across it is the only authoring left once the single inscribing hand has dissolved into the arrangement (the paragraph above). You set the control surfaces at the layers you can reach, and the rest runs on defaults that author in your absence: A4 names them, the platforms and weights and policies that co-author the family of outcomes unless they are disclosed and bounded. Some layers you compose. Some compose themselves, or were composed by a machine, and ship gated by a test rather than read line by line. That last is the developer's ordinary condition now, and it has a plain name worth keeping: dark code, the code trusted by its gate and not by its reading. It is a feature of deep nesting rather than a scandal of it. The honest question is never how to abolish the unread layer. It is where the answering happens once you cannot read everything.

It happens at the gate. When the implementation goes unread, the surface you actually author is the validation stack: the test, the ledger, the linter, the refusal path. Authorship survives the unread layer as the design of the interface and its proofs, the implementations coming and going beneath a contract that persists (DIOW). The stroke is the recorded decision rather than the hidden mechanism (A6). And the void the death of the single author opens is fillable by one thing only: an answerable maker, the one who signs the gate. Authorship distributes across the nesting, all the way down. Responsibility does not: it concentrates on the signature, by whatever qualification we choose to bound it. That is the grammar in a line. Distribute the authoring as wide as the systems demand, and concentrate the answering to a point that can be held to account. The breadth is not the bug. The breadth is the discipline, held to the floor `η` like any other load.

### 2.1 Parametric authorship, by reference

The formal apparatus of parametric authorship is set out in a companion working thesis (Clements, *The Fourth Dimension as Execution*) and is not re-derived here. This paper carries only what the case needs.

Authorship, in PA, is the design of control surfaces: you articulate a rule-set, set ranges, fix what must hold, and let a generator instantiate a family. The authored object is a tuple, the **Parametric Author Function**:

$$\mathrm{PAF} := \langle\, S,\ \mathcal{P},\ C,\ E,\ \Phi \,\rangle$$

Substrates `S` (tools, materials, models); a parameter space `𝒫` (the knobs, and their ranges); constraints `C` (what will not be done); environment `E` (budgets, governance, the weather you do not control); and a shaping functional `Φ` (how sensitively the work responds when you move a parameter). The thesis develops an agency metric, **Auth★**, and a cybernetic inversion (DIOW) by which tools must conform to a declared interface rather than the author adapting to the tool; I name these and point to them, no more.

The one axiom this paper leans its full weight on is **A1, Map-over-Mark**: *meaning is borne by stable features of the mapping from parameters to artefacts, not by any single token.* And the one formal condition it needs is dignity conservation, **A3**: dignity is scored across three scopes (personal, object, system), and is conserved only while their product stays positive and none falls below a declared floor:

$$\mathrm{conserve}(D) := \Bigl(\textstyle\prod_i d_i > 0\Bigr) \,\wedge\, \Bigl(\min_i d_i \geq \eta\Bigr)$$

The product is the load-bearing part. If any one dignity goes to zero, the whole goes to zero. There is no averaging your way out of a flattened person. The declared floor `η` is the form you refuse to break under load: the point past which a pose is abandoned rather than finished badly.

### 2.2 Objectification: the ethics and the aesthetics

The case is objectification taken at its word, so the framework's spine is Martha Nussbaum's (1995; 1999) analysis. Nussbaum's decisive move is to treat objectification as a cluster of analytically distinct features rather than a single thing, several of which, under the right conditions, can be benign rather than harmful. The workshop courts a few of these on purpose (inertness; reduction to body and appearance; fungibility) and works to keep three others firmly out of the room (violability; denial of subjectivity; silencing). Rae Langton (2009) adds three more features to the cluster and sharpens the account of when instrumentality curdles.

The benign reading has a critic, and the paper keeps her in the room rather than answering her too quickly. Catharine MacKinnon's objection (engaged here via the repository's hosted entry, [`OBJECTIFICATION.md`](https://github.com/AndreClements/README/blob/main/docs/lib/OBJECTIFICATION.md), attribution hers) is that under conditions of structural inequality, "consent" can be hollow from the start. This is not a footnote to be discharged. It is the live *if-not* that the governance architecture in [§4.3](#governance-as-architecture-software-engineering) exists to audit.

The aesthetic counter-weight comes from [object-oriented ontology](https://github.com/AndreClements/README/blob/main/docs/lib/OOO.md). Graham Harman (2011) holds that every object, human and not, keeps a withdrawn interior that exceeds any relation to it. Read through OOO, the deepest claim of the workshop inverts: to pose as a chair, taken seriously, does not degrade a person into a thing. It asks the room to grant the pose the withdrawn interior a real chair is already owed. Objectification, on this reading, is not the making of an object; it is the *denial* of the dignity an object already has. This is a transposition, and is marked as one: the move from Harman's metaphysical withdrawal to an ethical claim about dignity is offered, not derived. One cannot strictly entail the ought from the withdrawal, and the paper does not pretend to (*Host ≠ Author*, applied to OOO itself). The same withdrawal runs through the exhibition works, where the composite Eve, in the operator's own phrase, withdraws: and her withdrawal is perhaps also her unfurling.

### 2.3 The creativity of constraints

That a limit can be generative rather than merely restrictive is an old and well-attested idea, and the paper draws on its lineage rather than claiming it: Stravinsky's *Poetics of Music* (1942/47) on the freedom found inside self-imposed bounds; Boden's (1990/2004) account of constraint as the condition of creative search; Stokes (2005) on creativity *from* constraints; Elster (1999) on binding oneself to be more free; Kant (1790) on purposive form; and the experimental support gathered by Haught-Tromp (2017). The operator's voice has a name for this: the **Constraint Gift**. A prompt that closes the easy move ("do not draw a portrait, draw a doorway") gathers the attention and presses the hand toward the essential line. The limit reads as the thing that lets a family of marks cohere. This is resistance in the training sense: the closed-off easy move is the load the hand works against, and the essential line is the strength built by working against it rather than in its absence.

### 2.4 Risk, vulnerability, and the dignity gate

Marc Botha's "Risk" (2021) does the last piece of framing work, and it is the piece that keeps the aesthetics honest. Botha distinguishes risk *taken* from risk *imposed*: "when you externalise risk onto a vulnerable body, it becomes harm." That restates the benign/malignant line in the language of risk. Benign where the sitter takes the risk and holds it, exit live; malignant where the risk is laid on her. Botha's further claim, that agency itself requires vulnerability, meets the workshop's central paradox directly: choosing to bind oneself into an object-pose is an act of agency, a taking-on, not a giving-up. This is the threshold on which the whole study balances, and it sets up the defeater in [§6](#the-a3-beauty-thread).

Throughout, the framework is held in the maculate register METHODOLOGY_CII insists on: all of these systems are flawed, and the practice navigates the flaws rather than purifying them. And throughout, **Host ≠ Author**. Nussbaum's, Langton's, Harman's, and Botha's frameworks are hosted here as working dependencies, attribution theirs. The transpositions, and the posited claim A3★, are the author's, marked as his.

## 3. Method — practice-led, the workshop as instrument

### 3.1 The practice-led stance

This is not a study *about* a practice. The workshop *is* the inquiry, and the drawings, the vetoes, the dropped poses, and the debrief are its data. The methodology's risk heuristic applies: `risk_index = complexity × power × distance_from_wetware` (METHODOLOGY_CII §1). A hands-on drawing room is low on distance-from-wetware (everything happens in bodies, in a room, in person), which raises the evidential weight of what is felt there and lowers the opacity that haunts screen-mediated work. It also concentrates the power asymmetry in one place: the facilitator, who authored the frame and invited the risk, holds more of the room than he likes to admit. The method does not dissolve that asymmetry. It makes it visible and watches it.

### 3.2 Site and constraint design

The site is the UP 2-1 Gallery (University of Pretoria), within the run of the Eve Song happening (exhibition 20 July – 14 August 2026; workshops 27 July – 13 August). The bookable session has an [event page](https://andresclements.com/events/objectification-workshop/) and a [poster](https://andresclements.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/eve-song-objectification-workshop-poster.jpg) on the operator's site (both forthcoming: the site's events section is in build). Each object-prompt is a small PAF: the substrate is charcoal and A2 paper and a body; the parameter space is the prompt and its agile variants; the constraints are the dignity floor and the kept-out features of §2.2; the environment is the gallery, the timing, the policy; and the shaping functional is the sensitivity of the family of drawings to the choice of pose. The pose runs about fifteen minutes and is drawn **four figures to a page**: the page is a small matrix of the session, not a precious sheet, four reps to a set with the value in the accumulation and never in any single one. The full constraint vocabulary is versioned in the prompt pool (`prompt-pool.md`).

### 3.3 The consent and safety architecture as method

The architecture is not a pastoral add-on. It is the experimental apparatus, because it is what holds the benign conditions in place long enough to observe them.

- The prompt pool is **co-authored and vetoed by the sitters before the room ever sees it.** In PA terms this is A4 (Defaults Author) handled correctly: a prompt pool is a silent co-author of the family it yields, and the veto discloses and bounds it, in doing so making the sitter a real co-author of the constraint. This is the change room of the practice: the threshold where the object-conceit is tried on or refused, before anyone steps onto the floor.
- A **stop/rest signal**, agreed and honoured at any moment, no reason owed. This is the Empty Turn (METHODOLOGY_CII §9.3) given a body: the sovereign right to stop, which is "not a failure but a dignified exit." It is the withdrawal reflex raised to a first-class control action, the hand off the hot surface made part of the architecture rather than left to nerve.
- The right to come **off the stand mid-pose**, and **opting out never touches the fee.** Financial safety belongs to exit-liveness: an exit you cannot afford is not an exit.
- **Shared, casual breaks and a shared debrief**, the deload built into the session rather than waited for. Vulnerability is distributed, not assigned: to look, and to be seen looking, implicates the artist too.
- **Documentation only by specific consent, anonymised by default**, and (see [§7](#governance-and-research-ethics)) no research use of any session until the University's posing/documentation policy is confirmed.

### 3.4 The instrument: the Presence Ledger

A study needs an instrument that turns events into evidence. Borrowed from the working thesis's governance stack, the instrument here is a **provenance ledger** specialised for a live room: a **Presence Ledger** (`templates/presence_ledger.md`; instance at `presence-ledger.md`). Where a provenance ledger records claims and assets, a presence ledger records *presence and consent events*: a vetoed prompt, a rest called, a pose dropped with pay unaffected, a photo-consent granted and scoped, a dignity-flag raised and resolved, an Empty Turn. Each row names the axiom or CARDS axis it discharges, so the sheet *shows* the ethic operating rather than asserting it. A single row reads like a grip-strength test for the room: a small, cheap measure that stands proxy for the health of the whole. This is the indexical trace of A6: in parametric authorship the "stroke" is the recorded decision, and consent, recorded, is a kind of mark.

The worked example in Appendix C is illustrative until the real run. It is shown here, before the findings, on purpose: a method instrument has to be seen working before it can be trusted to carry an argument. (Fiertek's 2013 *Performance Guide to Eve-Song*, a document that is also its own research form, is the structural model for treating an operational artefact as a scholarly one.)

## 4. Case analysis — reading the room, and the wall, in PA

### 4.1 The pose as rule-manifold (semiotics)

Saussure (1916) and Peirce gave us a sign that points: a vehicle, a referent, an interpretant. Parametric authorship extends the triangle to generated families. The prompt is the **rule manifold** (`𝒫`); the family of drawings is the **generated vehicle family** (`𝒜`); the room's reading is the **interpretant field**. Meaning, in this frame, is what stays stable when the parameters are nudged (invariants) and what changes predictably when they are shocked on purpose (designed transitions). Style is the curvature: the way the family bends as the prompt moves.

The chair-pose is the cleanest instance, and it is the held tension the introduction set running as the engine of the paper: its meaning is *designed undecidability*. You cannot tell, from outside, whether a body folded into a chair has been diminished or honoured. The cycle that occasions the show says it more economically than any theory: to be visible is at once to be warned and to be invited, with no way to know which. That ambiguity is not noise in the sign. It is the sign. A1 holds: the meaning is in the mapping (this prompt, held by this room, under this consent), not in any one drawing of it.

### 4.2 The layered-averaging works: parametric authorship at exhibition scale

The three composite works in the show are the same move at the scale of a wall, and reading them is where the paper's central claim earns its keep. I anchor it in the material decisions, not in resemblance.

**Layered averaging is A1 made literal.** Thirty-five historical paintings of Eve (Dürer, Cranach, Masaccio, Michelangelo, Rubens, Valadon, and more) are aligned and averaged at equal weight, "yielding a figure that none contains alone." The rule-set plus the weighting is the rule manifold. The composite is the family. The meaning is in the mapping. No single painting is the sign. The sum of how she has been pictured is the sign.

**The 1/N is the dignity move, and this is the equivalence the whole paper turns on.** The decision to hold every source at equal one-thirty-fifth weight is, in the operator's own words from the work's statement, "the least technical and the most load-bearing decision in the whole pipeline: equal weight, no Eve crowned over the others, a quorum rather than a queue." Read formally, 1/N is a weighting. Read ethically, it is dignity conservation across the scope of all the sources: none flattened to zero, none crowned. The formal decision and the ethical decision are the *same decision*. One qualification belongs right here, planted with the equivalence rather than left for §6: equal weighting prevents crowning, but it does not by itself manufacture dignity. Provenance, consent, the conditions under which the sources were gathered, and the framing the room reads them through all still bear on it. The 1/N conserves dignity across sources only while those hold, and [§6](#the-a3-beauty-thread) names the case (a stock harvested without consent) where a perfectly even weighting would fail anyway. The arithmetic is necessary, and it is not sufficient. With that said: this is multipartiality (the operator's term for standing with more than one party at once without collapsing the difference) written as arithmetic. And it is the same ethic as the workshop's refusal to curate for the one best drawing: no precious sheet, no crowned source. The body-scale practice and the wall-scale practice share one commitment, and it is a number. It is the core the whole body conditions: the stabilising centre that every other movement, in the room and on the wall, transmits its load through.

**Shroud and Study are a designed transition.** The two works run the *same* thirty-five-source stock through *one* changed parameter: the alignment axis. *Study of Eve* aligns on the body, and the canonical standing nude turns out to have been a composite all along. *Shroud of Eve* aligns on each source's narrative placement, leaving every Eve where her own picture put her, so the figure recedes under the architecture of her iconography. One generator, one stock, one knob turned: invariants under the move (it is recognisably Eve, recognisably a composite) and a designed transition across it (presence versus recession). This is exactly the perturbation logic the working thesis specifies, realised on a wall.

**The trajectory of inversion is the curvature of `𝒫`.** Across the three works runs an inversion the artist names plainly: the most abstract-seeming work (*Shroud*, the figure all but dissolved) is empirically the most concrete (thirty-five paintings held exactly as they are), and the most concrete source (*Twelve Refractions*, one real Saturday, one named sitter) yields the most abstract image. The apparent abstraction holds the most empirical. The apparent concrete holds the most abstract. That is style as curvature of the parameter space, made visible across a family of three.

***Twelve Refractions* is the bridge.** It takes twenty-four photographs from a single consent-based life-drawing session and processes them twelve ways, held at equal 1/12 weight in a single √2 frame with a 2 cm margin of untouched paper. "Refraction, not fragmentation": one light through twelve lenses, recombined. It is the workshop's own kind of room, run through the averaging pipeline. Hand-accumulation (the four-figure page) and computational averaging (the composite) turn out to be sibling outputs of a common stock: hybrid training, two modalities loading one capacity, the studio and the pipeline conditioning the same eye. The forest, as the statement has it, extends. At the constitutive level (everything is parametric authorship) general life drawing, this workshop, and the averaging matrices are straightforwardly one practice. At the empirical level the honest claim is the narrower one the material already supports, that they are sibling outputs of a common grammar. Marking which level is meant keeps the bold sentence true at the level where it is true.

**Body as interface.** The works and the workshop share a model of the body the repository has elsewhere: skin contains, skin reads, skin displays, skin mediates, at once firewall and open port. To draw a body well is to negotiate that boundary. To average one is the same negotiation by other means.

**The anti-idol payoff.** All of this is, finally, an argument about how to honour a figure without fixing her. To idolise Eve and to blame Eve are one gesture in two coats: both swap a woman for a single picture of one. The layered-averaging works refuse the single image as such. Eve as simultaneously-many is the anti-idol, and it rhymes exactly with the workshop's move at the scale of one body: refuse the one fixed reading, hold the ambiguity, reach for presence instead. The depth that holds her comes from located offset rather than from number alone: n ≥ 2 *located* eyes, never floating ones. Thirty-five locations see around Eve in a way no two could, and still do not possess her, so depth and preserved opacity both rise together with n.

And the algorithm, to be exact about agency, does not author any of this. In the artist's own formulation: "the algorithm does not author the image; the image is afforded by it." The choosing is authored (the source-stock, the alignment, the curation, the 1/N). The press is automatic. This is why the practice is *composed*, not *generated*: the word marks where the agency sits.

### 4.3 Governance as architecture (software engineering)

The fourth discipline the lens crosses is software architecture, and it is not a metaphor. It is the same [SOLID](https://github.com/AndreClements/README/blob/main/docs/lib/SOLID.md) grammar the working thesis runs on. The room instantiates a **Sovereign Interface** of four parts. The consent script is the **Header** (it discloses the defaults: the conceit, the veto, the signal). The Presence Ledger is the **Ledger** (the immutable, signed trace). The stop/rest signal and the Empty Turn are the **Circuit Breaker** (a hard refusal path, a first-class control action). The facilitator's watch, pose by pose, is the **Dignity Linter** (the run-time check that the kept-out features stay out).

Two SOLID moves do real work here. Interface Segregation: consent is many small, purpose-built interfaces (veto a prompt, drop a pose, decline a photo), no single fat agreement that bundles unrelated permissions. And Dependency Inversion, in its dignity form: the room's high-level policy does not depend on the particular bodies or prompts in it; the prompts conform to the policy through an adapter. The prompt-pool veto *is* that adapter (the conformance map κ): a loaded prompt is either made conformant (changed, re-scoped) or blocked. No adapter, no engagement. A prompt that cannot pass the dignity interface does not enter the room.

### 4.4 Benign objectification, tested

Walk Nussbaum's cluster against the actual poses and the account stays honest. Inertness, reduction to appearance, and fungibility are present and courted: a body held still, read as form and weight, one "chair" formally like another. Violability, denial of subjectivity, and silencing are the three the architecture exists to exclude, and the exclusion is enacted as a set of mechanisms rather than asserted as a claim (the veto, the signal, the shared debrief, the fee that opting out never touches). The benign/malignant line runs straight through those three, and the hinge, every time, is who holds the exit.

Here the *if-not* stays live rather than being answered. MacKinnon's objection is not refuted by the apparatus; it is *watched* by it. A facilitator who authored the frame and invited the risk holds more of the room than consent-talk likes to admit. The dignity the exercise depends on is not a switch thrown at the start. It is built and watched, pose by pose, and it can fail at any one of them. And there is a real difference here, held without triumph: where the objectification is named, it can be argued with, refused, renegotiated mid-pose. That is the practice keeping its mobility, its range to adjust under load without the set collapsing. Where it stays ambient, those moves have less to fasten to. That is not a verdict on the conventional life room, which manages its care by other means and often manages it well. It is only an account of what the naming buys in this particular room. The paper does not close the gap between the two. For this inquiry, it builds a room in which the gap stays visible.

One consistency runs under all of this and is worth naming. The apparatus thinks in gradients rather than binaries, and has from the start: the dignity tensor is *scored* rather than flagged, consent is a state machine rather than a checkbox, the benign/malignant line is a threshold on a continuum rather than a wall, and the 1/N is the flattening of a weighting gradient to its evenest setting. Binaries were the foreign body here. The gradient is the native unit, and the sources get read on it like everything else. The move has a name in the operator's vocabulary: parallax with a tunable axis, where the axis is a control surface. You author the axis you read against rather than fixing two poles and standing between them, and the same parties take different relative positions as the parameter turns. The discipline that keeps this load-bearing is to name the axis at each use: an unparameterised gradient is just fog, and naming the axis is what keeps the depth.

### 4.5 The room in training

The body figure has given the paper its anatomy: a spine, organs, a load-bearing joint. What it has not yet named is the physiology, the question of how a body becomes capable. A body is not strong because it was described well. It is strong because it was loaded, recovered, and loaded again, and kept the gains as a change in the tissue. The room becomes a gym for the span of the workshops: a place arranged so a body can take load safely and keep what the load builds. The practice works the same way, and reading it as conditioning makes one more part of the formal apparatus legible without a single new citation.

Recall the loop from [§2](#theoretical-framework-three-bodies-of-thought-one-connective-tissue): stimulus, response, adaptation. The pose is the stimulus, the family of marks is the response, and the adaptation is what the hand keeps, pose to pose and year to year. Said in the trade's plainer words, the practice runs on **progressive overload**. The fourteen-year layered-averaging lineage (since *Aesthesia*, 2012) did not arrive finished. It was loaded a little more each cycle: the prompt pool escalated by agile variants, the source-stock grown, the alignment pressed onto harder axes. Overload is the readable face of the shaping functional `Φ`. To train is to move the parameter the work responds to, on purpose, and to keep what the response builds.

And the two dials a coach actually turns have names the paper already carries under other coats. **Volume** is how much: the count of poses, the four figures to a page, the thirty-five sources held at once. That is the parameter space `𝒫` and its ranges, the breadth of the family. **Intensity** is how hard: how sharply the constraint presses, how far the easy move is closed off, how steep the gradient the axis reads against. That is `Φ` again, felt as load. A practice is periodised by how it sets the two against each other across a session and across a lineage. The dignity floor `η` is the one reading that caps them both: you may raise volume, you may raise intensity, and you may never raise either past the form you refuse to break.

### 4.6 The dark-code instance

The grammar was built in a room, and it reads a second room the author works in daily. A developer now ships code they did not write by hand and often did not read: composed by a machine, gated by tests that may themselves be composed, layered on libraries layered on models nobody on the team has inspected to the bottom. Read through the same apparatus that read the pose and the wall, this is not a new problem. It is the nesting at full depth, and the PA grammar already carries the parts.

The gate is the authored surface. The room read its consent off the Presence Ledger rather than off a declaration. The developer reads correctness off a test suite rather than off the implementation. Both are A6: the recorded decision standing in for the unread interior, the trace doing the work the eye cannot. The defaults are silent co-authors, exactly as the prompt pool was (A4): the model's weights, the framework's conventions, the platform's economics, each authoring the family of outputs unless disclosed and bounded. And the algorithm does not author the result any more than the averaging pipeline authored the composite. The choosing is authored: the interface, the constraints, the tests that say what must hold. The press is automatic. The practice is composed and not generated at the keyboard for the same reason it is on the wall, because the word marks where the agency sits.

What does not distribute is the answering. A test suite is a gate, not an alibi. When the unread layer fails, the account is owed by the one who set the control surfaces and chose to ship this run, the sailor who does not author the wind and answers for the voyage all the same. This is the workshop's move at the scale of a build: refuse neither the breadth nor the responsibility, hold the exit live (the rollback, the kill switch, the refusal path are the Empty Turn given a deploy), and sign the gate you can actually hold.

## 5. Findings / propositions

The findings are tagged to the research questions and, where the evidence is concrete, to the rows of the Presence Ledger (Appendix C).

- **F1 (RQ1).** The bearer of meaning is the mapping, not the single sheet or source: the four-figure page and the thirty-five-source composite are both *families*, and removing any one member changes the family without unmaking it. Authorship is sensitivity-shaping under contract (A1).
- **F2 (RQ2).** Benign objectification is sustained by exit-liveness operating as a *live state machine*, not by a declaration made once at the start. In the worked ledger, a dropped pose, a rest called, and an Empty Turn are all penalty-free, and the benign condition holds because the exit kept evaluating true (A5).
- **F3 (RQ1, RQ2).** The pre-room veto makes the sitter a co-author of the constraint: in the worked example it moves the pool from v1.0 to v1.1, and the family the room then draws is the *post-veto* family. Co-authorship here is typed and real, not ceremonial (A4 inverted).
- **F4 (RQ2).** Vulnerability is distributed, not assigned: the shared breaks and shared debrief, and the fact that looking implicates the looker, are structural refusals of the model-as-victim / artist-as-rescuer flattening (CARDS Relatedness; the Karpman caution).
- **F5 (RQ2).** The stack is auditable: the Presence Ledger renders consent, refusal, and dignity-strain as inspectable rows, which is what lets a practice claim T3-grade evidence rather than anecdote (A6).
- **F6 (RQ3).** A3★ gains defeasible support *and* names its own falsifier ([§6](#the-a3-beauty-thread)).
- **F7 (RQ1).** The workshop (body scale) and the three works (wall scale) are one parametric practice: the 1/N composite and the four-figure page are sibling outputs of one stock. At the constitutive tier this is exact, since the universal claim makes them one; the scoped, empirical reading is the narrower "sibling outputs of a common grammar", and that is the version this finding rests on. And the move that demonstrates this (weaving disparate works into one continuous territory) is itself a function of the operator's voice signature: Landscapify, multipartiality, the distributed signature. The reading is the method performing itself, and this last is a constitutive-tier observation rather than an empirical finding offered as evidence: of course the method reads its own demonstration, which is what a constitutive lens does.
- **F8 (RQ1).** The grammar is wider than the room by design, and the width is the point: the four-figure page, the thirty-five-source composite, and the developer's dark-code build are instances of one practice, the authoring of responsibility and integrity across nested systems (the [Width as rigour](#width-as-rigour-one-practice-across-nested-systems) subsection in §2; [§4.6](#the-dark-code-instance)). At the constitutive tier they are one, since the universal claim makes them one. The scoped, empirical reading is the narrower and load-bearing one: sibling instances of a common grammar, each answering the "too wide" charge by naming what the breadth is for.

## 6. The A3★ beauty thread

The paper's most exposed claim is the operator's own, posited as a research thread and stated here verbatim:

> *Within authored/designed artefacts, the sensation of beauty correlates with (tracks, defeasibly) the legibility of a coherent, generative constraint: one explicitly authored or freely chosen, traceable through its execution, dignity-preserving, and read by the perceiver as enabling rather than merely restrictive. For any constraint realised through a person, "freely chosen" means chosen by the bound person and continuously revocable; the posed figure is in scope only while consent, rough equality, and live exit hold. The dignity gate is the defeater: coherence felt as beautiful while dignity erodes falsifies the claim, it does not satisfy it.* (A3★)

A3★ belongs to the scoped, falsifiable register named at the head of [§2](#theoretical-framework-three-bodies-of-thought-one-connective-tissue): it carries an outside and a defeater precisely because it is bounded down out of the universal, and is not the constitutive claim itself. Three things keep this from inflating into a theory of everything beautiful. First, "tracks" is correlational, not an identity: the claim says beauty *covaries with* legible, dignity-preserving constraint, not that it *is* it. Second, it is bounded, and the boundary is stated: it leaves out the sublime and the wild, formless generosity (sunsets, the sea, which nobody authored). The sublime is what lies past the VO2-max of authorship, the load no conditioning reaches, off the chart that A3★ is drawn on. Botha's aesthetics of risk sits on the far side of that line, and the object-pose stands exactly on the threshold between the two. Third, and most important, the claim carries a defeater rather than only confirmations: coherence felt as beautiful *while dignity erodes* does not satisfy A3★, it falsifies it. In training terms the gate is reps-in-reserve made ethical: the run counts only while there is margin left, an exit not yet spent. A beautiful run made while a sitter's exit was not in fact live is not a strong case for the claim. It is the case that breaks it.

The layered-averaging works are the cleanest test bed precisely because their constraint is so legible. The 1/N rule is explicitly authored, traceable through its execution, and dignity-preserving by construction (no source crowned), and the coherence one feels looking at the composite tracks that legibility closely. The falsifying case is specifiable in the same breath: a composite whose source-stock was harvested without consent. The present-body works do not fall there, because *Twelve Refractions* rests on the session's research-consent, an offer taken up rather than a quiet harvest. The gate is doing its work, which is the most a defeasible claim can honestly offer. Not immunity from failure: a precise account of where it would fail.

What A3★ still lacks, and what a practice-led study can supply without overreaching, is a small empirical handle: an instrument light enough to run in a live room without disturbing it. A short **legibility probe**, put to sitters after a session and to viewers at the wall, would ask four things and no more.

1. *What constraint did you read in the work?* — whether a rule was legible at all, and which one.
2. *Did the constraint feel enabling or restrictive?* — A3★ predicts that the felt beauty tracks the *enabling* reading.
3. *Did dignity seem preserved — yours, the sitter's, the figure's?* — the defeater lives here, since beauty reported while dignity is read as eroded is the falsifying case and not the confirming one.
4. *Where did the work strain?* — the maculate question, naming the place it did not hold.

Four prompts will not prove A3★, and are not meant to. They turn a posited correlation into something a room can actually observe: a legible constraint, an enabling reading, a preserved dignity, and a named strain, logged beside the Presence Ledger rows so the beauty claim answers to evidence the way the consent claims already do. Until the run, A3★ stays bounded, falsifiable, and (the honest word) mostly asserted. The probe is how it begins to stop being only that.

## 7. Governance and research ethics

The study is governed at tier T3 (publication / exhibition), which is the methodology's heaviest tier: a full provenance ledger with a validation method on every external claim, a read-aloud BODY check, at least two Gold-tier sources, typed co-authorship, and a mandatory limitations pass ([§8](#limitations-and-the-and-yet)). The consent, anonymisation, and exit provisions of §3.3 are the research ethics, not separate from them.

One dependency is open and is recorded as open. The University of Pretoria's explicit policy on posing, nudity, and the documentation of sessions has not been confirmed (it is being established through the department; see the internal briefing). Until it is, the run is a workshop, not research data, and the Presence Ledger's research-consent rows stay declined. This is not a hedge; it is the *if-not* kept honest. A provenance fallback is specified for the same reason: if live ledger discipline breaks in a busy room, the trace is reconstructed afterward as a dated, marked retrospective annotation rather than left as a silent gap.

**Host ≠ Author, and co-authorship shown not declared.** The hosted frameworks keep their attribution. And the typed co-authorship the methodology asks for is pointed at concrete preserved agency, not waved at: the sitters co-author the constraint through the veto; the curator ([Sue Clark](https://www.sueclark.co.za/copy-of-object-2024), Equilibrium Studio) authored the occasion and its frame; the vocalist (Ailyn Nienaber) carries the cycle whose reading the visual work sits beside; the recruitment and the policy question run through a named UP colleague (Zita Oranje); and theMachine(s) co-composed this text, named in the colophon rather than hidden in it. None of these collaborators is claimed as having endorsed the argument; each is credited for the agency they in fact exercised. The rights-held libretto and setting are paraphrased only, never reproduced.

## 8. Limitations, and the And-Yet

This is a single room, and it makes no industrial claim. Its external validity is the validity of a well-documented case, not a population.

And the opposite charge is owed an answer in the same breath, because the paper invites both at once. If one reader finds it too narrow, a single room standing in for a population, another will find it too wide, a grammar reaching from charcoal to software architecture to a wall of composites and calling them one thing. The width is avowed rather than trimmed: it is the practice the paper is about, the keeping of responsibility and integrity across nested systems, and a lens cut narrower than its terrain would simply fail to reach the joints it was made to read. *Take it or leave it* is the honest register here. The breadth carries its own failure mode, and the methodology names the discipline that contains it: a grammar wide enough to read anything can be tuned to read nothing, so the axis is named at each use ([§4.4](#benign-objectification-tested)), and an unnamed axis is fog rather than depth. Width held to that discipline is rigour. Width let off it is the very scatter the critic fears.

The facilitator's authored power is real and is not dissolved by naming it. MacKinnon's objection survives the apparatus, and should: consent under asymmetry can be hollow, and the most a built room can do is keep the asymmetry visible and the exit live. A3★ is posited, not proven; correlational, not causal; bounded, not total. The legibility probe specified in [§6](#the-a3-beauty-thread) is the modest first instrument for testing it, no more, and the claim stays mostly asserted until that probe has run. The universal claim sits at the other tier: it is unfalsifiable by design ([§2](#theoretical-framework-three-bodies-of-thought-one-connective-tissue)), which is a statement of its type rather than a hole in it, and the two-tier cut is what keeps that immunity from leaking into the scoped claims around it. And the worked ledger is illustrative until the real run, so the strongest empirical claims here are claims about a designed practice and its lineage (the layered-averaging method runs back to the operator's 2012 debut), not yet about a single dated afternoon. A lineage is a training log written in scar tissue: a record of which loads were survived and kept, fourteen years of them, and an honest log shows the strains as plainly as the gains.

There is one limit the emergent ethic cannot cross, and §8 should avow it rather than paper over it. The 1/N protects dignity among the parties already inside the coupling. It has nothing to say to the one who refuses the frame entirely (who will not enter the room, or who rejects multipartiality as such), and an emergent ethic, honestly described, *can* have nothing to say there: that silence is the price of being emergent rather than absolute. What the room can do is keep the Empty Turn open both inward and outward: a welcome for the *no* that arrives from outside the frame, an acknowledgement folded into the architecture that the architecture does not reach everyone it concerns. This does not solve the externality of refusal. It avows it. Not solved: avowed. The door stands open in both directions, and some who are owed a place will decline the room. The honest structure is the one that says so.

Two kinds of unknown sit under that avowal, and they are distinct in kind and not in degree. The *unknown unknown* is epistemic and contingent: not known now, knowable later, a humility about the present state of knowledge. The *withdrawn unknown* is ontological and permanent: the recession of every object from all relation, never exhaustible by anyone, a humility about knowability as such. An Empty Turn that bows to both bows to two floors at once. And the loop closes here, because the withdrawn unknown is the remainder the paper has been circling all along: dignity as being seen *with* remainder, the surplus that neither under-seeing nor over-seeing may capture. The discipline is to hold OOO as a regulative posture and not a metaphysical purchase (interested with, but not claiming, it): treat every party as harbouring a remainder you cannot exhaust, without buying Harman's full system. That is *Host ≠ Author* applied to OOO, and it is the correct restraint.

The honesty the show's own materials insist on belongs here too, unresolved. A man averaging images of Eve is not a woman singing them. The formal structure (thirty-five voices at equal volume, a forest in which no tree dominates the canopy) permits a feminist-informed reading; it does not claim one. And yet the structure is what it is. The composite is honest about being a representation of representations: there is no recovered "who"; Eve arrives already a category, a way the West learned to picture a woman. A man does what he can with his looking, and says so.

And the last and least comfortable turn, made in the open because the methodology requires it: this paper, a meditation on how knowledge gets objectified, was itself composed with the multi-agent machines now unsettling the academy whose forms it borrows. The gaze that objectifies a body and the system that objectifies knowledge are not distant cousins. They are the same move at different scales, and the paper runs both at once. The shelter it describes has to be large enough to hold the sitter and the scholar, the drawn body and the automated thought, and to admit, at the foot of the page, that one of the hands that built it is also among the things it is trying to protect. This paper is itself a piece of dark code, and the avowal has to go that far. Its claims ship gated behind the validation stack printed in its own back matter, the provenance ledger and the sceptic passes and the BODY check still pending, rather than behind a reader who inspected every inferential step. A gate is not the same as a reading, and the back matter says so plainly. What fills the gap is the only thing that ever fills it: an answerable maker who signs, and carries the account for what the arrangement brought forth. Maculate, then: navigation, not purification.

## 9. Conclusion — the page keeps the trace

Parametric authorship relocates agency to sensitivity design under contract, and in doing so it lets one lens read a life-drawing room, an ethics of consent, a piece of software architecture, and three composite pictures as one practice. Meaning lives in the map, not the single mark. The room, at its best, is a shelter: less a purification of the gaze than a structure that holds the vectors of integrity (dignity, consent, authorship, risk, attention) apart and intact, in tension, without flattening any of them. The composite Eve withdraws, and her withdrawal is perhaps her unfurling. The practice declines both the pedestal and the gutter, and reaches for presence instead.

The mark is meat-noise. The mapping is world-creating. The work is to keep authoring the rule that holds the two at equal weight, no source crowned, the exit live, and the door open. *En tog.* I invite you to look closely, and to dwell.
