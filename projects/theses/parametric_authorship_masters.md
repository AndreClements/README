## Abstract

This practice-led paper tests one claim. The claim — the **felt-beauty claim** — is that the felt beauty of an authored artefact tracks, defeasibly, the legibility of a coherent, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint. The lens is *parametric authorship* (PA): authoring the *rules* that produce a *family* of works, where meaning lives in the mapping from rule to family rather than in any single mark. The case is the *Objectification Workshop* designed for the Eve Song happening (University of Pretoria, 2026), read together with three layered-averaging works exhibited in the same show, in which thirty-five historical images of Eve are held at equal weight (1/N) until a figure emerges that none of them alone contains. PA's formal apparatus is cited from a companion working thesis and not re-derived: the paper carries inline only what it leans on, the Parametric Author Function tuple, the map-over-mark axiom (A1), and the dignity-conservation condition (A3). The argument turns on one load-bearing joint, the place where a formal decision and an ethical one prove to be the same: equal weighting (1/N) as dignity conservation, no source crowned. This is the cleanest test bed for the felt-beauty claim, since the 1/N constraint is at once the most legible and the dignity guarantee. A bespoke *presence ledger* turns the room into auditable evidence, and a short legibility probe, specified here and run viewer-side on the existing works ahead of the live workshop, gives the beauty claim its first contact with data. The felt-beauty claim carries its own defeater: coherence felt as beautiful while dignity erodes falsifies the claim rather than confirming it. Governance follows METHODOLOGY_CII at tier T3.

**Keywords:** parametric authorship; beauty; constraint; objectification; consent; dignity; life drawing; layered averaging; practice-led research; South African contemporary art.

---

> **A small glossary.**
> **Parametric authorship (PA)** — authoring the *rules* that produce a *family* of works, rather than inscribing one finished work. **The family** — the set of works a constraint yields; the unit of the practice. **PAF** — the Parametric Author Function, the authored tuple `⟨S, 𝒫, C, E, Φ⟩`: Substrates, Parameter space, Constraints, Environment, and a Shaping functional (how sensitively the work responds when you move a setting). **A1, Map-over-Mark** — meaning is borne by the mapping from rules to family, not by any single mark. **A3** is the *dignity-conservation* condition: dignity scored across three scopes (personal, object, system), conserved only while their product stays positive and none falls below a declared floor `η`. **Felt beauty** (the *beauty* claim) is the posited claim that felt beauty tracks, defeasibly, a coherent, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint — the authored-case **species** of the genus (art as the appreciable manifestation of creativity; see [theory_of_art](https://github.com/AndreClements/README/blob/main/docs/models/theory_of_art.md)), developed in [§6](#the-felt-beauty-thread). **Hyperstrate** — the cross-cutting layer of tools, defaults, policies, and logistics that any authoring runs *on*. **Maculate** — marked, flawed, worked-with rather than purified. **As-if / if-not / and-yet** — engage provisionally; audit where it strains; hold affordance for contradiction and externalities.

## 1. Introduction — From the unnamed gaze to the designed constraint

To draw from life is to look hard at a person and make of them, for a while, an object of attention. For the length of a pose they become, to the hand, less a whole person than a problem of edges: a line, a mass, the weight of a shoulder under a fall of light. The life room has long had good reasons to leave that mostly unspoken: a working focus to keep, a courtesy to the sitter, a craft whose conventions were earned honestly and still serve. This paper does not arrive to correct the tradition, nor to claim that naming is the better way. It begins where a more pointed companion essay leaves off (Clements, [*Objectively Objectifying Objectification*](https://andresclements.com/objectively-objectifying-objectification/)) by making one dynamic explicit rather than ambient (and only the one the inquiry turns on), then asking what is left of authorship once the looking is named. What it offers is a method, set beside the conventional one rather than over it, and read best as neither confession nor accusation.

I was invited to exhibit. I have decided to treat that invitation *as-if* it were a piece of presented research: provisionally, instrumentally, and with a straight face about the rigour even while keeping the tone light. The stance is borrowed wholesale from my README repository's engagement methodology (METHODOLOGY_CII §1): engage *as-if*, audit *if-not*, hold for the *and-yet*. *If-not* a formal presentation, then still: work made that I can stand behind, shown well. *And yet*: the theory of language at the foot of my own README applies here; Language is a magical, world-creating force. Language is also just the noise made by a piece of meat in a mouth. The two readings appears to not resolve. A held pose is the same shape of problem. A body worked *as-if* a chair is world-creating (it makes a study, a family, a small room of meaning) and it is also just a body, meat under a fall of light. Meaning fans out across the gap between those readings and never quite collapses to either. That is a engine of sorts for everything below.

The paper studies one practice at two scales. At the scale of a body: the **Objectification Workshop**, in which sitters pose as objects (a chair, a doorway, a tree) under actively held consent, and a small room draws them. At the scale of a wall: three composite works in the same exhibition ([*Shroud of Eve*](https://andresclements.com/artwork/shroud-of-eve/), [*Study of Eve*](https://andresclements.com/artwork/study-of-eve/), [*Twelve Refractions*](https://andresclements.com/artwork/twelve-refractions/)), each made by holding many source images at equal weight until a figure emerges that none of them alone contains. The two are one practice read at two scales, and parametric authorship is the frame that holds them together. The 1/N, equal weight with no source crowned, is the load-bearing joint: the place where a formal decision and an ethical one turn out to be the same decision (under conditions [§4.2](#the-layered-averaging-works-parametric-authorship-at-exhibition-scale) makes explicit, since equal weighting prevents crowning without, on its own, guaranteeing dignity). And it is the cleanest case the paper has for the one claim it sets out to test: that the felt beauty of an authored artefact tracks, defeasibly, a legible, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint. The paper calls that the felt-beauty claim, and the rest of the work is built to put it where evidence can reach it.

The posture throughout is mediating rather than adjudicating, in a conflict-resilience register. The several lenses meet in this one room, and the aim is to let them hold one another in tension rather than to crown any of them. Where the paper presses, it presses on a specific, clearly articulated dynamic (whether the exit stays live, whether the consent can be audited), and not on the conventional practice as such, which keeps its merit and often keeps its care by means of its own. What follows is offered as a lens: polished, and pointed here at one room. Where a reader points it next, and what they see through it, stays theirs. The paper holds no attachment to the view.

**Research questions.**

1. **(RQ1)** What is authorship when the work is a *family* (of drawings, of composites) produced under a chosen constraint, rather than a single inscribed mark?
2. **(RQ2)** Under what room conditions does deliberate objectification stay *benign* in Nussbaum's (1995) sense, and how does a governance architecture make those conditions legible and auditable rather than merely asserted?
3. **(RQ3)** Does the felt beauty of such an artefact track the legibility of a coherent, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint (the felt-beauty claim), and what would falsify that?

RQ3 is the question the paper stakes itself on. RQ1 and RQ2 prepare the ground for it: what authorship becomes once the work is a family, and what keeps the family's constraint dignity-preserving, so that RQ3 can be asked of a room that has earned the asking.

A note on register, because the paper will keep two of them in one room. It is academic where it reasons and hedges; it is plainer, and occasionally bodily, where it does not. This is deliberate, and it is methodological: the operator's voice signature treats [multipartiality](https://github.com/AndreClements/README/blob/main/docs/lib/MULTI_PARTIALITY.md) (standing with more than one party at once, without collapsing the difference) as an ethical stance, not a stylistic flourish. A paper about holding many readings at equal weight might choose hold its own registers that way too.

## 2. Theoretical framework — objectification, constraint, and the dignity gate

Two registers of claim run through this paper, and they answer to different rules. At its widest reach the hypothesis is constitutive: everything is parametric authorship, a body bringing forth a world by working its own surfaces, taking a stimulus, making a response, keeping the adaptation as a mark in the tissue (Maturana and Varela 1980; Thompson 2007, the autopoietic ancestor read through authorship). A claim that forbids no observation has no empirical content in Popper's sense, and this one forbids none: it is offered in the register of constitutive claims, judged by fertility and coherence rather than by a crucial experiment. The felt-beauty claim and the room-claims sit below that line, and the line is the point. They are *scoped*: they carry defeaters, name their boundaries, and answer to the Presence Ledger and to evidence. The felt-beauty claim has an outside (the sublime, the unchosen, the formless) and a falsifier (coherence felt as beautiful while dignity erodes) only because it is bounded down out of the universal and is not the universal itself. The cut keeps the unfalsifiable claim from borrowing the empirical credit of the apparatus around it, and keeps the scoped claims from inheriting its immunity to disproof. This paper spends almost all its weight below the line, on the felt-beauty claim and the room.

Ethics sits at that boundary, emergent rather than imported. The dignity gate is not an absolute and not a law of the universe: it arises within the coupling of the parties in the room, contingent, revocable, re-earned pose by pose, and causally real for all that. An emergent ethic runs one standing risk, that it collapse into the frame of whoever is strongest in the room. The 1/N is the answer, the paper's own central equivalence doing ethical service: the floor is held by the equal-weighted quorum and not by any single author, dignity conserved as a product across all scopes so that no surplus anywhere can buy the flattening of anyone. And the same boundary locates responsibility: authorship may distribute, but the account is owed by the one who set the control surfaces and chose to launch the run, the sailor who sets the sails and the tiller, not the wind, and answers for the voyage all the same. *En tog.*

The framework carries three bodies of thought the case needs: the ethics and aesthetics of objectification (§2.2), the creativity of constraints (§2.3), and an account of risk and vulnerability that sets the dignity gate (§2.4). Parametric authorship is named first, by reference, since it is the frame the other three are read through.

### 2.1 Parametric authorship, by reference

The formal apparatus of parametric authorship is set out in a companion working thesis (Clements, *The Fourth Dimension as Execution*) and is not re-derived here. This paper carries only what the case needs.

Authorship, in PA, is the design of control surfaces: you articulate a rule-set, set ranges, fix what must hold, and let a generator instantiate a family. It is setting the sails, as well as the till. The authored object is a tuple, the **Parametric Author Function**:

$$\mathrm{PAF} := \langle\, S,\ \mathcal{P},\ C,\ E,\ \Phi \,\rangle$$

Substrates `S` (tools, materials, models); a parameter space `𝒫` (the knobs, and their ranges); constraints `C` (what will not be done); environment `E` (budgets, governance, the weather you do not control: the *hyperstrate* any authoring runs on); and a shaping functional `Φ` (how sensitively the work responds when you move a parameter). The companion thesis carries the rest of the apparatus; this paper leans only on the two pieces that follow.

The one axiom this paper leans its full weight on is **A1, Map-over-Mark**: *meaning is borne by stable features of the mapping from parameters to artefacts, not by any single token.* And the one formal condition it needs is dignity conservation, **A3**: dignity is scored across three scopes (personal, object, system), and is conserved only while their product stays positive and none falls below a declared floor:

$$\mathrm{conserve}(D) := \Bigl(\textstyle\prod_i d_i > 0\Bigr) \,\wedge\, \Bigl(\min_i d_i \geq \eta\Bigr)$$

The product is the load-bearing part. If any one dignity goes to zero, the whole goes to zero. There is no averaging your way out of a flattened person. The declared floor `η` is the form you refuse to break under load: the point past which a pose is abandoned rather than finished badly.

### 2.2 Objectification: the ethics and the aesthetics

The case is objectification taken at its word, so the framework's spine is Martha Nussbaum's (1995; 1999) analysis. Nussbaum's decisive move is to treat objectification as a cluster of analytically distinct features rather than a single thing, several of which, under the right conditions, can be benign rather than harmful. The workshop courts a few of these on purpose (inertness; reduction to body and appearance; fungibility) and works to keep three others firmly out of the room (violability; denial of subjectivity; silencing). Rae Langton (2009) adds three more features to the cluster and sharpens the account of when instrumentality curdles.

The benign reading has a critic, and the paper keeps her in the room rather than answering her too quickly. Catharine MacKinnon's objection (engaged here via the repository's hosted entry, [`OBJECTIFICATION.md`](https://github.com/AndreClements/README/blob/main/docs/lib/OBJECTIFICATION.md), attribution hers) is that under conditions of structural inequality, "consent" can be hollow from the start. This is not a footnote to be discharged. It is the live *if-not* that the consent architecture in [§4.3](#benign-objectification-tested) exists to audit.

The aesthetic counter-weight comes from [object-oriented ontology](https://github.com/AndreClements/README/blob/main/docs/lib/OOO.md). Graham Harman (2011) holds that every object, human and not, keeps a withdrawn interior that exceeds any relation to it. Read through OOO, the deepest claim of the workshop inverts: to pose as a chair, taken seriously, does not degrade a person into a thing. It asks the room to grant the pose the withdrawn interior a real chair is already owed. Objectification, on this reading, is not the making of an object; it is the *denial* of the dignity an object already has. This is a transposition, and is marked as one: the move from Harman's metaphysical withdrawal to an ethical claim about dignity is offered, not derived. One cannot strictly entail the ought from the withdrawal, and the paper does not pretend to (*Host ≠ Author*, applied to OOO itself). The same withdrawal runs through the exhibition works, where the composite Eve, in the operator's own phrase, withdraws: and her withdrawal is perhaps also her unfurling.

### 2.3 The creativity of constraints

That a limit can be generative rather than merely restrictive is an old and well-attested idea, and the paper draws on its lineage rather than claiming it: Stravinsky's *Poetics of Music* (1942/47) on the freedom found inside self-imposed bounds; Boden's (1990/2004) account of constraint as the condition of creative search; Stokes (2005) on creativity *from* constraints; Elster (1999) on binding oneself to be more free; Kant (1790) on purposive form; and the experimental support gathered by Haught-Tromp (2017). The operator's voice has a name for this: the **Constraint Gift**. A prompt that closes the easy move ("do not draw a portrait, draw a doorway") gathers the attention and presses the hand toward the essential line. The limit reads as the thing that lets a family of marks cohere: the closed-off easy move is the load the hand works against, and the essential line is the strength that working against it builds. The felt-beauty claim stands on this lineage. If a freely-chosen constraint is what lets a family cohere, then the constraint read as *enabling* rather than merely restrictive is exactly the legibility the beauty claim predicts a perceiver will feel.

### 2.4 Risk, vulnerability, and the dignity gate

Marc Botha's "Risk" (2021) does the last piece of framing work, and it is the piece that keeps the aesthetics honest. Botha distinguishes risk *taken* from risk *imposed*: "when you externalise risk onto a vulnerable body, it becomes harm." That restates the benign/malignant line in the language of risk. Benign where the sitter takes the risk and holds it, exit live; malignant where the risk is laid on her. Botha's further claim, that agency itself requires vulnerability, meets the workshop's central paradox directly: choosing to bind oneself into an object-pose is an act of agency, a taking-on, not a giving-up. This is the threshold on which the whole study balances, and it sets up the defeater in [§6](#the-felt-beauty-thread).

Throughout, the framework is held in the maculate register METHODOLOGY_CII insists on: all of these systems are flawed, and the practice navigates the flaws rather than purifying them. And throughout, **Host ≠ Author**. Nussbaum's, Langton's, Harman's, and Botha's frameworks are hosted here as working dependencies, attribution theirs. The transpositions, and the posited felt-beauty claim, are the author's, marked as his.

## 3. Method — practice-led, the workshop as instrument

### 3.1 The practice-led stance

This is not a study *about* a practice. The workshop *is* the inquiry, and the drawings, the vetoes, the dropped poses, and the debrief are its data. The methodology's risk heuristic applies: `risk_index = complexity × power × distance_from_wetware` (METHODOLOGY_CII §1). A hands-on drawing room is low on distance-from-wetware (everything happens in bodies, in a room, in person), which raises the evidential weight of what is felt there and lowers the opacity that haunts screen-mediated work. It also concentrates the power asymmetry in one place: the facilitator, who authored the frame and invited the risk, holds more of the room than he likes to admit. The method does not dissolve that asymmetry. It makes it visible and watches it.

### 3.2 Site and constraint design

The site is the UP 2-1 Gallery (University of Pretoria), within the run of the Eve Song happening (exhibition 20 July – 14 August 2026; workshops 27 July – 13 August). The bookable session has an [event page](https://andresclements.com/events/objectification-workshop/) and a [poster](https://andresclements.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/eve-song-objectification-workshop-poster.jpg) on the operator's site (both forthcoming: the site's events section is in build). Each object-prompt is a small PAF: the substrate is charcoal and A2 paper and a body; the parameter space is the prompt and its agile variants; the constraints are the dignity floor and the kept-out features of §2.2; the environment is the gallery, the timing, the policy; and the shaping functional is the sensitivity of the family of drawings to the choice of pose. The pose runs about fifteen minutes and is drawn **four figures to a page**: the page is a small matrix of the session, not a precious sheet, the value in the accumulation and never in any single one. The full constraint vocabulary is versioned in the prompt pool (`prompt-pool.md`).

### 3.3 The consent and safety architecture as method

The architecture is not a pastoral add-on. It is the experimental apparatus, because it is what holds the benign conditions in place long enough to observe them.[^solid]

- The prompt pool is **co-authored and vetoed by the sitters before the room ever sees it.** In PA terms this is A4 (Defaults Author) handled correctly: a prompt pool is a silent co-author of the family it yields, and the veto discloses and bounds it, in doing so making the sitter a real co-author of the constraint. This is the change room of the practice: the threshold where the object-conceit is tried on or refused, before anyone steps onto the floor.
- A **stop/rest signal**, agreed and honoured at any moment, no reason owed. This is the Empty Turn (METHODOLOGY_CII §9.3) given a body: the sovereign right to stop, which is "not a failure but a dignified exit." It is the withdrawal reflex raised to a first-class control action, the hand off the hot surface made part of the architecture rather than left to nerve.
- The right to come **off the stand mid-pose**, and **opting out never touches the fee.** Financial safety belongs to exit-liveness: an exit you cannot afford is not an exit.
- **Shared, casual breaks and a shared debrief**, the rest built into the session rather than waited for. Vulnerability is distributed, not assigned: to look, and to be seen looking, implicates the artist too.
- **Documentation only by specific consent, anonymised by default**, and (see [§7](#governance-and-research-ethics)) no research use of any session until the University's posing/documentation policy is confirmed.

[^solid]: Read in software terms, this architecture is a SOLID (Martin 2017) "Sovereign Interface": the consent script as Header (it discloses the defaults), the Presence Ledger as the signed Ledger, the stop signal and the Empty Turn as Circuit Breaker, the facilitator's watch as a run-time Dignity Linter. That mapping is developed in the companion working thesis and not pursued here.

### 3.4 The instrument: the Presence Ledger

A study needs an instrument that turns events into evidence. Borrowed from the working thesis's governance stack, the instrument here is a **provenance ledger** specialised for a live room: a **Presence Ledger** (`templates/presence_ledger.md`; instance at `presence-ledger.md`). Where a provenance ledger records claims and assets, a presence ledger records *presence and consent events*: a vetoed prompt, a rest called, a pose dropped with pay unaffected, a photo-consent granted and scoped, a dignity-flag raised and resolved, an Empty Turn. Each row names the axiom or CARDS axis it discharges, so the sheet *shows* the ethic operating rather than asserting it. A single row reads like a quick vital sign for the room: a small, cheap measure that stands proxy for the health of the whole. This is the indexical trace of A6: in parametric authorship the "stroke" is the recorded decision, and consent, recorded, is a kind of mark.

The worked example in Appendix C is illustrative until the real run. It is shown here, before the findings, on purpose: a method instrument has to be seen working before it can be trusted to carry an argument. (Fiertek's 2013 *Performance Guide to Eve-Song*, a document that is also its own research form, is the structural model for treating an operational artefact as a scholarly one.)

## 4. Case analysis — reading the room, and the wall, in PA

### 4.1 The pose as rule-manifold (semiotics)

Saussure (1916) and Peirce gave us a sign that points: a vehicle, a referent, an interpretant. Parametric authorship extends the triangle to generated families. The prompt is the **rule manifold** (`𝒫`); the family of drawings is the **generated vehicle family** (`𝒜`); the room's reading is the **interpretant field**. Meaning, in this frame, is what stays stable when the parameters are nudged (invariants) and what changes predictably when they are shocked on purpose (designed transitions). Style is the curvature: the way the family bends as the prompt moves.

The chair-pose is perhaps the cleanest instance, and it is the held tension the introduction set running as the engine of the paper: its meaning is *designed undecidability*. You cannot tell, from outside, whether a body folded into a chair has been diminished or honoured. The cycle that occasions the show says it more economically than any theory: to be visible is at once to be warned and to be invited, with no way to know which. That ambiguity is not noise in the sign. It is the sign. A1 holds: the meaning is in the mapping (this prompt, held by this room, under this consent), not in any one drawing of it.

### 4.2 The layered-averaging works: parametric authorship at exhibition scale

The three composite works in the show are the same move at the scale of a wall, and reading them is where the paper's central claim earns its keep. I anchor it in the material decisions, not in resemblance.

**Layered averaging is A1 made literal.** Thirty-five historical paintings of Eve (Dürer, Cranach, Masaccio, Michelangelo, Rubens, Valadon, and more) are aligned and averaged at equal weight, "yielding a figure that none contains alone." The rule-set plus the weighting is the rule manifold. The composite is the family. The meaning is in the mapping. No single painting is the sign. The sum of how she has been pictured is the sign.

**The 1/N is the dignity move, and this is the equivalence the whole paper turns on.** The decision to hold every source at equal one-thirty-fifth weight is, in the operator's own words from the work's statement, "the least technical and the most load-bearing decision in the whole pipeline: equal weight, no Eve crowned over the others, a quorum rather than a queue." Read formally, 1/N is a weighting. Read ethically, it is dignity conservation across the scope of all the sources: none flattened to zero, none crowned. The formal decision and the ethical decision are the *same decision*. One qualification belongs right here, planted with the equivalence rather than left for §6: equal weighting prevents crowning, but it does not by itself manufacture dignity. Provenance, consent, the conditions under which the sources were gathered, and the framing the room reads them through all still bear on it. The 1/N conserves dignity across sources only while those hold, and [§6](#the-felt-beauty-thread) names the case (a stock harvested without consent) where a perfectly even weighting would fail anyway. The arithmetic is necessary, and it is not sufficient. With that said: this is multipartiality (the operator's term for standing with more than one party at once without collapsing the difference) written as arithmetic. And it is the same ethic as the workshop's refusal to curate for the one best drawing: no precious sheet, no crowned source. The body-scale practice and the wall-scale practice share one commitment, and it is a number. It is the centre the rest of the practice turns on, in the room and on the wall alike.

**Shroud and Study are a designed transition.** The two works run the *same* thirty-five-source stock through *one* changed parameter: the alignment axis. *Study of Eve* aligns on the body, and the canonical standing nude turns out to have been a composite all along. *Shroud of Eve* aligns on each source's narrative placement, leaving every Eve where her own picture put her, so the figure recedes under the architecture of her iconography. One generator, one stock, one knob turned: invariants under the move (it is recognisably Eve, recognisably a composite) and a designed transition across it (presence versus recession). This is exactly the perturbation logic the working thesis specifies, realised on a wall.

**The trajectory of inversion is the curvature of `𝒫`.** Across the three works runs an inversion the artist names plainly: the most abstract-seeming work (*Shroud*, the figure all but dissolved) is empirically the most concrete (thirty-five paintings held exactly as they are), and the most concrete source (*Twelve Refractions*, one real Saturday, one named sitter) yields the most abstract image. The apparent abstraction holds the most empirical. The apparent concrete holds the most abstract. That is style as curvature of the parameter space, made visible across a family of three.

***Twelve Refractions* is the bridge.** It takes twenty-four photographs from a single consent-based life-drawing session and processes them twelve ways, held at equal 1/12 weight in a single √2 frame with a 2 cm margin of untouched paper. "Refraction, not fragmentation": one light through twelve lenses, recombined. It is the workshop's own kind of room, run through the averaging pipeline. Hand-accumulation (the four-figure page) and computational averaging (the composite) turn out to be sibling outputs of a common stock: the studio and the pipeline drawing the same eye. The forest, as the statement has it, extends. At the constitutive level (everything is parametric authorship) general life drawing, this workshop, and the averaging matrices are straightforwardly one practice. At the empirical level the honest claim is the narrower one the material already supports, that they are sibling outputs of a common grammar. Marking which level is meant keeps the bold sentence true at the level where it is true.

**Body as interface.** The works and the workshop share a model of the body the repository has elsewhere: skin contains, skin reads, skin displays, skin mediates, at once firewall and open port. To draw a body well is to negotiate that boundary. To average one is the same negotiation by other means.

**The anti-idol payoff.** All of this is, finally, an argument about how to honour a figure without fixing her. To idolise Eve and to blame Eve are one gesture in two coats: both swap a woman for a single picture of one. The layered-averaging works refuse the single image as such. Eve as simultaneously-many is the anti-idol, and it rhymes exactly with the workshop's move at the scale of one body: refuse the one fixed reading, hold the ambiguity, reach for presence instead. The depth that holds her comes from located offset rather than from number alone: n ≥ 2 *located* eyes, never floating ones. Thirty-five locations see around Eve in a way no two could, and still do not possess her, so depth and preserved opacity both rise together with n.

And the algorithm, to be exact about agency, does not author any of this. In the artist's own formulation: "the algorithm does not author the image; the image is afforded by it." The choosing is authored (the source-stock, the alignment, the curation, the 1/N). The press is automatic. This is why the practice is *composed*, not *generated*: the word marks where the agency sits.

### 4.3 Benign objectification, tested

Walk Nussbaum's cluster against the actual poses and the account stays honest. Inertness, reduction to appearance, and fungibility are present and courted: a body held still, read as form and weight, one "chair" formally like another. Violability, denial of subjectivity, and silencing are the three the architecture exists to exclude, and the exclusion is enacted as a set of mechanisms rather than asserted as a claim (the veto, the signal, the shared debrief, the fee that opting out never touches). The benign/malignant line runs straight through those three, and the hinge, every time, is who holds the exit.

Here the *if-not* stays live rather than being answered. MacKinnon's objection is not refuted by the apparatus; it is *watched* by it. A facilitator who authored the frame and invited the risk holds more of the room than consent-talk likes to admit. The dignity the exercise depends on is not a switch thrown at the start. It is built and watched, pose by pose, and it can fail at any one of them. And there is a real difference here, held without triumph: where the objectification is named, it can be argued with, refused, renegotiated mid-pose. That is the practice keeping its mobility, its range to adjust under load without the set collapsing. Where it stays ambient, those moves have less to fasten to. That is not a verdict on the conventional life room, which manages its care by other means and often manages it well. It is only an account of what the naming buys in this particular room. The paper does not close the gap between the two. For this inquiry, it builds a room in which the gap stays visible.

One consistency runs under all of this and is worth naming. The apparatus thinks in gradients rather than binaries, and has from the start: dignity is *scored* so it can move under load, consent runs as a state machine that keeps re-evaluating, the benign/malignant line is a threshold on a continuum, and the 1/N is a weighting gradient flattened to its evenest setting. The gradient is the native unit, and the sources get read on it like everything else. The discipline that keeps it load-bearing is to name the axis being read against at each use: an unnamed gradient is just fog, and naming it is what keeps the depth.

## 5. Findings / propositions

The findings are tagged to the research questions and, where the evidence is concrete, to the rows of the Presence Ledger (Appendix C).

- **F1 (RQ1).** The bearer of meaning is the mapping, not the single sheet or source: the four-figure page and the thirty-five-source composite are both *families*, and removing any one member changes the family without unmaking it. Authorship is sensitivity-shaping under contract (A1).
- **F2 (RQ2).** Benign objectification is sustained by exit-liveness operating as a *live state machine*, not by a declaration made once at the start. In the worked ledger, a dropped pose, a rest called, and an Empty Turn are all penalty-free, and the benign condition holds because the exit kept evaluating true (A5, Exit-Liveness).
- **F3 (RQ1, RQ2).** The pre-room veto makes the sitter a co-author of the constraint: in the worked example it moves the pool from v1.0 to v1.1, and the family the room then draws is the *post-veto* family. Co-authorship here is typed and real, not ceremonial (A4 inverted).
- **F4 (RQ2).** Vulnerability is distributed, not assigned: the shared breaks and shared debrief, and the fact that looking implicates the looker, are structural refusals of the model-as-victim / artist-as-rescuer flattening (CARDS Relatedness; the Karpman caution).
- **F5 (RQ2).** The stack is auditable: the Presence Ledger renders consent, refusal, and dignity-strain as inspectable rows, which is what lets a practice claim T3-grade evidence rather than anecdote (A6).
- **F6 (RQ3).** The felt-beauty claim gains defeasible support *and* names its own falsifier ([§6](#the-felt-beauty-thread)).
- **F7 (RQ1).** The workshop (body scale) and the three works (wall scale) are one parametric practice: the 1/N composite and the four-figure page are sibling outputs of one stock. Constitutively, one practice; empirically, the scoped reading this finding rests on — sibling outputs of a common grammar.

## 6. The felt-beauty thread

Before the claim, the frame it is a species of. Art, in the operator's standing theory ([theory_of_art](https://github.com/AndreClements/README/blob/main/docs/models/theory_of_art.md)), is the appreciable manifestation of creativity, and creativity is the making of new relations between elements *towards the desirable* — where the desirable is not a constant but a dependent variable, fluttering across scales, domains of concern, and agencies. Beauty, on that account, is one *mode* of appreciation: the mode in which a setting of the desirable reads as coherence. The claim this section tests is the **authored-case species** of that genus — felt beauty, at the particular setting where the desirable is dignity-preserving coherence made through a freely-chosen, legible constraint. It is not a theory of all beauty, and it does not need to be: the sublime, the abject, and the dissonant are other settings the genus holds and this claim declines to speak for. That is what keeps the claim honest rather than anti-aesthetic — it is a species, not the whole, and there is no outside to the aesthetic to fall into.

The paper's most exposed claim is the operator's own, posited as a research thread and stated here verbatim:

> *Within authored/designed artefacts, the sensation of beauty correlates with (tracks, defeasibly) the legibility of a coherent, generative constraint: one explicitly authored or freely chosen, traceable through its execution, dignity-preserving, and read by the perceiver as enabling rather than merely restrictive. For any constraint realised through a person, "freely chosen" means chosen by the bound person and continuously revocable; the posed figure is in scope only while consent, rough equality, and live exit hold. The dignity gate is the defeater: coherence felt as beautiful while dignity erodes falsifies the claim, it does not satisfy it.* (the felt-beauty claim)

The felt-beauty claim belongs to the scoped, falsifiable register named at the head of [§2](#theoretical-framework-objectification-constraint-and-the-dignity-gate): it carries an outside and a defeater precisely because it is bounded down out of the universal, and is not the constitutive claim itself. Three things keep this from inflating into a theory of everything beautiful. First, "tracks" is correlational, not an identity: the claim says beauty *covaries with* legible, dignity-preserving constraint, not that it *is* it. Second, it is bounded, and the boundary is stated: it leaves out the sublime and the wild, formless generosity (sunsets, the sea, which nobody authored). The sublime is what lies past the reach of authorship, the formlessness no chosen constraint produces, off the chart the felt-beauty claim is drawn on (du Preez 2022, on the sublime as the unmasterable, sits on this line). Botha's aesthetics of risk sits on the far side of that line, and the object-pose stands exactly on the threshold between the two. Third, and most important, the claim carries a defeater rather than only confirmations: coherence felt as beautiful *while dignity erodes* does not satisfy the felt-beauty claim, it falsifies it. The gate is a matter of margin: the run counts only while a sitter's exit is still live, not yet spent. A beautiful run made while that exit was not in fact live is not a strong case for the claim. It is the case that breaks it.

The layered-averaging works are the cleanest test bed precisely because their constraint is so legible. The 1/N rule is explicitly authored, traceable through its execution, and dignity-preserving by construction (no source crowned), and the coherence one feels looking at the composite tracks that legibility closely. The falsifying case is specifiable in the same breath: a composite whose source-stock was harvested without consent. The present-body works do not fall there, because *Twelve Refractions* rests on the session's research-consent, an offer taken up rather than a quiet harvest. Not immunity from failure: a precise account of where it would fail.

What the felt-beauty claim still lacks, and what a practice-led study can supply without overreaching, is a small empirical handle: an instrument light enough to run in a live room without disturbing it. The instrument is a short **legibility probe** of four prompts, put to sitters after a session and to viewers at the wall, and logged beside the Presence Ledger rows so the beauty claim answers to evidence the way the consent claims already do.

> ### The felt-beauty legibility probe — instrument
>
> Four prompts, no more. Each maps to one term of the felt-beauty claim.
>
> 1. **Legibility.** *What constraint did you read in the work?* — whether a rule was legible at all, and which one.
> 2. **Enabling vs restrictive.** *Did the constraint feel enabling or restrictive?* — The felt-beauty claim predicts that felt beauty tracks the *enabling* reading.
> 3. **Dignity (the defeater).** *Did dignity seem preserved — yours, the sitter's, the figure's?* — beauty reported while dignity is read as eroded is the falsifying case, not the confirming one.
> 4. **The strain.** *Where did the work strain?* — the maculate question, naming the place it did not hold.
>
> *Recorded as one row per respondent: a read constraint, an enabling/restrictive rating, a dignity reading per scope, a named strain — filed against the work or the session.*

The probe runs in two passes, and one of them can run now. The **viewer-side pass** needs only the existing works: the three layered-averaging pieces are finished and hangable, so the four prompts can be put to a small set of viewers at the wall (or before documented images) ahead of the live workshop, giving the felt-beauty claim its first contact with data. The **sitter-side pass** waits for the workshop and for the University's documentation policy ([§7](#governance-and-research-ethics)), and stays declined until both are in place. Reported honestly, then: the viewer pass is a pilot, small-n, on the wall works alone. It can support or strain the felt-beauty claim. It cannot settle it.

Four prompts will not prove the felt-beauty claim, and are not meant to. Until the viewer pilot has run, the felt-beauty claim stays bounded, falsifiable, and (the honest word) mostly asserted. The probe is how it begins to stop being only that — and the paper stakes itself on the bet that, run, the readings will lean the way the felt-beauty claim predicts.

## 7. Governance and research ethics

The study is governed at tier T3 (publication / exhibition), which is the methodology's heaviest tier: a full provenance ledger with a validation method on every external claim, a read-aloud BODY check, at least two Gold-tier sources, typed co-authorship, and a mandatory limitations pass ([§8](#limitations-and-the-and-yet)). The consent, anonymisation, and exit provisions of §3.3 are the research ethics, not separate from them.

One dependency is open and is recorded as open. The University of Pretoria's explicit policy on posing, nudity, and the documentation of sessions has not been confirmed (it is being established through the department; see the internal briefing). Until it is, the run is a workshop, not research data, and the Presence Ledger's research-consent rows stay declined. This is not a hedge; it is the *if-not* kept honest. A provenance fallback is specified for the same reason: if live ledger discipline breaks in a busy room, the trace is reconstructed afterward as a dated, marked retrospective annotation rather than left as a silent gap.

**Host ≠ Author, and co-authorship shown not declared.** The hosted frameworks keep their attribution. And the typed co-authorship the methodology asks for is pointed at concrete preserved agency, not waved at: the sitters co-author the constraint through the veto; the curator ([Sue Clark](https://www.sueclark.co.za/copy-of-object-2024), Equilibrium Studio) authored the occasion and its frame; the vocalist (Ailyn Nienaber) carries the cycle whose reading the visual work sits beside; the recruitment and the policy question run through a named UP colleague (Zita Oranje); and theMachine(s) co-composed this text, named in the colophon rather than hidden in it. None of these collaborators is claimed as having endorsed the argument; each is credited for the agency they in fact exercised. The rights-held libretto and setting are paraphrased only, never reproduced.

## 8. Limitations, and the And-Yet

This is a single room, and it makes no industrial claim. Its external validity is the validity of a well-documented case, not a population.

The case is deliberately narrow, and that is a choice this version makes on purpose. An earlier draft reached much wider, reading the same grammar across software architecture, pedagogy, and the developer's daily condition. This version sets that breadth aside to put the paper's whole weight on one claim, tested in one place. What is lost is reach. What is bought is a paper an examiner can hold to account on the question it actually stakes, with a formal core small enough to check rather than a vocabulary to take on faith.

The facilitator's authored power is real and is not dissolved by naming it. MacKinnon's objection survives the apparatus, and should: consent under asymmetry can be hollow, and the most a built room can do is keep the asymmetry visible and the exit live. The felt-beauty claim is posited, not proven; correlational, not causal; bounded, not total. The legibility probe specified in [§6](#the-felt-beauty-thread) is the modest first instrument for testing it, no more, and the claim stays mostly asserted until the viewer pilot has run. The universal claim sits at the other tier: it is unfalsifiable by design ([§2](#theoretical-framework-objectification-constraint-and-the-dignity-gate)), which is a statement of its type rather than a hole in it, and the two-tier cut is what keeps that immunity from leaking into the scoped claims around it. And the worked ledger is illustrative until the real run, so the strongest empirical claims here are claims about a designed practice and its lineage (the layered-averaging method runs back to the operator's 2012 debut), not yet about a single dated afternoon. A lineage is a record of which decisions were survived and kept, fourteen years of them, and an honest record shows the strains as plainly as the gains.

There is one limit the emergent ethic cannot cross, and §8 should avow it rather than paper over it. The 1/N protects dignity among the parties already inside the coupling. It has nothing to say to the one who refuses the frame entirely (who will not enter the room, or who rejects multipartiality as such), and an emergent ethic, honestly described, *can* have nothing to say there: that silence is the price of being emergent rather than absolute. What the room can do is keep the Empty Turn open both inward and outward: a welcome for the *no* that arrives from outside the frame, an acknowledgement folded into the architecture that the architecture does not reach everyone it concerns. This does not solve the externality of refusal. It avows it. Not solved: avowed. The door stands open in both directions, and some who are owed a place will decline the room.

Two kinds of unknown sit under that avowal, and they are distinct in kind and not in degree. The *unknown unknown* is epistemic and contingent: not known now, knowable later, a humility about the present state of knowledge. The *withdrawn unknown* is ontological and permanent: the recession of every object from all relation, never exhaustible by anyone, a humility about knowability as such. An Empty Turn that bows to both bows to two floors at once. And the loop closes here, because the withdrawn unknown is the remainder the paper has been circling all along: dignity as being seen *with* remainder, the surplus that neither under-seeing nor over-seeing may capture. The discipline is to hold OOO as a regulative posture and not a metaphysical purchase (interested with, but not claiming, it): treat every party as harbouring a remainder you cannot exhaust, without buying Harman's full system.

The honesty the show's own materials insist on belongs here too, and it should be stated plainly rather than held lightly. A man averaging thirty-five historical images of Eve, theorising consent and the gaze, is working inside the very arrangement feminist visual-culture scholarship has taught us to distrust. Amanda du Preez's account of the gendered body in visual culture, and of how the West has learned to picture *woman* (2009a; 2009b), names the trouble exactly: Eve arrives already a category, a representation of representations, so to composite her is to operate on that inheritance rather than to recover a "who" beneath it. Rory du Plessis's (2015) work on the photographic archive and the institutional gaze sharpens it further, since to average an archive is to repeat, at one remove, the archive's own way of looking. The 1/N does real work against this (thirty-five voices at equal volume, no single picturing crowned), and it does not dissolve the asymmetry MacKinnon names: under structural inequality the consent of the depicted, most of them long dead and none of them asked, cannot be invoked at all.

So the paper commits rather than hedging. The layered-averaging works are honest about being representations of representations, and they make no claim to have given Eve back a voice. What they offer is a refusal of the single fixed image, held by a man who says where he stands and does not pretend the structure absolves him. The composite Eve withdraws, and her withdrawal is perhaps also her unfurling, and it is still his looking that arranged the room. Both are true, and the paper does not resolve them into one. *En tog.*

## 9. Conclusion — the page keeps the trace

Parametric authorship relocates agency to sensitivity design under contract, and in doing so it lets one lens read a life-drawing room, an ethics of consent, and three composite pictures as one practice, and ask of all three whether beauty tracks a legible, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint. Meaning lives in the map, not the single mark. The room, at its best, is a shelter: less a purification of the gaze than a structure that holds the vectors of integrity (dignity, consent, authorship, risk, attention) apart and intact, in tension, without flattening any of them. The composite Eve withdraws, and her withdrawal is perhaps her unfurling. The practice declines both the pedestal and the gutter, and reaches for presence instead.

The mark is meat-noise. The mapping is world-creating. The work is to keep authoring the rule that holds the two at equal weight, no source crowned, the exit live, and the door open. *En tog.* I invite you to look closely, and to dwell.
