## Abstract

> **Plain-English (≈120 words).** This paper takes one afternoon in a life-drawing room, where people pose *as objects* and a small group draws them, and reads it as a piece of research. The lens is *parametric authorship*: the idea that an artist authors **rules** that produce **families** of things, and that the meaning lives in the mapping from rule to family, not in any single mark. I show that the same idea reads a workshop, an ethics of consent, a piece of software architecture, and three composite pictures on a gallery wall, all at once. The thread that ties them is one small decision: hold every source at equal weight (1/N). That decision is also the ethics. A purpose-built *presence ledger* records whether the room kept its promises.

**Abstract (academic).** This practice-led paper argues that Parametric Authorship (PA) operates as *transdisciplinary connective tissue*: a single lens under which a consent-based life-drawing event becomes simultaneously legible as studio practice, analytic ethics and aesthetics, software-architecture governance, computational generative art, pedagogy, and semiotics. The case is the *Objectification Workshop* (designed for the Eve Song happening, University of Pretoria, 2026), read together with three layered-averaging works exhibited in the same show. PA's formal apparatus is cited from a companion doctoral thesis and not re-derived; the paper carries only the Parametric Author Function tuple and the dignity-conservation condition it needs. The argument is organised around the equivalence of a formal decision and an ethical one — equal weighting (1/N) as dignity conservation — and it operationalises a posited claim (A3★) that the felt beauty of an authored artefact tracks, defeasibly, the legibility of a coherent, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint. A bespoke *presence ledger* is offered as the data instrument that turns the room into auditable evidence. Governance follows METHODOLOGY_CII at tier T3.

**Keywords:** parametric authorship; objectification; consent; dignity; life drawing; layered averaging; object-oriented ontology; practice-led research; semiotics; South African contemporary art.

> **A small glossary, for readers arriving from different disciplines.**
> **Parametric authorship (PA)** — authoring the *rules* that produce a *family* of works, rather than inscribing one finished work. **PAF** — the Parametric Author Function, the authored tuple `⟨S, 𝒫, C, E, Φ⟩`: Substrates, Parameter space, Constraints, Environment, and a Shaping functional (how much your settings actually matter). **A1, Map-over-Mark** — meaning is borne by the mapping from rules to family, not by any single mark. **Hyperstrate** — the cross-cutting layer of tools, defaults, policies, and logistics that any authoring runs *on*. **Dignity tensor** — dignity scored across three scopes (personal, object, system); the conservation condition is that their product stays above zero and none drops below a declared floor. **Auth★** — an agency metric (named here, derived in the doctoral thesis). **Maculate** — marked, flawed, worked-with rather than purified. **The family** — the set of works a constraint yields; the unit of the practice. **As-if / if-not / and-yet** — engage provisionally; audit where it strains; hold the contradiction. **Empty Turn** — the sovereign right to stop, without penalty.

## 1. Introduction — From the unnamed gaze to the designed constraint

To draw from life is to look hard at a person and make of them, for a while, an object of attention. The life room has long had good reasons to leave that mostly unspoken: a working focus to keep, a courtesy to the sitter, a craft whose conventions were earned honestly and still serve. This paper does not arrive to correct the tradition, nor to claim that naming is the better way. It begins where a more pointed companion essay leaves off (Clements, *Objectively Objectifying Objectification*) by making one dynamic explicit rather than ambient (and only the one the inquiry turns on), then asking what is left of authorship once the looking is named. What it offers is not a confession, and not a charge laid against the convention: it is a method, set beside the conventional one rather than over it.

The posture throughout is mediating rather than adjudicating, in a conflict-resilience register. Several lenses meet in this one room (the craft tradition of the life class, the critical and feminist reading of the gaze, object-oriented ontology, the computational), and the aim is to let them hold one another in tension rather than to crown any of them. Where the paper does press, it presses on a specific, clearly articulated dynamic (whether the exit stays live, whether the consent can be audited), not on the conventional practice as such, which keeps its merit and often keeps its care by means of its own. In that spirit, what follows is offered as a lens: polished, and pointed here at one room. Where a reader points it next, and what they see through it, stays theirs. The paper holds no attachment to the view.

I was invited to exhibit. I have decided to treat that invitation *as-if* it were a masters presentation: provisionally, instrumentally, and with a straight face about the rigour even while keeping the tone light. The stance is borrowed wholesale from the repository's engagement methodology (METHODOLOGY_CII §1): engage *as-if*, audit *if-not*, hold the *and-yet*. *If-not* a masters, then still: work made that I can stand behind, shown well. *And yet*: the theory of language at the foot of the operator's own README applies here, and fans out. Language is a magical, world-creating force; language is also just the noise made by a piece of meat in a mouth. The two readings do not resolve. A held pose is the same shape of problem. A body worked *as-if* a chair is world-creating (it makes a study, a family, a small room of meaning) and it is also just a body, meat under a fall of light. Meaning fans out across the gap between those readings and never collapses to either. That is the engine of everything below.

The paper studies one practice at two scales. At the scale of a body: the **Objectification Workshop**, in which sitters pose as objects (a chair, a doorway, a tree) under actively held consent, and a small room draws them. At the scale of a wall: three composite works in the same exhibition (*Shroud of Eve*, *Study of Eve*, *Twelve Refractions*), each made by holding many source images at equal weight until a figure emerges that none of them alone contains. The wager of the paper is that the two are one practice read at two scales, and that parametric authorship is the name of their shared grammar.

**Research questions.**

1. **(RQ1)** What is authorship when the work is a *family* (of drawings, of composites) produced under a chosen constraint, rather than a single inscribed mark?
2. **(RQ2)** Under what room conditions does deliberate objectification stay *benign* in Nussbaum's (1995) sense, and how does a governance architecture make those conditions legible and auditable rather than merely asserted?
3. **(RQ3)** Does the felt beauty of such an artefact track the legibility of a coherent, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint (the posited claim A3★), and what would falsify that?

A note on register, because the paper will keep two of them in one room. It is academic where it reasons and hedges; it is plainer, and occasionally bodily, where it does not. This is deliberate, and it is methodological: the operator's voice signature treats multipartiality (standing with more than one party at once, without collapsing the difference) as an ethical stance, not a stylistic flourish. A paper about holding many readings at equal weight had better hold its own registers that way too.

## 2. Theoretical framework — three bodies of thought, one connective tissue

### 2.1 Parametric authorship, by reference

The formal apparatus of parametric authorship is set out in a companion doctoral thesis (Clements, *The Fourth Dimension as Execution*) and is not re-derived here. This paper carries only what the case needs.

Authorship, in PA, is the design of control surfaces: you articulate a rule-set, set ranges, fix what must hold, and let a generator instantiate a family. The authored object is a tuple, the **Parametric Author Function**:

`PAF := ⟨S, 𝒫, C, E, Φ⟩`

Substrates `S` (tools, materials, models); a parameter space `𝒫` (the knobs, and their ranges); constraints `C` (what will not be done); environment `E` (budgets, governance, the weather you do not control); and a shaping functional `Φ` (how sensitively the work responds when you move a parameter). The thesis develops an agency metric, **Auth★**, and a cybernetic inversion (DIOW) by which tools must conform to a declared interface rather than the author adapting to the tool; I name these and point to them, no more.

The one axiom this paper leans its full weight on is **A1, Map-over-Mark**: *meaning is borne by stable features of the mapping from parameters to artefacts, not by any single token.* And the one formal condition it needs is dignity conservation, **A3**: dignity is scored across three scopes (personal, object, system), and is conserved only while their product stays positive and none falls below a declared floor:

`conserve(D) := (∏ᵢ dᵢ > 0) ∧ (minᵢ dᵢ ≥ η)`

The product is the load-bearing part. If any one dignity goes to zero, the whole goes to zero. There is no averaging your way out of a flattened person.

### 2.2 Objectification: the ethics and the aesthetics

The case is objectification taken at its word, so the framework's spine is Martha Nussbaum's (1995; 1999) analysis. Nussbaum's decisive move is to treat objectification as a cluster of analytically distinct features rather than a single thing, several of which, under the right conditions, can be benign rather than harmful. The workshop courts a few of these on purpose (inertness; reduction to body and appearance; fungibility) and works to keep three others firmly out of the room (violability; denial of subjectivity; silencing). Rae Langton (2009) adds three more features to the cluster and sharpens the account of when instrumentality curdles.

The benign reading has a critic, and the paper keeps her in the room rather than answering her too quickly. Catharine MacKinnon's objection (engaged here via the repository's hosted entry, `OBJECTIFICATION.md`, attribution hers) is that under conditions of structural inequality, "consent" can be hollow from the start. This is not a footnote to be discharged. It is the live *if-not* that the governance architecture in §4.3 exists to audit.

The aesthetic counter-weight comes from object-oriented ontology. Graham Harman (2011) holds that every object, human and not, keeps a withdrawn interior that exceeds any relation to it. Read through OOO, the deepest claim of the workshop inverts: to pose as a chair, taken seriously, does not degrade a person into a thing. It asks the room to grant the pose the withdrawn interior a real chair is already owed. Objectification, on this reading, is not the making of an object; it is the *denial* of the dignity an object already has. The same withdrawal runs through the exhibition works, where the composite Eve, in the operator's own phrase, withdraws: and her withdrawal is perhaps also her unfurling.

### 2.3 The creativity of constraints

That a limit can be generative rather than merely restrictive is an old and well-attested idea, and the paper draws on its lineage rather than claiming it: Stravinsky's *Poetics of Music* (1942/47) on the freedom found inside self-imposed bounds; Boden's (1990/2004) account of constraint as the condition of creative search; Stokes (2005) on creativity *from* constraints; Elster (1999) on binding oneself to be more free; Kant (1790) on purposive form; and the experimental support gathered by Haught-Tromp (2017). The operator's voice has a name for this: the **Constraint Gift**. A prompt that closes the easy move ("do not draw a portrait, draw a doorway") gathers the attention and presses the hand toward the essential line. The limit reads as the thing that lets a family of marks cohere.

### 2.4 Risk, vulnerability, and the dignity gate

Marc Botha's "Risk" (2021) does the last piece of framing work, and it is the piece that keeps the aesthetics honest. Botha distinguishes risk *taken* from risk *imposed*: "when you externalise risk onto a vulnerable body, it becomes harm." That restates the benign/malignant line in the language of risk. Benign where the sitter takes the risk and holds it, exit live; malignant where the risk is laid on her. Botha's further claim, that agency itself requires vulnerability, meets the workshop's central paradox directly: choosing to bind oneself into an object-pose is an act of agency, a taking-on, not a giving-up. This is the threshold on which the whole study balances, and it sets up the defeater in §6.

Throughout, the framework is held in the maculate register METHODOLOGY_CII insists on: all of these systems are flawed, and the practice navigates the flaws rather than purifying them. And throughout, **Host ≠ Author**. Nussbaum's, Langton's, Harman's, and Botha's frameworks are hosted here as working dependencies, attribution theirs. The transpositions, and the posited claim A3★, are the author's, marked as his.

## 3. Method — practice-led, the workshop as instrument

### 3.1 The practice-led stance

This is not a study *about* a practice. The workshop *is* the inquiry, and the drawings, the vetoes, the dropped poses, and the debrief are its data. The methodology's risk heuristic applies: `risk_index = complexity × power × distance_from_wetware` (METHODOLOGY_CII §1). A hands-on drawing room is low on distance-from-wetware (everything happens in bodies, in a room, in person), which raises the evidential weight of what is felt there and lowers the opacity that haunts screen-mediated work. It also concentrates the power asymmetry in one place: the facilitator, who authored the frame and invited the risk, holds more of the room than he likes to admit. The method does not dissolve that asymmetry. It makes it visible and watches it.

### 3.2 Site and constraint design

The site is the UP 2-1 Gallery (University of Pretoria), within the run of the Eve Song happening (exhibition 20 July – 14 August 2026; workshops 27 July – 13 August). Each object-prompt is a small PAF: the substrate is charcoal and A2 paper and a body; the parameter space is the prompt and its agile variants; the constraints are the dignity floor and the kept-out features of §2.2; the environment is the gallery, the timing, the policy; and the shaping functional is the sensitivity of the family of drawings to the choice of pose. The pose runs about fifteen minutes and is drawn **four figures to a page**: the page is a small matrix of the session, not a precious sheet. The full constraint vocabulary is versioned in the prompt pool (`prompt-pool.md`).

### 3.3 The consent and safety architecture as method

The architecture is not pastoral add-on; it is the experimental apparatus, because it is what holds the benign conditions in place long enough to observe them.

- The prompt pool is **co-authored and vetoed by the sitters before the room ever sees it.** In PA terms this is A4 (Defaults Author) handled correctly: a prompt pool is a silent co-author of the family it yields; the veto discloses and bounds it, and in doing so makes the sitter a real co-author of the constraint.
- A **stop/rest signal**, agreed and honoured at any moment, no reason owed. This is the Empty Turn (METHODOLOGY_CII §9.3) given a body: the sovereign right to stop, which is "not a failure but a dignified exit."
- The right to come **off the stand mid-pose**, and **opting out never touches the fee.** Financial safety belongs to exit-liveness; an exit you cannot afford is not an exit.
- **Shared, casual breaks and a shared debrief**, because vulnerability is distributed, not assigned: to look, and to be seen looking, implicates the artist too.
- **Documentation only by specific consent, anonymised by default**, and (see §7) no research use of any session until the University's posing/documentation policy is confirmed.

### 3.4 The instrument: the Presence Ledger

A study needs an instrument that turns events into evidence. Borrowed from the doctoral thesis's governance stack, the instrument here is a **provenance ledger** specialised for a live room: a **Presence Ledger** (`templates/presence_ledger.md`; instance at `presence-ledger.md`). Where a provenance ledger records claims and assets, a presence ledger records *presence and consent events*: a vetoed prompt, a rest called, a pose dropped with pay unaffected, a photo-consent granted and scoped, a dignity-flag raised and resolved, an Empty Turn. Each row names the axiom or CARDS axis it discharges, so the sheet *shows* the ethic operating rather than asserting it. This is the indexical trace of A6: in parametric authorship the "stroke" is the recorded decision, and consent, recorded, is a kind of mark.

The worked example in Appendix C is illustrative until the real run. It is shown here, before the findings, on purpose: a method instrument has to be seen working before it can be trusted to carry an argument. (Fiertek's 2013 *Performance Guide to Eve-Song*, a document that is also its own research form, is the structural model for treating an operational artefact as a scholarly one.)

## 4. Case analysis — reading the room, and the wall, in PA

### 4.1 The pose as rule-manifold (semiotics)

Saussure (1916) and Peirce gave us a sign that points: a vehicle, a referent, an interpretant. Parametric authorship extends the triangle to generated families. The prompt is the **rule manifold** (`𝒫`); the family of drawings is the **generated vehicle family** (`𝒜`); the room's reading is the **interpretant field**. Meaning, in this frame, is what stays stable when the parameters are nudged (invariants) and what changes predictably when they are shocked on purpose (designed transitions). Style is the curvature: the way the family bends as the prompt moves.

The chair-pose is the cleanest instance, because its meaning is *designed undecidability*. You cannot tell, from outside, whether a body folded into a chair has been diminished or honoured. The cycle that occasions the show says it more economically than any theory: to be visible is at once to be warned and to be invited, with no way to know which. That ambiguity is not noise in the sign. It is the sign. A1 holds: the meaning is in the mapping (this prompt, held by this room, under this consent), not in any one drawing of it.

### 4.2 The layered-averaging works: parametric authorship at exhibition scale

The three composite works in the show are the same move at the scale of a wall, and reading them is where the paper's central claim earns its keep. I anchor it in the material decisions, not in resemblance.

**Layered averaging is A1 made literal.** Thirty-five historical paintings of Eve (Dürer, Cranach, Masaccio, Michelangelo, Rubens, Valadon, and more) are aligned and averaged at equal weight, "yielding a figure that none contains alone." The rule-set plus the weighting is the rule manifold. The composite is the family. The meaning is in the mapping. No single painting is the sign. The sum of how she has been pictured is the sign.

**The 1/N is the dignity move, and this is the equivalence the whole paper turns on.** The decision to hold every source at equal one-thirty-fifth weight is, in the operator's own words from the work's statement, "the least technical and the most load-bearing decision in the whole pipeline: equal weight, no Eve crowned over the others, a quorum rather than a queue." Read formally, 1/N is a weighting. Read ethically, it is dignity conservation across the scope of all the sources: none flattened to zero, none crowned. The formal decision and the ethical decision are the *same decision*. This is multipartiality (the operator's term for standing with more than one party at once without collapsing the difference) written as arithmetic. And it is the same ethic as the workshop's refusal to curate for the one best drawing: no precious sheet, no crowned source. The body-scale practice and the wall-scale practice share one commitment, and it is a number.

**Shroud and Study are a designed transition.** The two works run the *same* thirty-five-source stock through *one* changed parameter: the alignment axis. *Study of Eve* aligns on the body, and the canonical standing nude turns out to have been a composite all along. *Shroud of Eve* aligns on each source's narrative placement, leaving every Eve where her own picture put her, so the figure recedes under the architecture of her iconography. One generator, one stock, one knob turned: invariants under the move (it is recognisably Eve, recognisably a composite) and a designed transition across it (presence versus recession). This is exactly the perturbation logic the doctoral thesis specifies, realised on a wall.

**The trajectory of inversion is the curvature of `𝒫`.** Across the three works runs an inversion the artist names plainly: the most abstract-seeming work (*Shroud*, the figure all but dissolved) is empirically the most concrete (thirty-five paintings held exactly as they are), and the most concrete source (*Twelve Refractions*, one real Saturday, one named sitter) yields the most abstract image. The apparent abstraction holds the most empirical; the apparent concrete holds the most abstract. That is style as curvature of the parameter space, made visible across a family of three.

***Twelve Refractions* is the bridge.** It takes twenty-four photographs from a single consent-based life-drawing session and processes them twelve ways, held at equal 1/12 weight in a single √2 frame with a 2 cm margin of untouched paper. "Refraction, not fragmentation": one light through twelve lenses, recombined. It is the workshop's own kind of room, run through the averaging pipeline. Hand-accumulation (the four-figure page) and computational averaging (the composite) turn out to be sibling outputs of a common stock. The forest, as the statement has it, extends. General life drawing, this workshop, and the averaging matrices are one practice.

**Body as interface.** The works and the workshop share a model of the body the repository has elsewhere: skin contains, skin reads, skin displays, skin mediates, at once firewall and open port. To draw a body well is to negotiate that boundary. To average one is the same negotiation by other means.

**The anti-idol payoff.** All of this is, finally, an argument about how to honour a figure without fixing her. To idolise Eve and to blame Eve are one gesture in two coats: both swap a woman for a single picture of one. The layered-averaging works refuse the single image as such. Eve as simultaneously-many is the anti-idol, and it rhymes exactly with the workshop's move at the scale of one body: refuse the one fixed reading, hold the ambiguity, reach for presence instead.

And the algorithm, to be exact about agency, does not author any of this. In the artist's own formulation: "the algorithm does not author the image; the image is afforded by it." The choosing is authored (the source-stock, the alignment, the curation, the 1/N); the press is automatic. This is why the practice is *composed*, not *generated*: the word marks where the agency sits.

### 4.3 Governance as architecture (software engineering)

The fourth discipline the lens crosses is software architecture, and it is not a metaphor; it is the same SOLID grammar the doctoral thesis runs on. The room instantiates a **Sovereign Interface** of four parts. The consent script is the **Header** (it discloses the defaults: the conceit, the veto, the signal). The Presence Ledger is the **Ledger** (the immutable, signed trace). The stop/rest signal and the Empty Turn are the **Circuit Breaker** (a hard refusal path, a first-class control action). The facilitator's watch, pose by pose, is the **Dignity Linter** (the run-time check that the kept-out features stay out).

Two SOLID moves do real work here. Interface Segregation: consent is many small, purpose-built interfaces (veto a prompt, drop a pose, decline a photo), no single fat agreement that bundles unrelated permissions. And Dependency Inversion, in its dignity form: the room's high-level policy does not depend on the particular bodies or prompts in it; the prompts conform to the policy through an adapter. The prompt-pool veto *is* that adapter (the conformance map κ): a loaded prompt is either made conformant (changed, re-scoped) or blocked. No adapter, no engagement. A prompt that cannot pass the dignity interface does not enter the room.

### 4.4 Benign objectification, tested

Walk Nussbaum's cluster against the actual poses and the account stays honest. Inertness, reduction to appearance, and fungibility are present and courted: a body held still, read as form and weight, one "chair" formally like another. Violability, denial of subjectivity, and silencing are the three the architecture exists to exclude, and the exclusion is enacted as a set of mechanisms rather than asserted as a claim (the veto, the signal, the shared debrief, the fee that opting out never touches). The benign/malignant line runs straight through those three, and the hinge, every time, is who holds the exit.

Here the *if-not* stays live rather than being answered. MacKinnon's objection is not refuted by the apparatus; it is *watched* by it. A facilitator who authored the frame and invited the risk holds more of the room than consent-talk likes to admit. The dignity the exercise depends on is not a switch thrown at the start; it is built and watched, pose by pose, and it can fail at any one of them. And there is a real difference here, held without triumph: where the objectification is named, it can be argued with, refused, renegotiated mid-pose. Where it stays ambient, those moves have less to fasten to. That is not a verdict on the conventional life room, which manages its care by other means and often manages it well; it is only an account of what the naming buys in this particular room. The paper does not close the gap between the two. For this inquiry, it builds a room in which the gap stays visible.

## 5. Findings / propositions

The findings are tagged to the research questions and, where the evidence is concrete, to the rows of the Presence Ledger (Appendix C).

- **F1 (RQ1).** The bearer of meaning is the mapping, not the single sheet or source: the four-figure page and the thirty-five-source composite are both *families*, and removing any one member changes the family without unmaking it. Authorship is sensitivity-shaping under contract (A1).
- **F2 (RQ2).** Benign objectification is sustained by exit-liveness operating as a *live state machine*, not by a declaration made once at the start. In the worked ledger, a dropped pose, a rest called, and an Empty Turn are all penalty-free; the benign condition holds because the exit kept evaluating true (A5).
- **F3 (RQ1, RQ2).** The pre-room veto makes the sitter a co-author of the constraint: in the worked example it moves the pool from v1.0 to v1.1, and the family the room then draws is the *post-veto* family. Co-authorship here is typed and real, not ceremonial (A4 inverted).
- **F4 (RQ2).** Vulnerability is distributed, not assigned: the shared breaks and shared debrief, and the fact that looking implicates the looker, are structural refusals of the model-as-victim / artist-as-rescuer flattening (CARDS Relatedness; the Karpman caution).
- **F5 (RQ2).** The stack is auditable: the Presence Ledger renders consent, refusal, and dignity-strain as inspectable rows, which is what lets a practice claim T3-grade evidence rather than anecdote (A6).
- **F6 (RQ3).** A3★ gains defeasible support *and* names its own falsifier (§6).
- **F7 (RQ1).** The workshop (body scale) and the three works (wall scale) are one parametric practice: the 1/N composite and the four-figure page are sibling outputs of one stock. And the move that demonstrates this (weaving disparate works into one continuous territory) is itself a function of the operator's voice signature: Landscapify, multipartiality, the distributed signature. The reading is the method performing itself.

## 6. The A3★ beauty thread

The paper's most exposed claim is the operator's own, posited as a research thread and stated here verbatim:

> *Within authored/designed artefacts, the sensation of beauty correlates with (tracks, defeasibly) the legibility of a coherent, generative constraint: one explicitly authored or freely chosen, traceable through its execution, dignity-preserving, and read by the perceiver as enabling rather than merely restrictive. For any constraint realised through a person, "freely chosen" means chosen by the bound person and continuously revocable; the posed figure is in scope only while consent, rough equality, and live exit hold. The dignity gate is the defeater: coherence felt as beautiful while dignity erodes falsifies the claim, it does not satisfy it.* (A3★)

Three things keep this from inflating into a theory of everything beautiful. First, "tracks" is correlational, not an identity: the claim says beauty *covaries with* legible, dignity-preserving constraint, not that it *is* it. Second, it is bounded, and the boundary is stated: it leaves out the sublime and the wild, formless generosity (sunsets, the sea, which nobody authored). Botha's aesthetics of risk sits on the far side of that line, and the object-pose stands exactly on the threshold between the two. Third, and most important, the claim carries a defeater rather than only confirmations: coherence felt as beautiful *while dignity erodes* does not satisfy A3★, it falsifies it. A beautiful run made while a sitter's exit was not in fact live is not a strong case for the claim; it is the case that breaks it.

The layered-averaging works are the cleanest test bed precisely because their constraint is so legible. The 1/N rule is explicitly authored, traceable through its execution, and dignity-preserving by construction (no source crowned), and the coherence one feels looking at the composite tracks that legibility closely. The falsifying case is specifiable in the same breath: a composite whose source-stock was harvested without consent. The present-body works do not fall there, because *Twelve Refractions* rests on the session's research-consent, an offer taken up rather than a quiet harvest. The gate is doing its work, which is the most a defeasible claim can honestly offer. Not immunity from failure: a precise account of where it would fail.

## 7. Governance and research ethics

The study is governed at tier T3 (publication / exhibition), which is the methodology's heaviest tier: a full provenance ledger with a validation method on every external claim, a read-aloud BODY check, at least two Gold-tier sources, typed co-authorship, and a mandatory limitations pass (§8). The consent, anonymisation, and exit provisions of §3.3 are the research ethics, not separate from them.

One dependency is open and is recorded as open. The University of Pretoria's explicit policy on posing, nudity, and the documentation of sessions has not been confirmed (it is being established through the department; see the internal briefing). Until it is, the run is a workshop, not research data, and the Presence Ledger's research-consent rows stay declined. This is not a hedge; it is the *if-not* kept honest. A provenance fallback is specified for the same reason: if live ledger discipline breaks in a busy room, the trace is reconstructed afterward as a dated, marked retrospective annotation rather than left as a silent gap.

**Host ≠ Author, and co-authorship shown not declared.** The hosted frameworks keep their attribution. And the typed co-authorship the methodology asks for is pointed at concrete preserved agency, not waved at: the sitters co-author the constraint through the veto; the curator (Sue Clark) authored the occasion and its frame; the vocalist (Ailyn Nienaber) carries the cycle whose reading the visual work sits beside; the recruitment and the policy question run through a named UP colleague (Zita Oranje); and theMachine(s) co-composed this text, named in the colophon rather than hidden in it. None of these collaborators is claimed as having endorsed the argument; each is credited for the agency they in fact exercised. The rights-held libretto and setting are paraphrased only, never reproduced.

## 8. Limitations, and the And-Yet

This is a single room, and it makes no industrial claim. Its external validity is the validity of a well-documented case, not a population.

The facilitator's authored power is real and is not dissolved by naming it. MacKinnon's objection survives the apparatus, and should: consent under asymmetry can be hollow, and the most a built room can do is keep the asymmetry visible and the exit live. A3★ is posited, not proven; correlational, not causal; bounded, not total. And the worked ledger is illustrative until the real run, so the strongest empirical claims here are claims about a designed practice and its lineage (the layered-averaging method runs back to the operator's 2012 debut), not yet about a single dated afternoon.

The honesty the show's own materials insist on belongs here too, unresolved. A man averaging images of Eve is not a woman singing them. The formal structure (thirty-five voices at equal volume, a forest in which no tree dominates the canopy) permits a feminist-informed reading; it does not claim one. And yet the structure is what it is. The composite is honest about being a representation of representations: there is no recovered "who"; Eve arrives already a category, a way the West learned to picture a woman. A man does what he can with his looking, and says so.

And the last and least comfortable turn, made in the open because the methodology requires it: this paper, a meditation on how knowledge gets objectified, was itself composed with the multi-agent machines now unsettling the academy whose forms it borrows. The gaze that objectifies a body and the system that objectifies knowledge are not distant cousins; they are the same move at different scales, and the paper runs both at once. The shelter it describes has to be large enough to hold the sitter and the scholar, the drawn body and the automated thought, and to admit, at the foot of the page, that one of the hands that built it is also among the things it is trying to protect. Maculate, then: navigation, not purification.

## 9. Conclusion — the page keeps the trace

Parametric authorship relocates agency to sensitivity design under contract, and in doing so it lets one lens read a life-drawing room, an ethics of consent, a piece of software architecture, and three composite pictures as one practice. Meaning lives in the map, not the single mark. The room, at its best, is a shelter: less a purification of the gaze than a structure that holds the vectors of integrity (dignity, consent, authorship, risk, attention) apart and intact, in tension, without flattening any of them. The composite Eve withdraws, and her withdrawal is perhaps her unfurling. The practice declines both the pedestal and the gutter, and reaches for presence instead.

The mark is meat-noise. The mapping is world-creating. The work is to keep authoring the rule that holds the two at equal weight, no source crowned, the exit live, and the door open. *En tog.* I invite you to look closely, and to dwell.
