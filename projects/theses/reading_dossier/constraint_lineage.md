## Core argument

Across five works the lineage argues that a chosen limit is generative rather than merely restrictive: Stravinsky claims his freedom is greatest within the narrowest field of self-imposed obstacles; Boden formalises this as creativity that explores and transforms a defined conceptual space; Stokes shows artists (Picasso, Mondrian, Chanel) using precise constraints to preclude reliable options and force search toward novelty; Elster generalises precommitment and binding into a proposed "constraint theory"; and Haught-Tromp's two experiments give the empirical anchor, finding that requiring a given noun in a short rhyme raised rated creativity, with a carryover effect from mere practice with constraints. The shared thesis: limits narrow the option-space in ways that paradoxically widen what becomes findable.

## What to internalise — in your voice

This is the bedrock under A3★: felt beauty tracks a legible, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint. The lineage gives me each clause. Stravinsky supplies "freely-chosen" — the obstacle is invented, then honoured. Boden supplies "legible" — the constraint is a conceptual space you can see the edges of. Haught-Tromp supplies the only hard floor I have: rated creativity actually rises under the given-noun rule, and rises again with practice. So the Constraint Gift is less a romance, more a measurable lift.

And yet the lineage stops short of where my work needs it to go. None of them carry the dignity clause. Stokes precludes options to force search; Elster binds the self against the self. Both treat the constraint instrumentally — it serves the maker. My 1/N hinge does something they do not: it makes the constraint serve the sources. Equal weight, no image crowned. The limit is not chosen to sharpen my output but to refuse my dominion over thirty-five Eves.

That gap is the thing to hold. A man averaging 35 historical images of Eve, theorising consent: the literature would call my constraint generative. It is. En tog the generativity is not the point. The constraint is a dignity gate first, a creative engine second — and I should not let the lift seduce me into forgetting which comes first.
