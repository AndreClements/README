# Harman 2011 — *The Quadruple Object*

**Citation.** Harman, G. (2011) *The Quadruple Object.* Winchester: Zero Books (vii + 148 pp.). ISBN 9781846947001. **[Gold]**

**Access.** In copyright — abstract/summary only. Borrowable scan (Internet Archive, controlled lending): <https://archive.org/details/quadrupleobject0000harm>. (Substance carried by the summary + note below.)

## Core argument

Harman argues that every object — equally, a hammer, a nation, a fictional character, a quark — harbours a real interior that withdraws absolutely from all relation, including human perception and the object's own causal contacts. From this withdrawal he derives a fourfold structure (real object, sensual object, real qualities, sensual qualities) and a method he calls "ontography" for mapping the tensions between these poles. The book is his most compressed statement of object-oriented ontology, rejecting both "undermining" (dissolving objects downward into parts or process) and "overmining" (dissolving them upward into their effects or appearances).

## What to internalise — in your voice

The load-bearing claim, for me: every object keeps a withdrawn interior that exceeds any relation to it. Harman makes this universal — not just persons, but Eve, the hammer, the average itself. That generalises the dignity floor past the human, and I want to hold that carefully rather than swallow it whole.

Read this against the gender tension. When I average 35 historical images of Eve, OOO says the woman in each plate never arrives whole into my parametric grasp: she withdraws, and so does the composite. Useful — a metaphysical name for what the dignity gate already enforces phenomenologically: the source is never crowned, never exhausted, never fully had. 1/N as withdrawal-conservation.

And yet I should not let "withdrawal" launder consent. A real object that can never be fully accessed is not the same as a person who can refuse, exit, be wronged. Harman gives me reticence; he does not give me the ethics. The interior that exceeds my reach is a fact about being. The dignity it deserves is a choice I make. A3★: felt beauty tracking a legible, freely-chosen constraint — the constraint here being my own non-mastery, framed less as limit, more as the thing that keeps the composite honest.
