# Nussbaum 1995 — 'Objectification'

**Citation.** Nussbaum, M.C. (1995) 'Objectification', *Philosophy & Public Affairs* 24(4), Autumn 1995, pp. 249–291. DOI: 10.1111/j.1088-4963.1995.tb00032.x. **[Gold]**

**Access.** Open-access PDF (course posting). Local copy: [`sources/nussbaum_1995_objectification.pdf`](sources/nussbaum_1995_objectification.pdf) · source: <https://www.mit.edu/~shaslang/mprg/nussbaumO.pdf>

## Core argument

Nussbaum argues that "objectification" is not one thing but a cluster of seven analytically distinct features — instrumentality, denial of autonomy, inertness, fungibility, violability, ownership, and denial of subjectivity — that can occur together or separately. Against the assumption that objectification is uniformly bad, she contends that context is decisive: several of these features can be benign or even valuable within relationships marked by equality, consent and mutual respect (her example: using a lover's body as a pillow). The moral weight falls less on the act of treating-as-object and more on whether the broader relation continues to recognise the person as more than that object.

## What to internalise — in your voice

Seven features, not one verdict: instrumentality, denial of autonomy, inertness, fungibility, violability, ownership, denial of subjectivity. The gift here is the disaggregation. Objectification stops being a single moral alarm and becomes a terrain with named regions, some passable, some not. That maps almost too cleanly onto what I'm doing with PA: treating a face as parameters is instrumentality and fungibility at once, and the question is not whether I objectify (I do, the method is averaging) but under what surrounding conditions the floor still holds. Nussbaum's wager: context is everything, equality and consent can make a feature benign. My A3★ says felt beauty should track a legible, freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint — and here is the same hinge from the ethics side. The 1/N average is fungibility made literal: thirty-five Eves, equal weight, no source crowned. En tog. The dignity gate is what tells fungibility-as-method apart from fungibility-as-erasure. And I should sit in the discomfort she would name: a man averaging historical images of Eve, theorising the gaze, deciding which features stay benign. The disaggregation does not absolve that. It just refuses to let me pretend the question was already settled.
