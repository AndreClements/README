**Anti-patterns to avoid** (voice §7):
- "not X but Y" framing → additive ("less OR, more AND")
- Em-dashes as primary joint
- Semicolons as primary joint
- Resolving Eve's complexity into tidy synthesis
- Performing expertise on Heggie or opera
- Explaining any Afrikaans
- Spectacularising embodied content

## §3. CARDS Mapping

| CARDS axis | Application |
|---|---|
| **Competence** | Facilitation methodology: life drawing as a durable consent-based practice (2017–). Competence-as-protocol. |
| **Autonomy** | Life-model sovereignty. The model is a co-participant, not a passive object. Opt-out is structural. |
| **Relatedness** | Interdisciplinary happening: music + voice + visual + text in one room. Relation across substrates. |
| **Dignity** | Durational consent. The pose is honoured; the gaze is restrained. "Razor on skin" as ethical incision. |
| **Safety** | Timed poses, consent protocols, opt-out clauses — structural safety, not performative. |

**Diagnostic**: if the statement reduces Eve to symbol or the life-model to object, dignity has failed. Verify by reading aloud and asking: does this sound like someone who listens?
