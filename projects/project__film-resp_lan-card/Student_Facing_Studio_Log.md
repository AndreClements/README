**Welcome to your Studio Log.** This is your workbook for the "Filmic Response" project.

Think of your unique artistic style as your personal **vehicle**. It's what will carry you through your career. Are you taking a short trip, or are you building a powerful engine that can take you anywhere? This log helps you to start designing that engine, piece by piece.

Use this log to keep track of your ideas and choices. It will help you see how your work grows from the start to the finish. Filling this log out carefully is an important part of your final mark, because it shows us your thinking process.
