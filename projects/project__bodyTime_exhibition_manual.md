## **Lead Maintainer’s Note**

This document serves as the formal project manual and forensic aesthetic record for the `bodyTime()` exhibition. It is the first major field test of the **P(a)/PPP (`Prototype(artifact) 'over' Proposals, Pitches, and Prayers`)** axiom, where a fully realized body of work and its accompanying professional apparatus were used as the primary instrument for engaging the `Hyperstrate`.

The project's entire lifecycle—from the intimate, consent-driven studio praxis to the high-stakes curatorial `pings`—is documented here as a case study in applying the `human-OS` under real-world conditions.

---

## **§1. Intent & Abstract**

The project's core `intent` was to translate the embodied, consent-driven studio praxis of a long-running life drawing group into a set of high-integrity, public-facing artifacts. The goal was to create a "small, working model of a more ethical reality" that could be shared, validated, and used to build new, robust `edges` within the art world `Hyperstrate`.

The project yielded a cohesive body of 16 unique photographic works, a digital catalogue, and a series of targeted, protocol-driven engagements with key `nodes` in the network.

---

## **§2. Core Principles Binding This Project**

This project was a direct execution of the principles outlined in `A Pragmatics of Engagement`.

-   **`Maculate Design`:** The work embraces the "flaws" and nuances of real bodies and the soft-focus ambiguity of the double-exposure process. The catalogue itself retains minor design quirks (the single `so)d` smiley) as a deliberate rejection of an immaculate, corporate `Facade`.
-   **`P(a)/PPP Axiom`:** The entire engagement strategy was predicated on leading with the finished `Prototype(artifact)`—the catalogue—rather than a proposal.
-   **`Sovereignty, Not Victory`:** The goal of engaging with curators was not to "win" a sale or a show, but to establish a sovereign, peer-to-peer dialogue, retaining a live `exit()` path at all times.
-   **The `BODY` Check:** The entire project is grounded in the physical, embodied experience of the life drawing studio. The value of the final prints is explicitly tied to their material presence ("a material presence that a PDF can only approximate").

---

## **§3. The Artifacts (The Primary `Yield`)**

The project generated three primary, interlinked artifacts:

1.  **The Photographic Works:** A set of 16 unique, 1/1 double-exposure photographs, printed as archival pigment prints on cotton rag paper. Each work is an individual `yield` of the studio praxis.
2.  **The Digital Catalogue (`Catalogue-r13_Clements_bodyTime.pdf`):** A 41-page professional catalogue serving as the project's primary `interface` for the `Hyperstrate`. It includes the full body of work, the artist's statement ("Saying Yes, Softly"), technical specifications, provenance, and an at-a-glance availability checklist.
3.  **The Physical Exhibition:** The temporary installation of the works at Stokvel Gallery (15-16 March 2025), which served as the initial site of public `attestation`.

---

## **§4. The Praxis (The Execution)**

The project was executed in two distinct phases: the studio praxis and the `Hyperstrate` engagement.

### **4.1 Studio Praxis: "Saying Yes, Softly"**

The artworks were generated from a long-running (since 2017) life drawing practice built on a protocol of deep consent. As detailed in the catalogue's essay, the process begins not with drawing, but with a gentle "check-in" that diffuses traditional power dynamics between artist and model. This creates a space of quiet integrity where vulnerability is offered, not taken. The double-exposure technique is a direct translation of this layered, consensual, and time-rich environment.

### **4.2 `Hyperstrate` Engagement: Protocol-Driven `Pings`**

Following the successful initial showing, a targeted engagement campaign was initiated. Each `ping` was tailored to the specific function of the `node` being addressed.

-   **`Node`: The Curator**
    -   **Intent:** To establish a peer-to-peer dialogue and have the work evaluated by a key `node` in the secondary market.
    -   **Protocol:** Led with two strong `artifacts` (images), followed by the full `Prototype(artifact)` (the catalogue) upon request. The communication was designed to be a clean, high-integrity `Hyperstrate transaction`.

-   **`Node`: The Art Critic - *Authorized, Pending***
    -   **Intent:** To provoke discourse on the methodology behind the work.
    -   **Protocol:** Key of the `README` as an "open-source art practice / artist's book on hyperstrate" presenting the catalogue as a `yield` of that system.

-   **`Node`: The Art Consultant - *Authorized, Pending***
    -   **Intent:** To introduce a successful, collectible body of work to the primary market.
    -   **Protocol:** Key the series (the available works), also presenting the catalogue as a professional tool for collectors.

-   **`Node`: The Contemporary Gallerist - *Authorized, Pending***
    -   **Intent:** To introduce the `praxis` as a candidate for long-term representation.
    -   **Protocol:** Key the `bodyTime()` project as evidence of a mature and ongoing practice.

-   **`Node`: The International Juried Exhibition (Opale Art, "Echoes of Lynch")**
    -   **Status:** Complete / Attested.
    -   **Intent:** To extend the work's reach into a new, international context and place it in direct dialogue with a key art-historical influence (David Lynch).
    -   **Protocol:** Response to a direct, curated invitation. Submission of a refined package including a narrative artist biography, a formal artistic statement, and a critical text connecting the work to the exhibition's theme. This transaction serves as a high-integrity `attestation` from a respected international `node`.

### **4.3 The Facade Praxis: Dual Wall Texts**

A key component of the physical exhibition's `praxis` was a direct engagement with the system's "translation burden." To address the tension between the dense internal language of the `praxis` and the need for public accessibility, two distinct wall texts were presented side-by-side:

1.  **The Native Text:** A statement written in the high-density, specific lexicon of the `human-OS`. This text offered a direct, unfiltered view into the conceptual architecture behind the work, intended for `Observers` familiar with the system's grammar.

2.  **The Translated Text:** A carefully crafted version of the same core ideas, translated into localized, intermediate, second-language English. This text served as the primary `Facade` or `interface` for a general audience.

This act of "bilingual" presentation was not just a curatorial choice; it was a performance of the P(a)/PPP axiom. It provided the `Prototype(artifact)`—the art—and then offered two distinct `Proposals` for how to read it, respecting the sovereignty of the viewer to choose their own level of engagement. It made the act of translation itself a visible, honest part of the work.

## **§5. Validation & Attestation (The Results)**

The project's success is measured by the quality of the `attestations` received from the `Hyperstrate`.

-   **Market Attestation:** 8 of the 16 works (50%) were acquired by private collectors during the initial showing, including the culminating 32-exposure `bodyTime()` piece. This provides a strong, objective signal of the work's desirability.
-   **Curatorial Attestation:** *Positive.*

## **§5.1 Specific Attestations (Logged Events)**

### **Opale Art "Echoes of Lynch" Exhibition (June-July 2025)**

-   **Validation:** Following a direct curatorial invitation, the work was **selected by the jury** for inclusion in the final exhibition. This constitutes a formal `quorum` attestation.
-   **Curatorial Selection:** The work `20241101_1 (Toe)` was chosen for display, validating its conceptual alignment with the exhibition's Lynchian theme of tension and fragmentation.
-   **Praxis Translation:** The curatorial team successfully synthesized the artist's statements into a French caption that accurately reflected the core principles of the `bodyTime()` praxis. This validated the effectiveness of the project's `Facade` and the translatability of its core concepts.
-   **Logged Curatorial Text (Provenance):**
    > `André S. Clements questionne le regard porté sur le corps, à travers des images où le temps et le geste se superposent. Ses photographies naissent de séances de dessin où l’attention et le consentement tissent un espace de partage. Ici le corps ne s’expose pas, il se manifeste dans l’incertitude, entre apparition et effacement. Loin de toute recherche de spectaculaire, ses œuvres révèlent une présence fragile, attentive, où l’image ne cherche pas à capturer mais à laisser être, dans le respect du silence et du trouble.`

This event serves as a key validation of the P(a)/PPP axiom, where the high-integrity `Prototype(artifact)` (the work and its accompanying texts) generated a high-integrity `attestation` from an external, international `node`.
