## Context

**SAFFCA** (Southern African Foundation for Contemporary Art) is a non-profit connecting African and European art communities through:
- Cross-continental artist residencies
- SEED exhibitions and seasonal salons
- Visibility for unrepresented contemporary artists
- Multicultural dialogue between continents

**Entities:** SAFFCA NPC (South Africa) + SAFFCA.EU (Brussels, Abbaye de La Cambre)

**Current site:** Multi-lingual (EN/FR/NL), organizational focus, artist testimonials.
