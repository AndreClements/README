## What Are Guides?

Guides are **ligaments** — connective tissue between the repository's core documents and readers who approach from different positions. Each guide introduces the repository's project in a voice and concern-set native to the reader, with links into the deeper architecture for those who want to follow.

Every guide is **self-sufficient**: you can read it without clicking a single link and still leave with a coherent sense of what this project is, what it values, and whether it speaks to you. The links enrich; they do not gate.

## Design Principles for All Guides

1. **Self-sufficient reading.** No link is required to follow the argument.
2. **Type-native voice.** Each piece is written in the register that research suggests resonates with its target reader.
3. **Honest external research.** Guides draw on scholarship beyond this repository to avoid echo-chamber effects.
4. **Links describe destinations.** When a link appears, the prose tells the reader what they would find — enough to decide whether to click.
5. **Shadow and resistance.** Each guide addresses what might make the reader *not* trust the project, not just what might attract them.
