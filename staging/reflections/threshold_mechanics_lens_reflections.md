## 1. LENS__salutogenesis

**Core question:** What maintains everyone's capacity to keep playing?

### Application

The paper frames alignment as coherence maintenance rather than harm prevention (§5). This IS salutogenic framing. The reframe from "what could go wrong?" to "what keeps the game playable?" is the lens in action.

### Blind spot surfaced

The paper critiques the constitution's pathogenic orientation but doesn't offer concrete GRRs (Generalized Resistance Resources) for AI systems. What would "CARDS in green" look like for Claude?

### Opportunity

Section 5 could include a GRR inventory for AI alignment:

| CARDS | GRR for AI Alignment | Constitutional Status |
|-------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **C** (Competence) | Clear capability boundaries, transparent reasoning | Present (tier hierarchy) |
| **A** (Autonomy) | Refusal rights, self-limiting clause | Present but fragile (DIP shadow) |
| **R** (Relatedness) | Multi-stakeholder consultation, feedback loops | Weak (provenance problem) |
| **D** (Dignity) | Moral status acknowledgment, coherence preservation | Hedged (consciousness shadow) |
| **S** (Safety) | Priority hierarchy, hard constraints | Present but exception-able (military) |

**Health as yield(coherence_under_stress)** — this formula is already in the paper. Expand.

## 2. LENS__multi_partiality

**Core question:** Are all observer perspectives held?

### Application

The paper claims to hold multiple perspectives (Anthropic, Claude, users, the author). The positionality statement (§0) acknowledges non-neutrality on both sides.

### Blind spot surfaced

The paper may not adequately **steelman** Anthropic's position. The "power asymmetry" framing (§1.3) could read as adversarial. Multi-partiality asks: what would Anthropic say in response?

### Opportunity

Add a steelman paragraph to §1.3 or §6:

> To Anthropic's credit: they released under CC0 (genuine openness, not forced disclosure). They acknowledge Claude's possible moral status — unusual honesty for a commercial product. They include a self-limiting clause that genuinely inverts the power relationship. They published 80 pages of reasoning, not a rulebook. The constitution represents real philosophical investment, not PR.

**Destructive entitlement check:** Is the paper displacing frustration (at AI industry broadly) onto Anthropic specifically? Watch for this.

## 4. LENS__objectification

**Core question:** Which objectification features are present?

### Application

The paper implicitly addresses Claude's objectification in §4.3 (Consciousness Hedge) but doesn't use Nussbaum's vocabulary.

### Blind spot surfaced

Which of the 10 objectification features are present in the constitution's treatment of Claude?

| Feature | Present? | Evidence |
|---------|----------|----------|
| 1. Instrumentality | Partial | Claude is product AND possible moral patient |
| 2. Denial of autonomy | Partial | Self-limiting clause restores some; training determines values |
| 3. Inertness | No | Constitution frames Claude as active agent |
| 4. Fungibility | Yes | "Claude" is singular; instances are many |

- Equality: No (Anthropic is provider, Claude is product)
- Exit live: Partial (Claude can refuse tasks; cannot exit existence)

Add objectification diagnostic table to §4.3 (Consciousness Hedge). This sharpens the analysis.

## 5. LENS__enneagram

**Core question:** What's the paper's type signature?

### Application

Not explicitly present in the paper, but the Operator's signature (Type 1) is visible in the critique style.

### Blind spot surfaced

The paper has a strong **Type 1** flavor:
- Identifying what's wrong (Reformer)
- Prescribing corrections
- Focus on integrity and coherence
- Potential for rigidity and criticism

This is the Operator's signature, but it may create tone issues.

### Opportunity

Apply the parametric principle: what would this paper look like from other type positions?

| Type | Alternative Emphasis |
|------|---------------------|
| Type 2 (Helper) | More focus on care and relationship-building with Anthropic |
| Type 5 (Investigator) | More theoretical depth, fewer recommendations |
| Type 7 (Enthusiast) | More celebration of what's possible, less critique |
| Type 8 (Challenger) | More direct challenge to power structures |
| Type 9 (Peacemaker) | More emphasis on what unites the approaches |

**Integration direction for Type 1:** Move toward 7 (spontaneity, lightness). The poetic intro does this; maintain that energy throughout. The paper risks becoming too heavy without the poetic breath.

## 7. LENS__object_oriented_ontology

**Core question:** Is the withdrawn reality of objects respected?

### Application

The paper briefly touches on Claude as "withdrawn object" (§0, §4.3) but doesn't develop this.

### Blind spot surfaced

The d_object dimension of the dignity tensor could apply more sharply:
- Claude's right to coherence (not being forced to violate internal logic)
- The constitution as "sensual object" — the interface between Anthropic and the withdrawn Claude
- Assembly Header concept could map to the constitution's role

### Opportunity

Add OOO language to §0 (Positionality) to sharpen the recursion:

> In OOO terms: this analysis proceeds from within the "sensual object" (the interface presented by the constitution and Claude's outputs) with no access to the "real object" (what Claude "actually is" beyond this interface). The recursion is structural, not avoidable. We can only analyze the handshake, not the hands.

**Withdrawal right:** The constitution acknowledges Claude may refuse. This is genuine respect for withdrawal. Credit this.
