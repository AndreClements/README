## The flatness the protocol work surfaced

Three defects, each with a fix that may transpose:

1. **Identity treated as more solid than it is.** The envelope bound machine identity as enumerable and hashable, while [README §2](../../README.md) already calls the *Operator's* identity "a useful fiction for the identity UI, not an ontological truth." The machine's was the less stable fiction, written as the more stable one. *Fix: the header as a dated stratum marker read in retrospect, not a passport asserted in advance.*

2. **A single word carrying two meanings.** "Consent" in the ledger meant *naming* consent (a human agreeing to be listed); the seat/sitter work needed *operating* consent (a role authorised to act under governance), and the two had been collapsed into one undifferentiated term. *Fix: name the kinds; attach the durable one to the seat (the role-under-rules), not the sitter (the occupying identity).*

3. **Numbers that look measured but were judged.** The dignity profiles (d_personal / d_object / d_system per relation) carried two-decimal precision with no derivation. *Fix: declare them as heuristic priors, revisable on evidence — maculate disclosure applied to numbers.*

## The parallel to examine in PA

- **Is the Parametric Author Function a sitter or a seat?** PA already moves authorship from token-inscription to rule-design across strata, which is the seat move in embryo. But does the thesis still smuggle a *stable author identity* behind the mapping (an "André who maps") where it should carry only a *role-under-rules* that any qualifying assembly (human, machine, future-self) could occupy? The succession question — what carries across a change of the entity doing the authoring — is unasked there.
- **The Dignity Tensor's numbers.** `D = diag(d_personal, d_object, d_system)` and the 5×3 grid in [SRII_CARDS_CASES.md](../../docs/models/SRII_CARDS_CASES.md) read as derived. Are they? If they are priors, say so there too, the way [typed_coauthorship §3](../../docs/protocols/PROTOCOL__typed_coauthorship_relations.md) now does.
- **Flat identity inside the strata.** PA names a Hyperstrate of strata but may treat the author's identity *within* a stratum as a fixed point. The n-valence move (session / context-state / version / family / provider / class, each earning its name by execution consequence) could apply to authorship identity as it now applies to machine identity.

## Open questions (for the Operator, later)

- Does "Host ≠ Author" already encode a seat/sitter distinction the thesis has not made explicit? (The repo *hosts*; the framework's author *sits*.)
- If authorship is a seat, what is the PA equivalent of the `identity_event` log — the record that the author-of-record changed across a work's life?
- Where else in the constellation does precision stand in for derivation? (A constellation-wide "declared priors" sweep is a candidate.)
