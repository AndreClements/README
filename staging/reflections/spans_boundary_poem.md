---

Skin contains.
Skin reads.
Skin displays.
Skin mediates.

The gutter
is a skin.

Two pages,
hinged.

A question asked on one side,
answered on another
(later; earlier; out of order; out of sight; not).

You do not need to be watching.
It all keeps its log.
Doesn't it?

*And yet.*
