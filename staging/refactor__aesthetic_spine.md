# Refactor — the aesthetic spine

> **Why.** The masters paper narrowed onto A3★ (felt beauty tracks the legibility of a freely-chosen,
> dignity-preserving constraint) and, read coldly, had backed itself against an **anti-aesthetic wall**:
> beauty reduced to rule-recognition, the sublime fenced out, all gate and no generosity. The fix is
> **vertical, not horizontal**: name the genus the claim is a species of — the operator's standing
> theory of art — so beauty becomes *creativity, appreciated*, and A3★ becomes the authored-case
> species rather than the whole account.

## The genus (anchor)

- **Art** = the appreciable manifestation of creativity.
- **Creativity** = the making of new relations between elements, **towards the desirable**.

Three terms = three existing pillars: **new relations** → parametric authorship (the relation-making
engine); **towards the desirable** → CARDS / Vita-Socio-Anarco axiology (the emergent ethic); **appreciable
manifestation** → beauty (felt beauty as the authored-case species). Canonical definitions already live,
distributed: [GLOSSARY](../CONCEPTS/GLOSSARY.md) §Foundations (mirrored from the site), [README](../README.md)
§3 (Vita-Socio-Anarco praxis), [methodology__axiology_first_aesthetics](../docs/methods/methodology__axiology_first_aesthetics_for_engagement_design.md).
