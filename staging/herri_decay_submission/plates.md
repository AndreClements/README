## Captions as set

Captions run as printed in the essay. They carry place, capture time, and camera, in that order,
and nothing else. The time of day is doing work in this set (nine of the thirteen are night
frames) and should not be trimmed to a year.

**Plate 8, Zandspruit — carries an editorial condition.** Caption reads: *"Zandspruit, 13 June
2012, 15:26. A ball game on a closed road."* The caption names the game, not the unrest; the smoke
stays in the frame, unnarrated. This is the operator's ruling: the frame sits in the register of
reportage, witness and the resilience of youth, and the reasoning is carried by the essay's
Zandspruit passage, not by the caption. **The plate publishes only with that passage standing
beside it.** Do not add a caption gloss about protest, service delivery or hardship, and do not run
the image alone.

**Plate 5, place.** `Standerton Elektries` is **inscribed on the carriage** (with running number
`14160`); the photograph was made in **Braamfontein**. Four essay drafts and an earlier version of
this manifest gave Standerton as the location, having taken it from the filename. Corrected here on
the operator's confirmation. The stencil on the flank reads `MAG NIE GEBOGGEL OF / LOS GERANGEER
WORD NIE`, read off the 6000px original. Full record:
[provenance_ledger__herri_decay_plates.md](provenance_ledger__herri_decay_plates.md) §2–§3.

**Plate 7, `school_hillbrow`.** Undated by declaration, not by omission. If herri's house style
requires a year, the honest answer is "undated" rather than a guess. Note also that the archive
copy is 1080 × 1350 — an Instagram crop, so already a re-save — and that the frame shows a camera
**on** its tripod, cable release attached, screen lit: a working setup, not wreckage (ledger §4).

**Plate 1, `IMG_1278`.** 2023 is certain; the day is not. The PNG export carries no timestamp and
no original has been found under `ArtPraxis/`. The essay's *"three months earlier"* is the
operator's own recollection, kept verbatim rather than adjusted to fit an export date. If the
original can be retrieved from the G1X archive or the Lightroom catalogue before 1 August, both
this caption and that interval can be made exact.
