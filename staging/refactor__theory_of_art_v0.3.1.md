## 3. The findings

### 3.1 VALUE / WEIGHT / VALENCE — **confirmed. The node contradicts itself.**

The brief asks whether L195 is superseded by L188. It is. On an ordinary reading the contradiction is
direct — both halves inside the same section, ten lines apart:

| | the node says | at |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | **Value is weight** | L195 |
| (b) | **weight is how much, not whether** | L188 |
| ∴ | value is how much, not whether | — |

*(The second reader's caveat is fair and accepted: value, weight, whether are not typed terms in the
node, so this little derivation is an illustration, not a proof. The counterexample below is what
carries the finding, and it needs no formal system.)*

But the node also holds its own counterexample, **two lines above the premise**:

- L187 — *"The society that refused* Fountain *was in violent concern and no desire whatever."*
- L189 — *"a work can be unliftable and hated."*

*Unliftable and hated* is **high weight, negative valence**. If value were weight, the node would be
committed to saying *Fountain* had **maximal value in the field that threw it out** — and would have
no term left in which to say what the hating was. **The hating is the *whether*, and value-as-weight
has no slot for it.** The node states the counterexample and the superseded claim within ten lines of
each other.

So the operator's settled formulation (staging L188–191) is not a refinement — it already contains
the repair:

> **Relational mass** belongs to the **made configuration**.
> **Value** is the field-dependent **container**.
> **Weight** is the **magnitude registered** there.
> **Valence** tells us whether that weight attracts, repels, threatens, delights.

Value = ⟨weight, valence⟩. Both field-dependent. *Fountain* in the Society's field = ⟨enormous,
negative⟩. `Value is weight` returns ⟨enormous⟩ and drops the sign — which is why it cannot tell a
beloved masterpiece from a hated urinal when both are unliftable.

**What the finding licenses, and what it does not.** The contradiction rules out *value = unsigned
magnitude*. It does **not** by itself select the positive ontology: *value as container* (the
operator's settled wording, staging L189, already carried by the essays index), *value as the signed
registration of mass in a field*, and *value as the pair ⟨weight, valence⟩* are all consistent with
every fact above. The candidate below uses the container framing because it is the operator's own;
the choice among the three is his, and nothing in the diagnosis forecloses it.

### 3.2 CONSERVATION — **confirmed. Retire the word. Keep the paragraph.**

The essay retired conservation (staging R8, L223–227): *"Neither attention nor relation is a
demonstrated conserved quantity. **Keep:** cost · non-additivity · persistence · reactivation.
**Drop:** conservation."* Verified: the word appears **nowhere** in essay v0.3.0.

The node's item 4 body is almost entirely the essay's surviving content and is **sound**. The fault
is the label and one clause:

- **The label misfiles the item.** The section is *"Where the figure holds under load"* (L198) — a
  list of **correspondences** between the physics figure and art. Items 1–3 are genuine
  correspondences. Item 4 is not one. What it actually establishes is **cost**: mass has a price,
  paid from outside. **Cost is not conservation.** Cost says you cannot get mass without spending;
  conservation says a total is preserved across transformations. The only conserved quantity in play

  art.**

  storage reading (*"It is **not stored in the work**"*; *"does not decant energy into the canvas"*)

### 3.3 WHERE MASS LIVES — **confirmed, and slightly worse than latent.**

The brief asks whether L180 is a *latent* version of the boundary bug. It is a real one — the node
does not commit the error (L215–216 supplies the right answer), but **L180 and L214 cannot both be
the definition of relational mass**, and the node is running both.

| line | says |
|---|---|
| **L180** (the definition) | "the relations creativity deposits **in a work**" |
| **L214** (the demonstration) | "*Fountain*, whose porcelain was ordinary and whose **made configuration** was not" |
| **L325** (the provenance row) | "it **belongs to the made configuration**… **not to the porcelain**" |

**The killer is in the node's own worked example.** L214–215 lists the made configuration as *"the
turn, the title, the signature, **and a society that had promised no jury**."* **You cannot deposit a
society inside a urinal.** The configuration includes a relatum the maker did not make and which is
nowhere in the object. L180's container metaphor **cannot hold the node's own Fountain account** —
which is why L215 has to say *"it was never in the porcelain"* as a **correction** rather than as an
entailment of the definition.

**The provenance row is more correct than the definition it records.** That is the partial-absorption
pattern exactly: the ledger got the finding; the definition never did.

Note also what "in a work" does under the node's own axiom. If art is **configured relations**, then
the work **is** the configuration — so relations cannot be deposited *in* it; there is no container
prior to the deposit to receive them. `in a work` quietly makes the work a **vessel that has mass**
rather than a configuration that **is** mass.

**Candidate — provisional. The minimal (essay-style) fix and the node-register fix are different:**

- *Minimal, delocating only:* "the relations **a making leaves behind it**" — the essay's R7 move.
  Takes the location out, puts nothing in, so *Fountain* never breaks it.
- *Node-register, recommended:* delocate **and** state the positive account, because the node's job
  is to say where mass lives — e.g. "the relations a making leaves behind it: its **relational
  mass**, belonging to the **made configuration**, invariant across who is looking."

The R7 warning against planted exposition (staging L221: *"silence is the better seed"*) is
calibrated to the essay's brief and **does not govern here**. **Keep** `invariant across who is
looking` and `Mass is what is made; it does not move when the room does` — both are correct and
load-bearing.

**Independently corroborated twice.** The repo sweep reached this finding without being told it, and
`staging/reflections/review__toa_conversation_2026-07-07.md` **L28** already records the operator's
own account: *"value is generated by the local field, **not resident in the work**."* Mirrored at
**af.md L168** (§8.1).

### 3.4 THE LOOP — **confirmed absent; adding it is a proposal, not a repair.**

`grep` returns **no** "Stieglitz", **no** "Blind Man", **no** "resign", **no** "further making",
**no** "new making" anywhere in the node. The loop's return path (**weight → new making → new mass**)
is entirely missing.

**The node has the refusal but files it under the wrong mechanism.** *"The society that refused
Fountain"* appears at L187 — inside the **Field** bullet, doing a **concern≠desire** job. It never
appears in **Curvature** (L213–222), where *Fountain*'s continuing bending is asserted.

**Item 2 is not the loop.** *Withdrawal and revival* (L207–212) is: same mass, field withdraws, new
field arrives, mass re-weighed. **No new making, no new mass.** The loop is a different mechanism:
weight **provokes further making**, and those makings **deposit new mass**.

**Reclassified after second-reader review: the absence is confirmed; the addition is proposed, not
forced.** A first draft of this proposal argued the node *needs* the loop to explain *Fountain*'s
growing curvature. That overstated it, twice:

- **The node asserts persistence, not growth.** L216 says the work *"is still bending the room"* — a
  fixed mass bending steadily is fully consistent with that. The century of *growth* is in the essay
  and the history, not in the node's text. It was imported.
- **There is a fourth option the first trichotomy missed** (the second reader supplied it): a fixed
  configuration's influence can spread **field-side** — more fields taking the same mass up — with no
  new mass anywhere. The node as written is not contradictory without the loop.

What the loop actually does for the node is **prophylactic**: it forecloses the natural misreading
that reception thickens the original mass — the collapse the operator named, and the death of the
mass/field distinction. And it is the **operator's settled conceptual result** (staging L193–198:
*"mass → weight → new making → new mass… A loop, not a category collapse"*). **Landing it in the node
is adopting his settled formulation, and it needs his explicit yes — a theoretical addition, not an
error-repair.**

**Placement, if adopted — and a deliberate restraint.** The loop belongs in the **Weight bullet**
(the art-side definition), **not** as a fifth item under *"Where the figure holds under load"* —
because that section claims **correspondences with the physics figure**, and whether physics has a
counterpart to this loop was not verified, so no correspondence is claimed. The finding does not need
it. Its *Fountain* demonstration can sit in **Curvature**, where the continuing curvature is
asserted.

**If the demonstration names the history, the tripwires bind** (staging L275, L308; all ✅ verified in
essay L46): the resignation ✓ and the Stieglitz photograph → unsigned *Blind Man* editorial ✓ are
safe. **No J.L. Mott. No named editorial author — it is unsigned. No "hidden behind a partition."**
The node currently names nobody in the Fountain passage; adding the resignation would be its first
attribution, and *"Duchamp was on its board, and resigned"* is the essay's verified form.

weight draws or repels"* and never uses the word. That keeps the canonical statement legible to a
