### 1.4 Why Not Just Expand CLEAN_LANGUAGE.md?

Clean Space is a **distinct methodology** from Clean Language:

| Dimension | Clean Language | Clean Space |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Primary medium | Verbal metaphor | Physical space and movement |
| Toolkit size | ~12 questions | 6 questions + 4 directing statements |
| What gets externalised | Internal symbolic landscape (described) | Spatial network (walked, stood in, seen from) |
| Session modality | Seated dialogue | Client moves around room |
| Emergence mechanism | Iterative metaphor development | Network effects across populated spaces |
| Key influence | Ericksonian hypnotherapy | Complexity theory, self-organising systems |
| Typical duration | Variable | 15–90 minutes |

The difference is sufficient to warrant a separate entry (like CARDS vs. SDT — related lineage, distinct methodology).

### 4.5 The SkillsClinic Frame: 2nd-Order Facilitation

This is the distinctive utility that justifies a separate entry:

```
SkillsClinic problem:
  Technical person has rich internal model (architecture, solution, analysis)
  → Needs to present it externally (talk, pitch, documentation)
  → Translation gap: internal model ≠ presentation structure
  → Especially hard for non-visual thinkers who can't "just draw it"

Clean Space as 2nd-order tool:
  Facilitator (SkillsClinic) doesn't need domain expertise
  → Guides client through Clean Space process
  → Client physically walks their own thinking
  → Spatial arrangement IS the structure (no separate "visualisation" step)
  → Network effects produce emergent organisation
  → Result: client discovers their own presentation structure

Why "2nd order":
  1st order = helping someone prepare a presentation (content, slides, delivery)
  2nd order = giving them a process that lets them discover their own structure
  → The facilitator facilitates the facilitation, not the content
```

This maps cleanly to the existing repo's concern with **sovereignty** — the technical person remains the expert; the facilitator provides process, not interpretation.
