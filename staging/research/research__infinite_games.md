## Core Constructs (L0)

| Construct | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Finite Game** | Known players, fixed rules, definite endpoint. Played *to win*. Produces titles. |
| **Infinite Game** | Known and unknown players, changeable rules, no endpoint. Played *to continue play*. |
| **Society vs. Culture** | Society = finite institution (bounded, rule-enforced). Culture = infinite (open, deviation-welcoming). |
| **Machine vs. Garden** | Machine = finite artefact (task-completion). Garden = infinite artefact (growth, spontaneity). |
| **Surprise** | Finite players train against surprise. Infinite players play *in expectation of being surprised*. |
| **Power vs. Strength** | Power = control over others (finite). Strength = self-sufficient capacity that does not diminish others (infinite). |
| **Poietai** | Infinite players as creators — "joyful poets of a story that continues to originate what they cannot finish." |
| **Voluntary participation** | "If a person must play, that person cannot play." All play requires freedom. |

**Central thesis:** The value of a game is not in its outcome but in its capacity for continuation. There is only one infinite game — and all finite games are played within it.

### SE-1: Agnoletti et al. (2022) — Gold

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Key Claims** | (1) Carse's finite/infinite distinction applies diagnostically to healthcare systems. (2) "Ethical fading" is the mechanism by which infinite games (patient care, medical vocation) are transformed into finite games (resource competition, career survival). (3) Scarcity conditions — economic pressure, pandemic effects, inflation — drive finite-ification of inherently infinite systems. |
| **Methodology** | Theoretical framework application + qualitative case study (Italian public healthcare system, 2021–2022). Not quantitative-empirical; uses Carse as an analytic lens applied to public data (tax revenue allocation, inflation effects on health budgets). |
| **Relevance** | **High.** Demonstrates that the finite/infinite distinction has diagnostic utility in real-world institutional settings. Directly supports the "game-type diagnostic lens" gap hypothesis. The "ethical fading" construct maps to CARDS: scarcity erodes professional autonomy (A) and patient dignity (D). |
| **Notes** | Open access via PMC. Peer-reviewed (Discover Health Systems, Springer). Most directly operationalised application of Carse in the Gold tier. Limitation: single-country case study; relies on Sinek's derivative framing alongside Carse. |

### SE-2: Harré & Madden (2017) — Gold

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Key Claims** | (1) Infinite play conditions can be empirically created and their effects measured. (2) Values emerging under infinite conditions: connection to people/nature, expression, personal strengths, vitality, spirituality. (3) Values under finite conditions: systems/regulations, success/status, money, ownership, domination — valued only instrumentally. (4) Workshop intervention produces awareness, reflection, choice, sharing, praxis. |
| **Methodology** | Mixed-methods empirical study. N=1,085 participants across 30 workshops in New Zealand. Paper dart games operationalise finite/infinite play conditions. Pre/post observation + written participant responses. Strongest empirical validation of Carse's framework in the literature. |
| **Relevance** | **Very High.** The only large-N empirical operationalisation of finite/infinite games. Directly addresses the primary critique of Carse (no empirical grounding). Values found under infinite conditions map strongly to CARDS: Autonomy (expression), Relatedness (connection), Dignity (vitality as self-worth). Demonstrates that finite-game framing transforms nature from intrinsic value to instrumental "resource." |
| **Notes** | Access via ResearchGate preprint. Published in *Ecopsychology* (peer-reviewed). The CARDS mapping is the strongest of any secondary source. Key L2 citation if needed: Harré's (2018) book-length treatment (*The Infinite Game*, Auckland University Press — Silver tier). Limitation: predominantly Western, educated, self-selected sample. |

### SE-4: Nguyen (2020) — Gold

| Dimension | Assessment |

### Draft Utility Statement

> Finite and Infinite Games provides a **game-type diagnostic lens** — a clean, memorable binary (finite/infinite) that complements existing continuum-based frameworks in the repository. It offers:
>
> 1. **Diagnostic power:** Any system, relationship, or design choice can be interrogated as finite-game or infinite-game thinking. This produces immediate, actionable insight.
> 2. **Philosophical grounding for parametric authorship:** The infinite player as parametric author — setting constraints that enable continuation rather than determine outcomes — provides the deepest philosophical justification for the repository's central design methodology.
> 3. **Threat model for gamification:** Via Nguyen (2020), the finite/infinite lens identifies precisely where gamification (finite thinking applied to infinite domains) degrades autonomy through "value leakage."
> 4. **CARDS Autonomy axiom:** Carse's "if a person must play, that person cannot play" is the strongest external philosophical statement of CARDS Autonomy in the library.
> 5. **Bridging function:** Connects Salutogenesis (health-creation as infinite game), SOLID (OCP as infinite-game architecture), and Multi-Partiality (holding the relational field open) under a single unifying metaphor.

### Parametric Authorship Narrative

The connection between Carse's infinite games and parametric authorship is the **strongest integration hook** in this candidate and warrants explicit narrative treatment in the eventual lib entry.

**The argument runs:**
- Parametric authorship = designing constraints within which others can create.
- Finite games have fixed rules imposed externally; infinite games have rules that change by agreement of the players.
- The parametric author is, by definition, an infinite player: they set parameters (constraints) not to determine outcomes but to enable continuation of creative play.
- Carse's "poietai" — "joyful poets of a story that continues to originate what they cannot finish" — is a near-perfect description of the parametric author's stance.
- The infinite game is the *only* game type compatible with parametric authorship. A finite-game author controls outcomes; an infinite-game author enables them.

**Implication for the lib entry:** This connection should feature prominently in the lib entry's Utility section (§1) and should be developed into a formal integration mapping in the Cross-Framework Integration section. It is the candidate's unique contribution to the repository beyond what any other lib entry provides.
