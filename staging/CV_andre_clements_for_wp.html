Profile

Systems architect and creative technologist with 30 years across software engineering, visual arts, and facilitation. I design systems that are auditable, consent-aware, and values-centered — bridging technical architecture with the processes people actually use.

Core practice: building coherent structures across domains that don't usually talk to each other — code and art, technology and ethics, automation, philosophy and 'common' human judgment.

What I Do

Systems Architecture & Integration Design and maintain multi-system platforms with long uptimes. Specialise in API integration, permission-based access control, idempotent sync patterns, and documentation-as-specification.

Human-AI Collaboration Develop structured methodologies for working with AI tools — including provenance tracking, validation protocols, and consent-based workflows with accountable authority. Have built formal methodology for Computational Intelligence (CI) collaboration (see METHODOLOGY_CI).

Consent-Informed Design Apply consent architecture across technical and human systems: permission regimes, boundary protocols, graceful exits, and dignity-preserving interfaces. This capability spans code (API permissions, access control) and facilitation (workshop protocols, community guidelines).

Creative Technology Bridge visual/experiential design with engineering. Build tools for artists and creative workflows. Treat aesthetics as a design constraint, not an afterthought.

Facilitation & Experience Design Design and run consent-based workshops, community spaces, and learning experiences. Trained in Clean Language facilitation and systems-informed group dynamics.
