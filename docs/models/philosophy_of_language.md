## The couplet (axiom)

> *Language is a magical, world-creating force.*
> *Language is also just the noise made by a piece of meat in a mouth.*
> — [README.md](../../README.md)

Both are true at once. The first reading is **generative**: language brings forth a world, makes
relations that were not there, binds and looses. The second is **material**: it is breath over wet
tissue, a body's vibration, perishable and dumb. The theory's whole content is in the **refusal to
resolve** the two: meaning is not the force *minus* the meat, nor the meat *minus* the force. It
**fans out across the gap** between them, and never collapses to either. This is the *and-yet* held as
a standing condition rather than a move made once.

## Three joints (to develop)

1. **Language as relation-making medium** — a native instance of the [genus](theory_of_art.md):
   to speak is to make new relations between elements towards the desirable (or away from it). The
   sign is a constraint that produces a family of utterances.
2. **The body makes the noise** — the meat half is owed to [philosophy_of_body](philosophy_of_body.md):
   the noise is a bodily output, and the body is the final arbiter of whether the world-creating
   reading is grounded or only glossed. A held pose is the same shape of problem: world-creating
   *as-if* a study, and also just a body under a fall of light.
3. **Reading is running** — the joint to the thesis (A0, equation-as-machine): a formal expression
   is an executable grammar, and to read it is to run it. Language is, in this register, the machine
   and the noise the machine makes.
