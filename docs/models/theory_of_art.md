# **Theory of Art** (v0.3.0)

# /docs/models/theory_of_art.md (v0.3.0)

**Lead Maintainer's Note.** This is a foundational node. It does not coin the theory of art — that
theory is the operator's, stated canonically in the [site glossary](https://andresclements.com/glossary/)
and mirrored in [CONCEPTS/GLOSSARY.md](../../CONCEPTS/GLOSSARY.md) §Foundations, and lived in
[README.md](../../README.md) §3 (*"Art as creativity manifest. Love, its risk."*). What this node does
is **consolidate** the theory that was distributed across glossary, praxis kernel, methodology, and the
parametric-authorship thesis, and name it as the **genus** the rest hang from. Where it borrows
(Kant on purposive form, OOO on the withdrawn object, Nussbaum on the cluster), attribution stays
theirs — **Host ≠ Author**. The *assembly into one genus* is the author's, and is held lightly.

## The axiom

> **Art is the appreciable manifestation of creativity.**
> **Creativity is the making of new relations, towards the desirable.**
> **The desirable is a dependent variable** — a field a-flutter in drafts and
> gusts across domains of concern and among agencies.
> Appreciable  — intersubjective.
> More plainly:
> what Art is,
> is shared but not universal,
> subjective but not isolated.
> Authorship is dead, long live authorship, and the magnificent dance in her mighty sails.

### The genus has no outside

There is no anti-aesthetic *outside* to escape to. A maker who professes or pursues an anti-aesthetic
has not left the aesthetic; they have **flipped the upstream parameters** — reset what counts as
desirable (toward the raw, the abject, the dissonant, the deliberately ugly). The work never stops
being aesthetic; it is the same machinery at a different setting. The brutalist slab, the noise track,
the readymade are configurations in the genus, not its negation. This is the genus's strength: it has
no edge to fall off, only regions. There is no anti-aesthetic wall to rescue beauty from; the
"anti-aesthetic" is a coordinate inside the space, not its edge.

Three terms carry the axiom, and each is a pillar the project already stands on.

### 1. *New relations* — the generative pillar

Creativity, at root, is **relation-making**: holding elements (previously unrelated, or otherwise
related) and bringing forth a relation that was not there before. 
The relata are not some fixed cosmic inventory waiting to be related — they are
individuated *locally*, by the domain of concern doing the appreciating, and *new* is relative to that domain. 
The elements are named by the field, not before it. This is what
[parametric authorship](../../projects/theses/parametric_authorship.md) is the engineering, the steering, of —
authoring the *rules* that produce a *family*, where the work is the mapping and not the mark (A1, Map-over-Mark). 
A clean instance in the constellation is the 1/N layered average, eg. thirty-five
historical images of Eve held at equal weight until a figure emerges *that none of them alone contains*. 
The constraint is the relation-making engine. Creativity is what it does.

### 2. *Towards the desirable* — the axiological pillar (a dependent variable)

A new relation is not yet art, and the orientation that makes it so is **axiological**: the relations
that count are made *towards the desirable*. But the desirable is not fixed. It is a **dependent
variable** — it varies across domains of concern and among agencies, both read at every magnitude:

- **Scale** — the magnitude at which the other two are read, not a third axis of the same kind:
  every agency exists at a scale, every domain of concern is scaled. What is desirable for a cell is
  not what is desirable for an organism, a studio, a society, an ecosystem. The dignity tri-scope
  (personal / object / system) is this index, named small. (A fellow artist once heard *scales* here
  in the other sense — the weighing kind — reading the axiom's earlier registration, whose "across
  scales" wording v0.3.0 has since revised out. The misreading turned out to belong: a field read
  across magnitudes is where the same mass weighs differently on different scales, in both senses at
  once. The side-door word entered through a formulation that no longer exists, delivered the
  mass–weight–field model, and was released from the canonical text — the homonym's honourable
  discharge. The earlier wording survives in the Registrations and site keys, so nothing is orphaned.)
- **Domain of concern** — the aesthetically desirable, the ethically desirable, the economically and
  ecologically desirable do not coincide, and a work lives in several at once.
- **Agency** — *whose* desirable? The sitter's, the curator's, the institution's, the machine's, the
  long-dead depicted. [Multipartiality](../lenses/LENS__multi_partiality.md) is the discipline of
  holding more than one setting of the desirable at once without collapsing them.

So "towards the desirable" points at **weather**, not a fixed star — and the weather is *derived*:
many masses curving one shared field make an N-body system, and gustiness is what that looks like
from inside (the mass–weight–field model below carries the derivation). This is
why the methodology evaluates **axiology-first**: not because the desirable is a known constant to check against, but
because *which* desirable is in force, at which scale and for which party, is the first thing a making
settles, knowingly or not. And it reconciles the canonical split cleanly: **Creativity** orients
toward the desirable as a free variable; **Radical Creativity** is creativity with that variable
**bound** to Vita-Socio-Anarco (life first, social relatedness next, then non-domination). The
GLOSSARY split is the variable and one of its bindings, not two rival definitions.

### 3. *Appreciable manifestation* — the aesthetic pillar (beauty is one mode)

The third term is reception. Creativity becomes art when it is **manifest** (brought to a surface) and
**appreciable** (able to be felt, read, dwelt in).

> **Appreciation** is the felt registration of creativity — the sense, before one can say it, that a
> relation was made, toward *some* setting of the desirable. **Beauty** is the *mode* of appreciation
> when that setting reads as coherence held toward the desirable. Shift the setting and appreciation
> takes another mode — the **sublime** (the desirable pitched past mastery, at the formless), the
> **abject**, the **dissonant** — without ever ceasing to be aesthetic. Beauty is not the whole of
> appreciation; it is one of its weathers.

[philosophy_of_body](philosophy_of_body.md) §2 already holds the floor under all of this: a
manifestation is appreciable only if it is grounded in fragile, embodied truth — *"beauty that is not
grounded in this is only decoration."* Appreciation is cashed out in a body, whatever its mode.

Body and field are not rivals. The **body is the site** where weight is registered — mass cashed out
in fragile contact; the **field is the condition** that sets how much it weighs. The intersubjective
gloss (*appreciable, shared but not universal*) and the body-grounding are one claim at two scales: a
weight is felt in *a* body and contested across *many*. Standing is graded accordingly — dignity
standing reaches every patient that can be affected (sitter, material, the machine), while the
franchise to *testify* that a relation was made tracks provenance-light and falls silent past the
wall (the machine appreciates from *past the wall*, where the authored claim makes no promise). The
genus needs only that appreciation lands in *some* body; *who* may testify to it is the
responsibility question, held in the CII stack and the parametric-authorship thesis, not here.

The **authored-case species** the masters paper tests is *felt beauty*: in an authored artefact, felt
beauty tracks, defeasibly, the legibility of the creativity — the new relations toward the desirable —
realised through a freely-chosen, dignity-preserving constraint. It carries a defeater and an outside
**precisely because its setting of the desirable is fixed** (coherence + dignity-preservation): it is
one species at one setting, not the whole genus. The legibility it tracks is the legibility *of the
relation-making*, not of a rule admired for its own sake. That is what keeps it from reading as
rule-worship — and the genus around it is what keeps it from having to pretend it is all of beauty.

## The mass–weight–field model

The three pillars leave one thing underspecified: *what varies when the desirable varies?* The
dependent-variable claim says the desirable is not a `const`; this model says what it is a variable
*of*. It reads the axiom through gravity.

- **Mass** — the relations creativity deposits in a work: its **relational mass**, invariant across
  who is looking. The generative pillar, restated. *"Creativity is the making of new relations"* and
  *"value is ontological mass"* are one claim. Mass is what is made; it does not move when the room
  does.
- **Field** — the desirable: the dependent variable of §2, configured differently by each domain of
  concern, each agency, each magnitude it is read at.
- **Weight** — what is *felt*: value as it lands in a body, a market, a canon. Weight is relational
  mass taken up by a local field. Mass alone is not yet value; it is structure awaiting a field to be
  weighed in. The two older formulations — *value is ontological mass*; *the desirable is a dependent
  variable* — turn out to be the two factors of one product.

> **Value is weight — the work's relational mass taken up by a field of desire; no mass, no weight;
> no field, no weight; and mass enough curves the field.**

Where the figure holds under load:

1. **Binding.** In physics a bound system's mass is not the sum of its parts (the mass defect); the
   binding *is* mass. *"New relations"* and *"relational mass"* say one thing run through `E = mc²`:
   mass is non-additive at the binding level, which is art's rule too — two good halves do not make
   twice the work.
2. **Withdrawal, and revival.** When a work's world dies, its mass persists and its weight goes to
   zero: structure intact, field gone. Art-death without any physical change — and the reason revival
   is possible at all, since a new field can take up mass that outlived the old one's withdrawal.
   This is the axiom's closing line as mechanism: authorship dies and lives again because the mass
   outlasts the field. *(World-withdrawal is Heidegger's; exhibition value succeeding cult value is
   Benjamin's — both named for lineage, not authority; hosted, unverified, pending a primary read.)*
3. **Curvature.** Mass does not merely sit in the field; enough of it bends the field. Canon
   formation is curvature — Duchamp, a body whose object-side mass was near nil and whose field-side
   relation permanently bent the space every later work falls through. This is the landscape equation
   at its widest setting (body as terrain, attention as weather, relation as topology), and it is why
   the desirable *gusts*: many masses jointly curving one shared field is an N-body system — no closed
   form, sensitive to small change. The *"field a-flutter in drafts and gusts"* is the derived
   behaviour of multi-polar mass–field dynamics, seen from inside — gustiness that falls out of the
   many-body field.
4. **Conservation.** Creativity does not mint mass from nothing; it *binds* energy flowing in from
   outside art — labour, attention, materials, lives — into relational mass. Conservation holds
   because the ledger runs past art's edge: there is no outside of the aesthetic *within* the field
   (the genus has no outside), but there is an outside *of art*, and energy crosses it both ways.
   Works decay; attention withdraws; bound energy dissipates back into the general economy.
   Expansive, not expansionist.

**En tog** — one strain, kept in the open rather than smoothed away. Physics identifies gravitational
mass (what attracts) with inertial mass (what resists acceleration); in art, the works that pull
hardest and the works that most resist reinterpretation may not be the same works. Whether they come
apart is an open question, possibly a productive one. A footnote, not a fatality.

## Settings of the desirable (species under the genus)

The genus is wide; the project does not test all of it. Naming the setting keeps each claim honest
about which weather it actually speaks for.

| Setting of the desirable | Mode of appreciation | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| coherence + dignity, via a legible freely-chosen constraint | **beauty** (felt beauty) | masters paper §6; [LENS__parametric_authorship](../lenses/LENS__parametric_authorship.md) |
| the formless, past mastery (the un-authored) | **the sublime** | the genus holds it; the authored species does **not** claim it (du Preez 2022) |
| the raw, the broken, the deliberately ugly | the **anti-aesthetic / abject / dissonant** — flipped parameters, still inside | the genus has no outside |
| the well-being of all life, social relations, non-domination | **radical artistry** | GLOSSARY §Foundations; Vita-Socio-Anarco |

The sublime and the abject are the load-bearing cases. The masters paper *brackets* them out of the
felt-beauty claim, and that bracketing reads as anti-aesthetic only if felt beauty is mistaken for the
whole theory. Under the genus it is not: they are other settings of the desirable, fully aesthetic,
simply outside the species the authored claim scopes to. The genus holds them; A3★ declines to *claim*
them. *En tog.*

## Registrations — the axiom in other keys

The axiom is stated in more than one place, in more than one wording. To keep that from reading as
drift, this node names the **canonical text** and treats the rest as **registrations in another
key** — the same claim modulated for its medium, not rival definitions.

- **Canonical (this node):** *Art is the appreciable manifestation of creativity. Creativity is the
  making of new relations, towards the desirable.* With the gloss: *value is weight — relational mass
  taken up by a field of desire.*
- **Site / Foundations key** — [GLOSSARY](../../CONCEPTS/GLOSSARY.md) §Foundations: the discursive
  statement (*"…new or different relations between elements…"*). Site-canonical; its "between
  elements" wording is the earlier registration, to be re-mirrored when the site is next updated.
- **Praxis key** — [README §3](../../README.md): *"Creativity is significant relation. Art is
  creativity manifest. Love, its risk."*
- **Masters key** — the felt-beauty species, scoped to one setting (coherence + dignity).
- **Afrikaans key** — [theory_of_art.af.md](theory_of_art.af.md): *"Kuns is die waardeerbare
  vergestalting van kreatiwiteit…"* — a transposition, not a translation; the flourish keeps its own
  idiom (*"die magiese dans in haar vlae en seile"*).

Wording drift across keys is **declared modulation**, logged here — not silent inconsistency. (The
site re-mirror of the canonical wording is a separate, operator-run step.)
