## 0. Frame

* **Room is alive.** Expression (including mess) is welcomed.
* **Container first.** No one gets drafted into a private storyline.
* **Exceptions are adjudicated by the present host, case-by-case.**

> **Poetic brief:** The room of flying tables, is a room that breathes. 

## 1. Terms of art

### Multi-partiality (over neutrality)

* **Multi-partiality** *stands with* more than one party at once: listening for each object’s/agent's stakes and granting adequate attention without collapsing difference. It is *bias-aware stewardship*, not referee theatre.
* **Neutrality** (as anti-pattern) withdraws; it often launders power by pretending not to see it.
* **Power Equilibriam** *looks like* take your power, but only your own power. No More No less. Even when and where it is true that *'power is not shared, power is taken'*. 

### Conflict resilience (over conflict resolution)

* **Resilience** equips the room to *carry friction and or heat without tearing*: pause, slow, route, and continue. We prefer durable capacity over tidy endings.
* **Resolution** (as anti-pattern) aims to end disagreement; it risks premature closure and costs superficiality of relations.

### Prioritisation sequence

1. **Vitality** — protect autonomy as **choice** and **capacity** (dignity, safety, capacity).
2. **Connection/Relatedness** — enable seeing/being-seen without conscription. (expression, exploration, embodyment)
3. **Power asymmetry / Anti-coercion** — notice gradients; resist banal coercion (the everyday push that makes people smaller). (balance, compassion, integrity/honesty)

## 3. Invariants (minima to keep the field intact)

* **No conscription.** The room is not a courier for anyone’s private message.
* **No coercion/shaming.** Technique critique ≠ identity critique.
* **No triangle recruitment.** Private subtext may exist; it may not enlist third parties.

## 4. Affordances (explicitly allowed)

* **Maculateness**: imperfect, sensual, status-charged, ambiguous moves.
* **Play**: poses, prompts, performative edges.
* **Status theatre**: tolerated **until** it recruits others or degrades dignity.

## 5. Signals & Heat

* **Host heat-check (silent 1–5):** shame-heat, status-theatre, room-attention load.
  If any hits **4**, perform an **Interrupt** (§6).
* **Quiet Tap-Out (consent to pause):** palm-signal to host → host pauses set.

## 6. Interrupts (fast, minimal, non-carceral)

* **Pause:** “Breath break — new set in 60s.”
* **Privatise:** “Take that as a 1‑to‑1 note after the set.”
* **Route critique:** “Comments via host; keep the field clean.”

## 7. Light Operators (knobs, not rules)

* **Pose Brief (optional, ≤10s):** model states “line/mood” in one sentence.
  *Keeps critique aesthetic, not personal.*
* **Advice Hygiene:** technique/fitness pointers by request or after-set, 1‑to‑1.
  *Not censorship; dignity guard.*
* **Time Integrity:** timer visible; overruns are host-only decisions.

## 8. Contact-Laundering Guard (non-negotiable)

The room is not an alibi or relay.
**Policy:** If A wants B, **contact must be direct and consentful, off‑room**.
Host is not an inter-participant router.

## 9. Session Flow

**Before**

* Host scan: roles, prior entanglements, fragility points.
* State the minima (§3) and the existence of Tap‑Out.
* Optionally use Pose Brief.

**During**

* Run timer; hold aesthetic focus.
* Apply Interrupts when heat ≥4.
* Convert call-outs → via-host comments.

**After (short debrief)**

* Three checks: (1) any triangle recruitment? (2) dignity intact? (3) drawing field protected without muting expression?
* Note deltas for next session.

## 10. Failure States → Recovery

* **F1: Subtext colonises the room.**
  *Recovery:* Pause → Privatise → Resume with neutral brief.
* **F2: Public shaming spike.**
  *Recovery:* Interrupt → Reframe as technique → Offer 1‑to‑1 after-set.
* **F3: Contact laundering attempt.**
  *Recovery:* Invoke §8 verbatim; do not transmit; continue session.
* **F4: Host in the triangle.**
  *Recovery:* Name it; hand off briefly if viable or pause & reset brief or context.

## 11. Metrics (lightweight)

* **Resets per session** (interrupt count)
* **Recruitment incidents** (F3/F4)
* **Field Integrity Score** (host’s 1–5 composite after debrief)
* **Participant dignity notes** (brief, anonymised)

## 12. Micro-messages (templates)

* **To a participant seeking relay:**

  > I don’t carry messages. If you want contact, do it directly and with consent, outside the session.
* **To a model mid-heat:**

  > Quick breath. We’ll resume in 5 mins with a simple line/mood brief.
* **To a critic crossing into identity:**

  > Route via host; keep it to the work, not the person.

## 13. Theory fit (stated plainly)

* **Parametric Authorship** — Host as operator assesses and transparantly adjusts parameters (time, routing, interrupt thresholds) to maintain distributed authorship without drama triangle theatre. \[1]
* **Landscapification** — Treat the session as terrain with flows; participants modulate flow without flattening relief. \[2]
