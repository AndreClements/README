## The returned marks

**This file records marks. It does not amend v1.0.0.** Whether any mark enters a v1.1.0 is the operator's decision, deferred, and the deferral is the point: a returned reading is a gift held, and holding is an act with its own record.

A poet correspondent, sent the poem and then the voiced reading, returned the poem with punctuation-only changes: their account (paraphrased; the verbatim rests in the private archive) was that the words stood untouched, and that the handful of marks that moved were set where the voiced reading seemed, to their ear, already to place them. The correspondent responded to the *voiced* poem. That is why the marks read the way they do, and why the relation types the way it does below.

### The truncation, declared

The returned text survives only as a message preview, cut mid-word at the third word of S7 (the second single-line refrain). Marks for S7–S13 are unrecovered. ### The pattern, as inference

What the six marks do, read together: a colon after the doubled-verb line, opening the list it announces; a dash setting off the stanza's turn; a semicolon at the one pivot. S10 and S12 carry the doubled-verb figure too, so the pattern *may* continue past the cut. That is an inference (ledger I-01), recorded and left alone.
