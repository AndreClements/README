# Urban Decay is a Verb

*v0.1.0 ~ 1,300 words · thirteen plates · Johannesburg, and elsewhere, 2012–2026*

_A photo-essay. The pictures are not illustrations; several of them are the argument, and the text is what I owe them._

---

The garbage trucks stopped coming, and when they came back the whistles were beautiful. Nothing about the trucks had changed.

Not the staircase burning. Cable burning under it: insulation cooked off for the copper. The smell is what tells you, and only if you have lived there.

## Banked

A city's mass is banked relation. Grids, girders, reticulation, 4am shifts poured as concrete and left to set. Somebody surveyed that corner. Somebody costed that span. Somebody carried a theodolite up a hill in 1936 and somebody else has been paying for it ever since, in instalments nobody itemises.

That is what is standing there. Not stone. Held decisions.

> **Plate 2 · `Life(ASClem_2015_07_29_1293)`**
> *Life.* Johannesburg, 29 July 2015, 21:33. Canon EOS 500D.

Decay is not the mass leaving. It is the field withdrawing (capital, concern, the weighing) while the structure stands like a score nobody sings. The lights still work in some of the windows. The lease is still on somebody's books at a number that stopped being true around 2009.

## The whistle

Weight arrives. That is the part nobody warns you about.

The trucks came back and the whistle went through the yard and it was the best sound of that year, and if you had recorded the same man whistling at the same bin in a good month, you would have heard nothing at all. The sound had not improved. We had.

Nothing was added to the truck. Nothing was added to the street. What came back was the weighing.

> **Plate 4 · `_MG_0800`**
> Burnt flatbed, highway outside the city, 22 June 2016, 01:45. Canon EOS 750D.

And it runs the other way in the same year: a truck burnt down to its frame at twenty to two in the morning, load cooked across two lanes, the pylons still walking over the hill behind it. Somebody withdrew a service. Somebody burned a truck. The truck is the same object in both sentences.

Meanwhile, a ball game. The road is shut, which makes it for one afternoon the best pitch in the district. Boys will take a field wherever a field is offered.

## Harvest

Decay is not a noun, and the verb has a subject.

Under the staircase, the cable. Insulation cooked off in a shallow gully, copper lifted out of it, copper sold. That is not weather. That is somebody's Sunday, spent crouching in smoke, for a few hundred rand and a burn on the forearm.

Call it what it is: the configuration dismantled to liberate the substance. Making, run backwards, at a much worse rate. Everything that went into laying that cable (the trench, the survey, the shift, the sign-off, the whole banked argument) is being cashed out at scrap value by a person who never saw a cent of the original.

*Verval.*

Noun and verb in the same six letters, and Afrikaans does not tell you which one you are getting.

> **Plate 6 · `MagNieGeboggelWordNie(StandertonElektries_2016)`**
> Standerton, 5 June 2016, 00:19. Canon EOS 750D.

A carriage on a dead siding, stripped to its ribs, and along the flank the stencil is still perfectly legible: MAG NIE GEBOGGEL OF LOS GEHANGEER WORD NIE. The rule outlived the institution that meant it. Nobody obeys it. Nobody has bothered to deface it either. It goes on being a rule, on the side of a thing, in the dark.

## The window

Now the window.

It is in the frame at the top and again at the bottom, and it is slightly greasy. I could have cropped it out in about four seconds. It is there because some months earlier I had gone in on foot and been beaten for the camera: Hillbrow, the early hours of the second of January. The camera went. One tripod leg stayed. I posted the shiners.

One night is not a life. The sentence stops there. I read back what I wrote that week and I still decline the equivalence I could have claimed; I suspect we all have our Hillbrow.

> **Plate 8 · `school_hillbrow`**
> Johannesburg, undated. A camera on the tarmac, screen lit, on a tripod that has had a night. A school's board overhead, a phone number for an abortion pasted on the pole below.

I did not compose that, and I have never improved on it.

So: to photograph a ruin is to weigh it in a field the people inside it cannot afford. Say so, or the picture lies. The window is me saying so. It is the toll, paid in the frame, at the cost of the frame.

Which is a narrower thing than a refusal of people, and I have written it down more absolutely than I hold it. What I decline is the profit: the frame that converts somebody's worst week into my good year. That does not generalise into a rule against faces. Reportage is a register. Witness is a register. Two boys and a ball on a closed road are not a misfortune I am harvesting; they are a Wednesday afternoon, and the resilience in it is theirs, and I would rather it were seen than not. The rule that covers every room has never been written. There is only ever this room.

## The green

> **Plate 9 · `Durban Deep_2021(FNMBJ_Jul21_MG_0887)`**
> Durban Deep, 17 July 2021, 16:37. Canon EOS 6D Mark II.

Then the green comes in over the top of the frame without permission.

Grass in the doorway, a tree through the roof sheet, the plan of the building still perfectly readable underneath, because a wall goes on being a wall long after it stops being a room. The building is still there. Something else has started weighing it, on a scale the original owner never held and could not have read. The termites are enthusiastic. The grass has views.

> **Plate 10 · `FacesinBordeaux_2019_MG_6016e`**
> Bordeaux, 26 April 2019, 22:30. Canon EOS 6D Mark II.

And you start seeing faces in the render. Everybody does. There is nobody in that wall (the wall is old and damp and hung with netting), and yet a face arrives, because a face is what I am equipped to find. What a thing is made of is one question. What is looking is another, and it is the one that keeps moving.

## The gold

> **Plate 11 · `OWN-tower(2014_07_30_1459_tmp)`**
> Johannesburg, 30 July 2014, 22:54. Canon EOS 600D.

The gold left long ago and the streets still bend around where it was. Main Reef Road runs where the reef ran. The whole strung-out shape of this place is an ore body nobody has touched in a lifetime. An absence can hold a shape in a city the way a heavy thing holds a shape in a sheet, and everything afterwards travels the bent line and calls the bending *the way to town*.

The small ones go the same way. A tower with VIDEO TOWN bolted to the top of it, a rabbit in a top hat, 5 DVD GAMES. Not one part of that has stopped existing. Every letter is there and the paint is honestly not bad. What went is the entire world in which a person drove somewhere on a Friday to rent a film and drove back on Sunday to return it. The sign is in excellent condition. Nobody can read it.

## Elsewhere

> **Plate 12 · `Midnight(Paris 2019)`**
> *Midnight.* Paris, 4 May 2019, 00:00:41. Canon EOS 6D Mark II.

A bed on the pavement, made up, pillow squared, at forty-one seconds past midnight. The camera is precise about this. The title turned out to be true to the minute.

It is in here because this is not a Johannesburg speciality. Withdrawal is a thing fields do everywhere, and the wealthy ones do it more quietly, under better lighting, one doorway at a time. Bordeaux, eight days earlier, was a wall coming apart under netting, and nobody calls that a decaying city. It decays at the ordinary rate. It is simply weighed by people who can still afford the weighing.

Point this camera only south and the pictures begin arguing something I do not believe.

---

## Still

> **Plate 13 · `EventHorison(aPinhole-Poem)[pinhole_on_digital-doubleExposure-2017-2026]`**
> *Event Horison (a pinhole poem).* Pinhole on digital, double exposure, 2017–2026.

The last one is a pinhole laid over a digital frame, nine years in a single sheet, and it will not resolve. I have stopped wanting it to.

Mass that persists can always be re-weighed, and never only on the original owner's scale. The building does not know it has been abandoned. The street does not know the gold is gone. The copper is worth exactly what the copper is worth.

Still.

***One correction to the seed, declared rather than silent.*** *The seed records the refusal to photograph people more absolutely than the operator holds it. The nuance is the one the objectification lens already carries in this repository — no blanket rule survives contact with a particular room.*
