## The line

> Almost anything can be turned into drama, and luckily almost any drama can be turned into art.

Said aloud to a room about to spend a morning drawing people posing as objects. It does two things
at once and that is why it is worth keeping. It concedes the first half without flinching —
*almost anything*, including this room, including the conceit we are about to run — and then it
declines to treat the concession as a verdict.

*Luckily* is doing more work than it looks like. Not *therefore*. Not *and so*. The convertibility
is a gift rather than a mechanism, and the word admits it.

## The follow-on, same opening

> But what might it mean to 'turn into art'? Given we are talking about a conflict relation of some
> sort, well that depends on what art is to you, 'cause if there is one thing we know it is that
> opinions on that topic can differ wildly. If for argument sake we say as I like to, that art is,
> among other is-es, the appreciable manifestation of creativity which is both about finding and
> holding relations, as well as orienting and being alive through those constellations.

The move is to refuse to smuggle the definition in. *That depends on what art is to you* is not a
throat-clear — it is multipartiality applied to the theory itself, in the moment of stating it.
*For argument sake*, *as I like to*, *among other is-es*: three hedges in one sentence, all of them
preserving the room's autonomy rather than protecting the claim.

Note also that this is the axiom **in the mouth**, not on the page. `theory_of_art.md` has *"the
making of new relations, towards the desirable."* The room got *"finding and holding relations …
orienting and being alive through those constellations."* Both are the same claim; the spoken one
adds **holding** and **being alive through**, which the canonical wording leaves to be inferred.
Worth carrying back — the spoken registration may be the better one.

## What it is for

**A theory of drama's economics, which the art world is a case of.**

Drama is a relation-making machine that runs cheap and fast. It arrives as weight and banks
nothing — the charge is real, the residue is nil, and when the field moves on there is nothing left
standing. Art proper deposits something that survives the field's withdrawal, which is exactly why
revival is possible at all.

So *turning drama into art* names a conversion: from field into mass. Not making the conflict go
away — that is resolution, and the host methodology is explicit that resolution risks premature
closure and costs superficiality of relations. Banking it as configuration instead.

The three seats are the machine's parts and rotate by design. The exits are already hosted here
(Choy's Winner's Triangle, Emerald's TED) and the repo's own extensions are conflict **resilience**
over resolution, multipartiality over neutrality, and the power-equilibrium clause:

> take your power, but only your own power. No More No less. Even when and where it is true that
> *'power is not shared, power is taken'*.

## The guard, which travels with the idea

The framework turns Persecutor the moment it is used to read someone's grievance. `KARPMAN_DRAMA_TRIANGLE.md`
§4.5 states it plainly and the statement is load-bearing, not decorative:

> Calling someone's pain a "Victim role" can be the ultimate Persecutor stance.
> **Mitigation:** Use the triangle as a *pattern descriptor*, not a *character judgement*. The
> question is never "Are you a Victim?" but "Are we caught in a drama pattern?"

Consequence for any public writing off this seed: **name the roles, not the model.** Blame, rescue,
injury, glamour, lament. The apparatus stays under the floor. This is not squeamishness — a piece
that diagnoses a named living author with a therapeutic framework performs the exact move it is
warning about, in its own second act.

## And-Yet

*Almost* is carrying the whole sentence and has not been tested. Which drama does **not** convert?
The suspicion is that the residue is where the interesting cases live — some harm is not material,
and the confidence that anything can be made into work is itself a kind of extraction if it is not
checked. The line was said in a room that had built its consent apparatus first. Said in a room
that had not, it would be a different sentence entirely, and probably a worse one.
