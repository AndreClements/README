T-shirt / merch concept.

Front: **How tight is your polysemiologism?**

The line works because it *is* one. "Tight" reads as:
- precise (technical craft)
- close-knit (relational density)
- slang for "good" (aesthetic approval)
- constrained (operating under pressure)

All four readings land simultaneously. The question answers itself.

Could also work as: sticker, tote, print, poster for studio wall.
