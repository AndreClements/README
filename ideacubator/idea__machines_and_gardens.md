# Idea: Machines and Gardens

## Core Proposition

The machine-garden is an operational mode. Structure (machine) serves emergence (garden); ideally neither dominates. A machine is complete at construction, evaluate-able by specification. A garden doesn't have a complete, it is actualised capacity for ongoing life, autopoeises. Interesting work happens at the seam — intersectionality — where finite structure enables infinite growth without constraining it.

This is a compound/conjuction: the machine-garden. Working systems that sustain creative practice tend to operate in this mode. (Hypothesis?)

## The Practice Spine

Across media, the same operation repeats: **holding multiplicity without collapse**.

- **Photography** — 500 exposures composited into *Bridge (Phase 6)*. The multiple-exposure technique makes visible that any single frame is a lie of selection. The machine (algorithmic compositing) serves the garden (accumulated seeing).
- **Painting** — *'Tis all snakes and ladders* layers a tesseract, HEXACO tokens, Alice-as-Infanta, a skull, and bubble-wrap texture into a single surface. Not collage but a diagram of simultaneous readings that refuses to resolve. The machine (structural scaffolding) serves the garden (polysemic accumulation).
- **Drawing** — The Multistate works explicitly name the operation: multistability, perceptual load-balancing. Recycled charcoal dust from discarded drawings becomes new medium — temporal recursion embedded in material. The machine (three-layer protocol, "The Crowd") serves the garden (emergent mark-making).
- **Code** — Life Drawing Randburg CMS: ConsentGate, DignityException, "govern via slope not policing." SAFFCA '26: Parametric Authorship, CII, CARDS axiology. Ethical commitments held in operational architecture rather than argumentative prose. The machine (code architecture) serves the garden (cultural emergence).
- **Facilitation** — Life drawing sessions as co-created space. Models and artists in slow time. The machine (session protocol, timer, pose structure) serves the garden (relational encounter, presence, the bodyTime() photographs).

## Connection to And-Yet

The machine is the **As-If** — provisional structure, adopted knowing it is incomplete. The garden is the **If-Not** — what escapes structure, what grows beyond specification. The machine-garden is the **And-Yet** — both held, neither collapsed, the contradiction sustained as generative tension.

## Connection to Botha

Marc Botha's "Risk" chapter (2021) identifies that code holds complexity which flattens in discourse. The Life Drawing Randburg CMS is a working counter-example: axiological commitments embedded in architecture rather than argued in prose. The machine-garden mode is the structural reason this works — the code-as-machine is also a code-as-garden, growing with each commit, never finished, evaluated by its capacity to sustain ethical practice over time.

See: `ArtPraxis/studio/2026-marc-botha-reading-thread.md` (practice connections, "The flattening problem")

## Open Questions

- Is the machine-garden a thesis or a methodology? (Probably both — parametric authorship is the thesis, machine-garden is a mode of practice.)
- What is the failure mode? INFINITE_GAMES.md identifies "Mechanisation (MG-1)" — when a garden is treated as a machine. Is there a reciprocal failure — when a machine is treated as a garden? (Possibly: when structure dissolves into pure emergence, nothing accumulates, nothing can be found again. The chaos failure.)
- How does this relate to the Kandinsky dependency? Artistry/care (garden) endures over spectacle/fashion (machine-as-spectacle). But the machine in machine-garden is not spectacle — it's infrastructure. The bad machine is the one optimised for attention rather than growth.
- How does machine-garden relate to wild-life?
