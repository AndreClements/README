## Core Provocation

**Stocks and flows** as vocabulary for how the generator pattern actually works: stocks accumulate (photograph archives, sketchbook pages, computational outputs), flows transform (the drawing session, the averaging pipeline, the feedback loop between them), and what emerges is not designed but yielded — the average as emergent form.

## Narrow Framing: The Averaging Convergence

Overlapping charcoal figures on a sketchbook page are manual averaging — the hand doing what `layer-average.py` does computationally. The impulse is identical: stack enough instances of a gesture and the essential form surfaces. The individual pose becomes a note in a melody.

The difference is not method but grain. The hand selects, compresses, emphasises. The algorithm treats each source equally (the 1/N ethical position). The sketchbook privileges the raised arm; the computational average weights every pose identically. Both produce emergent forms — but the hand's emergence carries the artist's attention, while the algorithm's carries the category's consensus.

And yet: curated subsets reintroduce authorial selection into the computational pipeline. The artist chooses which photographs enter the average. The parametric constraints — what to include, what batch size, what alignment — are the authorial act (PA §5.3). The hand and the algorithm converge: **the artist designs the parameters; the process yields the form**.

## Broad Framing: Systems Vocabulary for the Generator Pattern

The generator pattern ("designed to persist, to process, and to yield a continuous stream of varied, often contradictory, outputs") describes *what* the system does. Stocks and flows describe *how*:

- **Stocks** — repositories of accumulated material available for repeated withdrawal. A photograph archive. A sketchbook's pages. A body of averaged images. A life's contexture. Stocks grow richer with use; they are not depleted by withdrawal but enriched by what the withdrawal teaches.
- **Flows** — durational transformations that move material through the system, depositing new stocks as they go. A drawing session. A computational pipeline. A conversation. Flows have rates: fifteen-minute life drawings and slower reference-based work are different flow rates through the same process.
- **Feedback loops** — flows that cycle back to enrich the stocks they draw from. The November photographs (stock) yield the April reference drawing (flow) which enriches the sketchbook (stock) which informs the next session (flow). The loop is the generator's engine.

This vocabulary applies beyond the averaging case: any README method that persists and yields (paintWithScalpel accumulating cuts, Landscapify building continuous territory, meticulousBlur layering ambiguities) operates through stocks and flows. The vocabulary is general; the averaging convergence is the anchor instance.
