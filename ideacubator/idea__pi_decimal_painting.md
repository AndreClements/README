## The seed

A painting of the decimal expansion of Pi. Two candidate forms, held side by side rather than chosen:

1. **n-geometry projection** — the digits walked as a path on a simple projected geometry (ten stations, or whatever n the rule declares; the digit stream does the drawing, the painter sets the projection). Something like that.
2. **Periodic-table form** — digits laid as a grid of cells in the manner of the pigment test grid triangles: each cell a digit rendered as a pigment/mix, so the expansion reads as a swatch field, the way the test grids already read. The analogue precedent is on the studio wall; Pi would sit inside an existing material grammar rather than importing a new one.

Either way the authorship is parametric in the plain sense: the operator designs the mapping (digit → position, digit → pigment), and the sequence — fixed, inexhaustible, nobody's — executes. What gets signed is the projection, never the digits.
