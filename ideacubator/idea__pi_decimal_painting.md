## The seed

A painting of the decimal expansion of Pi. Two candidate forms, held side by side rather than chosen:

1. **n-geometry projection** — the digits walked as a path on a simple projected geometry (ten vertices, or whatever n the rule declares; the digit stream does the drawing, the painter sets the projection). Something like that.
2. **Periodic-table form** — digits laid as a grid of cells in the manner of the pigment test grid triangles: each cell a digit rendered as a pigment/mix, so the expansion reads as a swatch field, the way the test grids already read. The analogue precedent is on the studio wall; Pi would sit inside an existing material grammar rather than importing a new one.

Either way the authorship is parametric in the plain sense: the operator designs the mapping (digit → position, digit → pigment), and the sequence — fixed, inexhaustible, nobody's — executes. What gets signed is the projection, never the digits.

## Poetry-research-informed considerations

Distilled from the operator's conversation with an external instrument (ChatGPT, 2026-08-21), archived verbatim in private custody at `ArtPraxis/vault/logistics_of_the_heart/pcap__logistics_matrices_looking_2026-08-21.md` (it quotes gated poem lexis, so the full text stays there; relocated from PRIVATE_META 2026-08-22). What travels here is what bears on the painting:

- **Matrix in, line out.** Thought and attention operate as a relational field; utterance forces it through a serial aperture. Pi's decimal expansion is exactly such an object: a single relational constant forced to speak one digit at a time, forever. The painting can run the aperture backwards — return the line to a field, and let co-presence do what sequence cannot.
- **An alternative painting sequence:** *field → recurrence → displacement → occlusion → return → exception*, in place of background → middle ground → foreground → finish. The digit stream supplies the recurrences and exceptions for free; the layers do the displacing and occluding.
- **Form transpositions that fit the digits.** The sestina's operation (permutation of a limited set) is native to a ten-glyph alphabet; the refrain's (a motif returning under altered conditions) is what a recurring digit *is* once intervening layers have changed the field it returns to. Glaze behaves like refrain — transforming what remains visible beneath rather than adding; scumble behaves like memory — concealment without erasure.
- **The governing thread:** meaning as an event produced *between* elements under changing constraints, held relationally rather than narratively. Recurrence need not repeat. A work need not go somewhere; it can rotate a relational object and keep exposing altered configurations — which is a fair description of what an expansion of Pi does, and of what a painting of one could do.
